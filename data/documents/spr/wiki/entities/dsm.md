# Departmental Stores Manager

The [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officer]] appoints a Departmental Stores Manager (DSM) to help supervise all procurement and stores management in the department (SPR 135). The DSM may be assisted by Supplies Grade and/or non-Supplies Grade staff. Purchasing and stores work is normally done by Supplies Grade staff; the CO must make enough of them available. See [[08-stores-management]].

## Inventories and ledgers

DSM accounts for inventory stores received and issued (SPR 715). That includes:

- ensuring the store-unit officer records receipts and issues and cross-references Inventory Stores Ledger Sheet (GF 270) and Inventory Sheet and Distribution Record (GF 272) with vouchers such as GF 219 or GF 277;
- arranging an inventory check at least once a year and sending discrepancies under SPR 1030;
- keeping a control index of inventory sheets in use.

Inventory vs non-inventory is a [[stores-classification]] question: generally permanent or non-consumable items with a unit cost at or above $1,000 at purchase (SPR 710(b)).

If ledgers are lost or damaged, the CO appoints a board of survey for a full stock-take. After the CO endorses the findings, DSM opens a new ledger for stores recorded in the lost books if their value does not exceed $250,000. Above that, the department needs [[dgl|DGL]]’s prior approval (SPR 630).

## Inspections

DSM assists the CO in general supervision and control of stores and stores accounts (SPR 896):

- inspect each store unit under direct control at least once every two years, including security, and report on GF 238 (and GF 241 if needed);
- carry out surprise stock and security checks at irregular intervals at least once every three months unless DGL authorises otherwise. If inspecting every unit is impractical, the unit head or deputy does the check. Findings go in the Surprise Inspection Book (GF 235).

The public officer who approves formal purchase orders reports to a more senior ranked DSM (SPR 255). After a replacement of lost stores under SPR 1075, DSM supports the issue on GF 277.
