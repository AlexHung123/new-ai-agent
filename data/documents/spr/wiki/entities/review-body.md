# Review Body on Bid Challenges

Procurement decisions are reviewable by the relevant authorities. For procurements covered by the [[wto-gpa|WTO Agreement on Government Procurement]], they are also reviewable by the Review Body on Bid Challenges (SPR 108). Contractors, suppliers, or service providers may challenge alleged breaches of WTO GPA to that Review Body (SPR 160(b)). Ordinary process-or-result complaints still go first to the department, the relevant tender or consultants selection board, or The Ombudsman (SPR 160(a)). See [[01-authority]].

## Time to lodge a challenge

The Note to Tenderers at Appendix III(E)1 (required in WTO GPA tender documents under SPR 345) states:

- a supplier who believes a GPA breach has occurred may lodge a challenge **within ten working days** after he knew or reasonably should have known the basis of the challenge;
- the Review Body **may** receive and consider a late challenge, but **shall not** consider one filed later than **30 working days** after the basis was known or reasonably should have been known.

The Review Body’s Rules of Operation are available from its Secretariat at the Trade and Industry Department, or on request. The Note encourages the supplier to seek resolution with the procuring department first; the department must consider that complaint impartially and in time, in a manner not prejudicial to corrective measures through the Review Body.

The Review Body is not a general procurement ombudsman. It hears alleged **breaches of WTO GPA**, not ordinary dissatisfaction with a quotation or a non-GPA tender. Covered procurements must include Appendix III(E)1 in the tender documents (SPR 345). Chapter IV consultancies are not bound by WTO GPA (SPR 450(e)), so they do not go to this Body.

## How this sits beside departmental complaints

The [[controlling-officer|CO]] must still run an impartial departmental complaint system and give a substantive reply within ten working days, or an interim reply (SPR 160). Complaints about process or result may also go to the relevant tender or consultants selection board, The Ombudsman, or ICAC if corruption is suspected (SPR 160(a)). A GPA bid challenge is a separate track; it does not replace that duty. Departments must keep WTO GPA files for not less than three years from contract award (SPR 530(d)). [[doj|D of J]] advises on whether a buy is covered and on limited-tendering grounds.
