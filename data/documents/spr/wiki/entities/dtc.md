# Departmental Tender Committee

Every [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officer]] must establish a Departmental Tender Committee (DTC) of not less than three persons (SPR 310(f)). The chair is a directorate officer not lower than D2, and must be at least one rank higher than the chair of the Tender Assessment Panel for that exercise. The DTC considers or decides acceptance of tenders within the SPR 116 limits. See [[03-tenders]].

## Limit and coverage

SPR 116 gives DTC authority for stores, services, and revenue contracts **except works**, up to $10 million (the SPR 220(a) departmental limit). Works awards in the simplified non-WTO GPA band sit with the CO personally under SPR 310(h), not with the DTC. Above the DTC ceiling, the case goes to a [[ctb|subsidiary tender board]] or CTB.

[[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] has delegated acceptance of tenders in a DTC’s purview and within its financial limit (SPR 390(a)), and negotiation authority for those same tenders (SPR 385(c)(iv)).

## Disagreement and monthly report

If DTC members divide on a contract award, the Chairman may refer the matter to the CO for a ruling (SPR 310(g)). DTCs send the relevant tender board, at month-end, details of every award where the lowest conforming offer (highest for a revenue contract), or the highest overall scorer where a [[marking-scheme]] was used, was **not** accepted, and the reasons.

## How it sits in the process

A procurement that uses [[tendering]] procedures — including a quotation-value buy that the department elects to tender — is subject to the relevant board or DTC (SPR 221). Tender reports to a DTC follow departmental procedures; reports to a tender board have a four-week / five-clear-working-day timetable (SPR 375). Boards and DTCs normally meet; circulation is the exception (SPR 375(c)).

A DTC may also approve [[tendering|tender]] cancellation in its purview (SPR 380(e)) and, with the relevant board, marking schemes that deviate from the SMS Framework (SPR 350). Works contracts that the CO awards personally under SPR 310(h) do not use the SPR 375 tender-report format (SPR 375(d)).

DTC chairs, members, and secretaries sign an undertaking on taking up the role and renew it at least annually (SPR 187). See [[conflict-of-interest]] and [[01a-conflict-of-interest]].
