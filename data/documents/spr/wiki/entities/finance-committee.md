# Finance Committee of the Legislative Council

The Finance Committee (FC) of the Legislative Council is not a tender board. It appears in SPR where public money leaves the SPR delegation: high-value [[write-off]], donations that fall outside the surplus-stores rules, and the funding that [[parallel-tendering]] must not pre-empt. See [[10-audit-writeoff]] and [[11-surplus-disposal]].

## Write-off

Where loss or deficiency of stores is caused by fraud, suspected fraud, or negligence of a public officer (other than the small theft band in SPR 1070(a)(i)), write-off exceeding $500,000 requires Finance Committee approval (SPR 1070(a)(ii)). Cases not exceeding $500,000 in that class go to [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]]. Non-fraud write-offs never reach FC; above $900,000 they stop at PS(Tsy) (SPR 1070(b)).

## Donations

A departmental disposal authority may donate surplus stores to a non-government organisation only if the body is established solely or principally for educational, scientific, cultural, or charitable purposes, will use the items for a non-profit-making purpose, and the gift creates no capital or recurrent cost for Government (SPR 1140). A donation that does **not** meet those conditions needs the **prior** approval of the Finance Committee (SPR 1142). Donations that do meet SPR 1140 but exceed $300,000 need PS(Tsy), not FC (SPR 1141).

## Funding and parallel tendering

[[controlling-officer|COs]] may invite tenders before funding is secured, but they must not award a contract and must not indicate to the successful bidder that Government would accept its bid unless and until funding is in place (SPR 337–338; FC No. 3/2020). One stated risk of [[parallel-tendering]] is that Government may be seen to be pre-empting Finance Committee / Legislative Council. For Capital Works Reserve Fund works, parallel tendering is the default; exemption above $30 million needs the Director of Bureau.

Cancellation approval is not required if, in a parallel exercise, FC has not approved the funding application before tender validity expires (SPR 380(d); same idea for consultants at SPR 470(b)). If the accepted tender exceeds the approved estimates, the CO must obtain an increase in commitment or project estimate before acceptance (SPR 390).

Financial Circulars that set operational limits (for example FC 3/2009 on simplified works tenders, FC 2/2019 on marking schemes and restricted routes) are issued under the [[sfst|SFST]] umbrella and have equal force with SPR (SPR 105). FC itself is the legislature’s money committee, not the SPR interpreter — that remains [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]].
