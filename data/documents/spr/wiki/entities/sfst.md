# Financial Secretary / Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury

The Financial Secretary / Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury ([[sfst|SFST]]) makes the Stores and Procurement Regulations under section 11(1) of the Public Finance Ordinance (Cap. 2) (SPR 100). Volume 4 of [[government-regulations]] lists FS / SFST / [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] as the officers authorised to amend, supplement, apply, interpret, and make exceptions to SPR (SPR 4). Day-to-day application, interpretation, and exceptions sit with PS(Tsy).

SPR 4 also names FS / SFST as the authority for Volume 3 Part 1 (Financial and Accounting Regulations) and SFST alone for Volume 6 (Accommodation Regulations). The [[finance-committee|Finance Committee]] is a Legislative Council body; it is not the SPR-making authority.

## What FS / SFST appoints

- [[ctb|Central Tender Board]], and authority for PS(Tsy) to appoint subsidiary tender boards and for [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officers]] to appoint [[dtc|DTCs]] (SPR 310(a)).
- Consultants selection boards ([[ccsb|CCSB, AACSB, EACSB]]), each of not less than three persons and chaired at not lower than D2 (SPR 425).

## When a case may reach FS / SFST

If members of CTB seriously disagree on a contract award, the Chairman may refer the matter to FS / SFST for advice (SPR 310(b)). That is the only SPR path that takes a live tender award above PS(Tsy) to the political head.

## Delegations that originate here

SPR 220 records that the Financial Secretary has delegated to COs the authority to procure stores, services, and revenue contracts up to the quotation and departmental limits. SPR 426 records that SFST has delegated consultant selection and appointment up to the departmental limit in SPR 220(a) — exercised through a DCSC.

FS / SFST issues Financial Circulars and FSTB Circular Memoranda that supplement SPR with equal force (SPR 5, 105). Operational dollar and marking-scheme rules often live in those circulars (for example FC 2/2019, FC 3/2009, FC 3/2020) rather than in the SPR text itself. See [[01-authority]].
