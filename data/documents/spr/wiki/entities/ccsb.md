# Consultants selection boards

[[sfst|FS / SFST]] appoints consultants selection boards of not less than three persons, each chaired by a directorate officer not lower than D2 (SPR 425). Unless further delegated, [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] remains the approving authority for appointing consulting firms (SPR 410). See [[04-consultants]] and [[consultants-selection]].

## The four bodies (SPR 116, 425–426)

| Body | Role | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Consultants Selection Board (CCSB) | Advises PS(Tsy) on all consultancies except those of AACSB, EACSB, and DCSCs | No dollar limit |
| AACSB | Approves architectural and associated consultants (other than DCSC cases) | No dollar limit in its field |
| EACSB | Approves engineering and associated consultants (other than DCSC cases) | No dollar limit in its field |
| Departmental Consultants Selection Committee (DCSC) | Appointed by the [[controlling-officer\|CO]]; ≥3 persons; chair ≥ D2 and at least one rank above the assessment-panel chair | ≤ $10 million |

AACSB and EACSB follow their published Handbooks and DEVB TC(W), not the CCSB article sequence (SPR 425(b)–(c)).

## CCSB two-stage process

CCSB normally works in two stages (SPR 430(a)):

- **Stage 1** — invite proposals from the recommended firms; approve the consultancy brief and the method of assessment.
- **Stage 2** — appoint the recommended firm, subject to fees and other terms in the negotiating brief.

Technical weighting on the [[marking-scheme]] is normally around 60–70%, unless the board or DCSC is satisfied otherwise (SPR 427). Technical assessment is finished before fee envelopes are opened (SPR 445(c)–(d)).

## Direct approach is reserved

Authority to approach one consulting firm without a competitive selection rests with PS(Tsy). It is **not** delegated to COs at any value (SPR 430(b)). After that approval, the department still sends a combined Stages 1 and 2 paper to CCSB (above the departmental limit) or DCSC (at or below it).

SFST has delegated selection and appointment up to the SPR 220(a) departmental limit to COs, exercised through the DCSC — list of firms, briefs, marking schemes, pre-contract negotiations, appointment, and cancellation (SPR 426).
