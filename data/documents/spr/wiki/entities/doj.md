# Department of Justice / LAD(W)/DEVB

The Department of Justice (D of J) is the legal adviser on SPR procurements. For works, Legal Advisory Division (Works) of the Development Bureau (LAD(W)/DEVB) takes the corresponding role. Briefing out of legal work by the Secretary for Justice is outside Chapter III and Chapter IV (SPR 300(a)(vi), 405(c)). Most contracts use a standard form; execution queries go to D of J, with notes at Appendix V(A) (SPR 505(c)). See [[05-contract-admin]] and [[03-tenders-process]].

## When departments must ask

- **[[national-security]]** — legal issues in the procurement process, together with GLD on procedure (SPR 109A(c)).
- **Single / restricted [[tendering]]** — legal advice on the proposed route from the [[wto-gpa]] perspective, attached to the approval paper (SPR 325(d)(vii)).
- **[[direct-engagement]]** covered by WTO GPA — Articles XIII or III; ask D of J and/or LAD(W)/DEVB if in doubt (SPR 331).
- **Standard and non-standard forms** — amendments to standard forms, and any non-standard form, must be cleared with D of J or LAD(W)/DEVB (SPR 345). Non-works contracts estimated above $300 million: all non-standard components (bills of quantities, particular specifications, special conditions) go to D of J before issue; substantial later amendments are re-vetted. Below $300 million the [[controlling-officer|CO]] (or a designated officer not below Chief Supplies Officer / Chief Executive Officer) decides whether to send non-standard components.
- **National-security clauses** — standard forms at SPR 345(a)(iv)–(vi) must allow disqualification and termination in the interest of national security. If those clauses are not used, clear with the policy bureau and D of J and/or LAD(W)/DEVB (SPR 345).
- **Non-conforming recommendation** — clear with D of J, or LAD(W)/DEVB for works, and attach the advice (SPR 370(g)).
- **Withholding a contract-award notice** on national-security or public-interest grounds (SPR 390(c)).
- **Consultancy briefs and agreements**, including [[conflict-of-interest]] clauses (SPR 192, 196, 460).
- **Contract execution** — non-Hong Kong counterparties, capacity or signatory doubts, and any execution query (SPR 505). Guidance is at Appendix V(A).
- **[[contract-variation]] that may be a new WTO GPA procurement** (SPR 520(e)).
- **Negotiations** — a D of J member sits on high-value or complex tender and consultancy negotiation teams (SPR 385(j), 450). Authorised negotiators seek D of J on legal aspects and [[dgl|DGL]] on commercial aspects (SPR 385(i)).
- **Fee disclosure** in consultancy agreements and the Code on Access to Information (SPR 465(d)).
- **Personal-injury claims** against Government under the Employees’ Compensation Ordinance or at common law — notify the firm, the Commissioner for Labour, and D of J (SPR 510(b)).
