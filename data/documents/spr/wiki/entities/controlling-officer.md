# Controlling Officer

A [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officer]] (CO) is accountable to the public for the department’s purchasing decisions and must see that they are justified and documented (SPR 108). COs procure stores, services, and revenue contracts within the SPR financial limits and manage what is bought (SPR 125). They must apply [[value-for-money]] and the other principles in SPR 106–109, the [[national-security]] duty in SPR 109A, and a culture of SPR compliance. See [[01-authority]].

## Departmental system

COs may issue departmental instructions if they comply with SPR and [[dgl|DGL]] has approved them; works and works-related consultancies may also follow DEVB TC(W) (SPR 126). The departmental system must have checks and balances and a clear split of roles. Segregation of duties is agreed before tenders are invited. Officers must be alert to actual, potential, and perceived [[conflict-of-interest]]. For [[wto-gpa]] procurements, COs observe the Agreement (SPR 127).

COs appoint:

- a [[dsm|Departmental Stores Manager]] to supervise procurement and stores (SPR 135);
- a [[dtc|Departmental Tender Committee]] (SPR 310(f));
- a Departmental Consultants Selection Committee (SPR 426);
- a Departmental Disposal Committee (SPR 1105).

They reply promptly and fully to queries from the Director of Audit or DGL (SPR 130) and personally ensure complaints are handled impartially within ten working days (SPR 160).

## Personal authorities (not sub-delegated as of right)

- Single or restricted [[tendering]] for stores, non-works services, and revenue contracts within the SPR 220(a) departmental limit (SPR 325(b)(i)).
- [[direct-engagement|Direct engagement]] of a contractor / supplier / service provider for the same class and limit (SPR 332).
- Award of non-WTO GPA works ≤ $30 million; and of non-WTO GPA works above $30 million but ≤ $52 million if awarded to the lowest conforming bidder or highest overall scorer (SPR 310(h)).
- Some [[write-off]] bands, including theft ≤ $50,000 and non-fraud losses ≤ $900,000 (SPR 1070).

A one-firm consultant approach is **not** delegated to COs (SPR 430(b)); that stays with [[pstsy]].
