# Director of Government Logistics / Government Logistics Department

[[dgl|DGL]] advises departments on stores and procurement practice and runs compliance checks against prescribed criteria (SPR 120(a)). In that work DGL has access to all records, books, vouchers, documents, and receipts relating to procurement exercises and government stores in any public officer’s possession (SPR 120(b)). DGL’s audit role does not relieve other officers of their own duties (SPR 115). See [[01-authority]].

## Circulars and central buying

DGL may issue GLD Circulars and Circular Memoranda on stores and procurement. With [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] approval those instructions have equal force with SPR (SPR 120(c)).

DGL is the procurement agent for stores above the SPR 220(a) departmental limit. Departments request procurement of stores that are neither in GLD unallocated stock nor covered by a GLD bulk contract (SPR 235(a)). Below that limit, departments may still ask GLD to prepare documents and invite tenders, or run their own exercise (SPR 235(b)). GLD tenders are accepted through the [[ctb|GLD Tender Board]] (stores / non-works services / revenue ≤ $60 million). DGL or his delegates initiate single or restricted [[tendering]] for GLD tenders (SPR 325(b)(ii)), and DGL (or designated officers) may shorten a [[wto-gpa]] tender period for those tenders and for stores tenders within the departmental limit (SPR 340(f)). Departments follow DGL’s supplementary guidelines on purchasing cards for low-value buys (SPR 260(a)).

COs’ departmental instructions on procurement and stores must be approved by DGL before use (SPR 126). Direct departmental purchase of items that GLD holds in unallocated stock or covers by a bulk contract also needs DGL’s prior consent (SPR 245).

## Stores, audit, and write-off

GLD unallocated stores are bought in bulk and charged first to the Unallocated Stores Suspense Account under Cap. 2 s.30, which DGL administers (SPR 705). On issue, DGL tells the Director of Accounting Services to debit the department and credit the suspense account. See [[stores-classification]] and [[07-accounting]].

GLD’s Procurement and Stores Management Audit Section verifies stocks at least once every three years and reports to the [[controlling-officer|CO]] (SPR 1005). DGL also appoints Boards of Survey (SPR 1010). On loss involving fraud, suspected fraud, or negligence, the CO reports to DGL (copying PS(Tsy), DAS, and D of A); DGL may enquire and advise (SPR 1035). Where negligence (not fraud) is ≤ $50,000, the CO may write off or require replacement only subject to DGL’s advice; disagreement goes to PS(Tsy) (SPR 1035(d)).

## Surplus disposal

If a surplus item has saleable value, the departmental disposal authority asks DGL to arrange commercial [[disposal]] — sale to a GLD disposal term contractor, or a quotation, tender, or auction (SPR 1145). Exceptional sale methods need PS(Tsy) approval, submitted through DGL. DGL also posts CCGO surplus notices for not less than ten working days (SPR 1135). DGL may assist departments to receive specialised imported stores such as arms and ammunition (SPR 900). See [[09-overseas-stores]].
