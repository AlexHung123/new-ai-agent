# Permanent Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury (Treasury)

[[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] is the default approving authority under SPR. The SPR 4 note authorises PS(Tsy) to apply, interpret, and make exceptions to SPR and related Financial Circulars. If a regulation does not name another officer, the answer is usually PS(Tsy). See [[01-authority]].

## Default acceptances and appointments

- Acceptance of a tender, unless delegated to a tender board or department (SPR 390(a)). [[ctb|CTB]] advises PS(Tsy) on tenders above subsidiary-board limits (SPR 310(b)).
- Appointment of consulting firms, unless delegated to [[ccsb|consultants selection boards]] or departments (SPR 410). [[ccsb|CCSB]] advises PS(Tsy) outside AACSB / EACSB / DCSC fields (SPR 425(a)).
- Approval of [[direct-engagement|direct engagement]] of a contractor / supplier / service provider, and of the offer (SPR 332). COs hold this only personally, and only for stores, non-works services, and revenue contracts within the SPR 220(a) departmental limit.
- A direct approach to one consulting firm without competition. This is **not** delegated to [[controlling-officer|COs]] at any value (SPR 430(b)).

## Negotiations, lists, and other defaults

PS(Tsy) is the approving authority for [[tendering|tender]] negotiations and has delegated that power to subsidiary boards, [[dgl|DGL]] (GLD tenders), COs / designated directorate officers (certain non-WTO GPA works ≤ $52 million), and [[dtc|DTCs]] within the departmental limit (SPR 385(c)). PS(Tsy) also approves selective-tender lists (except public-works lists, which sit with PS(W)) and, on CTB advice, prequalified tendering (SPR 320, 330). Shortening a [[wto-gpa]] tender period in extreme urgency is likewise a PS(Tsy) decision, delegated to DGL for GLD tenders and stores tenders within the departmental limit (SPR 340(f)).

Other defaults that remain here unless a regulation names someone else:

- not publishing a contract-award notice on national-security or public-interest grounds (SPR 390(c));
- donation of surplus stores exceeding $300,000 (SPR 1141);
- selling surplus stores by a method other than SPR 1145 (application through DGL);
- amending or excepting GLD’s stock-check criteria (SPR 1000(b));
- processing surcharge under Cap. 2 s.32 (SPR 1085). COs report surcharge circumstances to PS(Tsy) (SPR 110(b)).

High [[write-off|write-offs]] come here: fraud / negligence cases other than the small theft band, and non-fraud losses exceeding $900,000 (SPR 1070). [[sfst|FS / SFST]] still makes the Regulations; PS(Tsy) applies, interprets, and excepts them.
