# Central Tender Board and subsidiary tender boards

[[sfst|FS / SFST]] appoints the Central Tender Board (CTB) and has authorised [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] to appoint subsidiary tender boards (SPR 310(a)). CTB advises PS(Tsy) on acceptance of all tenders above the subsidiary boards’ limits, and advises or decides on tenders, contracts, and subsidiary boards generally (SPR 310(b)). Unless further delegated, PS(Tsy) remains the accepter of tenders (SPR 390(a)). See [[03-tenders]] and [[compare-approving-authorities]].

## Dollar limits (SPR 116)

| Body | Coverage | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CTB | All procurement types | No dollar limit (advises PS(Tsy)) |
| GLD Tender Board | Stores, non-works services, revenue | ≤ $60 million |
| Public Works Tender Board | Works and related services | ≤ $200 million |
| [[dtc\|DTC]] | Stores, services, and revenue except works | ≤ $10 million |

Quotation sits below these boards: works ≤ $7 million; other stores / services / revenue ≤ $1.35 million; consultancy quotations ≤ $3 million (SPR 220).

## Two subsidiary boards

The two subsidiary boards are the GLD Tender Board and the Public Works Tender Board (SPR 310(d)). If members of a subsidiary board divide on a contract award, the Chairman refers the matter to CTB. If CTB members themselves seriously disagree, the Chairman may refer the award to FS / SFST (SPR 310(b)).

Subsidiary boards send CTB, at month-end, details of every award where the lowest conforming offer (highest for a revenue contract), or the highest overall scorer where a [[marking-scheme]] was used, was **not** accepted, and the reasons.

## How departments approach CTB

Submissions (tender reports, works [[marking-scheme]] use, deviation from the SMS Framework, prequalification and the prequalified list) go in seven copies plus the original to the Secretary, CTB. The [[controlling-officer|CO]] or a directorate representative must sign or endorse; that officer may be asked — or may ask — to attend (SPR 310(c)). Membership and terms of reference are in the Civil and Miscellaneous Lists (SPR 310(e)).

PS(Tsy) has delegated [[tendering|tender]] negotiation authority to subsidiary boards for tenders in their purview (SPR 385(c)(i)).
