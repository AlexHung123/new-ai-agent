# Disposal of surplus stores

Chapter XI covers store items that have become surplus to a department’s requirements — inventory and non-inventory, serviceable or not. Items that can be used for trade-in are not surplus stores (SPR 1100). Dangerous or confiscated goods are Chapter XII, not this page. Loss or deficiency is [[write-off]], not disposal. See [[11-surplus-disposal]] and [[compare-stores-classes]].

## Who decides

Each [[controlling-officer|CO]] appoints a Departmental Disposal Committee (DDC): chair at or above senior professional level, and at least two other members at or above Assistant Supplies Officer / Executive Officer II or equivalent (SPR 1105). The DDC may approve disposal at or below the stores [[quotation]] limit in SPR 220(a) ($1.35 million). The CO or a designated directorate officer may, on the DDC’s recommendation, approve above that (SPR 1106). “Value” is GLD stock price, original purchase cost, or replacement cost (SPR 1107). The departmental disposal authority (DDA) is the DDC or the designated officer, depending on value (SPR 1115).

## The SPR 1110 tree (short form)

1. **Is it serviceable?** The DDA decides (SPR 1115).
2. **Unserviceable non-inventory** → dump if worn out / broken and certified beyond economical repair, or technically obsolete (SPR 1120).
3. **Unserviceable inventory** → sell to a [[dgl|GLD]] disposal term contractor, or dump if it is on the GLD waiver list (SPR 1125). If neither applies and there is residual value (display, training, preservation), follow the serviceable-allocated path; otherwise dump (SPR 1126).
4. **Serviceable GLD unallocated** → return to GLD (SPR 1130, 875).
5. **Serviceable allocated** (inventory and non-inventory) → ask DGL to post a CCGO notice for **not less than ten working days** (SPR 1135). Transfer to another department (or trading fund, possibly for a charge) on a first-come-first-served basis (SPR 1136).
6. **Not wanted in Government** → consider **donation** and **commercial disposal** in parallel; dump only if donation is unfit and sale is not cost-effective (SPR 1137, 1150).

## Donation, sale, dump

Donation to a non-government organisation is allowed if the body is educational, scientific, cultural, or charitable, will use the item non-profit, and the gift creates no Government capital or recurrent cost (SPR 1140). Over $300,000 needs [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] (SPR 1141). Outside SPR 1140 needs [[finance-committee|Finance Committee]] (SPR 1142).

Saleable items go to DGL for commercial disposal — term contractor, quotation, tender, or auction (SPR 1145). Other sale methods need PS(Tsy), applied through DGL. Dumping that does not fully meet the conditions needs DGL’s prior consent; DGL consults PS(Tsy) if in doubt (SPR 1151). If more NGOs apply than stock exists, DGL advises the releasing department whom to give what, using past donation records for equity (SPR 1144). COs still account for every movement; dumped items follow Appendix VII (SPR 1155). This tree is not used for trade-in, nor for materials in a dangerous condition (SPR 1200).
