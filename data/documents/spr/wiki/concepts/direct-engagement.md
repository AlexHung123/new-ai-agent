# Direct engagement

Direct engagement means engaging a contractor, supplier, or service provider **without** going through the Chapter III tender procedures (SPR 300(a)(v), 331). It is narrower than single [[tendering]]: there is no bidding process at all. Open bidding remains the norm; direct engagement is the exception and must be justified to the approving authority (SPR 109(a)(ii)). See [[03-tenders]] and [[compare-procurement-routes]].

## When it may be used

Only when open, selective, restricted, or single tendering would **not** be an effective means of obtaining the stores or services or procuring the revenue contract, supported by **very strong** justifications — for example [[national-security]], extreme urgency, or protection of patents, copyrights, or other exclusive rights (SPR 331). It must not be used to avoid competition.

If the buy is covered by [[wto-gpa]], departments refer to Article XIII and, where that article does not apply, Article III, and ask [[doj|D of J]] and/or LAD(W)/DEVB if in doubt.

## Who may approve (stores / services / revenue)

Authority to **initiate** direct engagement **and** to **accept** the offer rests with [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] (SPR 332). For stores, services excluding works and consultancy, and revenue contracts **not exceeding** the SPR 220(a) departmental limit ($10 million), that authority has been delegated to [[controlling-officer|COs]] **personally**. Requests, irrespective of value, are signed or endorsed at directorate level.

Approving officers follow FC No. 2/2019 (as updated) and exercise due diligence (SPR 333). Because there is no bidding, the paper must include, in addition to the SPR 325(d) single/restricted items (background, estimate, why open tender is ineffective, time-constraint explanation if used, shortlist criteria, policy-bureau support for PS(Tsy) cases, GPA legal advice, longer-term plan), the contracting strategy (Government interest and public perception) and the negotiation strategy with a baseline on cost or revenue.

## Consultants: one-firm approach is separate

Approaching a single consulting firm without a competitive selection is **not** this SPR 331 route. It is SPR 430(b), reserved to PS(Tsy) and **not** delegated to COs at any value. After PS(Tsy) approval the department still goes to [[ccsb|CCSB or DCSC]] on a combined Stages 1 and 2 paper. See [[consultants-selection]].

## After approval

Keep a separate record of each direct-engagement contract: department, value and kind of goods or services, and a statement of the circumstances that justified the route (SPR 390(c)(ii)). For a GPA-covered engagement the record also states the limited-tendering condition relied on (SPR 390(c)(iii)). Award still follows SPR 505–510. [[conflict-of-interest]] declarations still apply. Direct engagement is listed among the Chapter III exceptions (SPR 300(a)(v)); it is not a shortcut inside a live tender.
