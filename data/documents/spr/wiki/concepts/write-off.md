# Write-off

Write-off clears a **loss or deficiency of stores** from the ledger after the discrepancy has been investigated. It is not [[disposal]] of surplus, and it is not a [[contract-variation]]. The process sits in Chapter X after stock verification. See [[10-audit-writeoff]].

## How a case starts

When a [[controlling-officer|CO]] finds a physical/ledger discrepancy, deficiencies go out on GF 277 and surpluses come on charge on GF 278 (SPR 1030). If fraud, suspected fraud, or negligence is involved, the CO reports immediately in writing to [[dgl|DGL]], copying [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]], DAS, and D of A, and to the police if a criminal act is suspected (SPR 1035(a)). The investigation report follows, with recommendations, action against the officer, and steps to prevent recurrence (SPR 1035(b)). DGL may enquire (SPR 1035(c)).

Where **negligence (not fraud)** is involved and the value does **not exceed $50,000**, the CO may, subject to DGL’s advice, write off under SPR 1070 or require replacement under SPR 1075. If DGL disagrees, PS(Tsy) decides (SPR 1035(d)).

## SPR 1070 bands

**Caused by fraud, suspected fraud, or negligence**

| Case | Value (each case or any one cause) | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theft or suspected theft (SPR 1035(f)) | Not exceeding $10,000 | Deputy / Assistant Head of Department / Bureau of D2/DL2 or above |
| Theft or suspected theft (SPR 1035(f)) | Not exceeding $50,000 | Head of Department / Bureau or CO personally (any rank) |
| All other fraud / negligence | Not exceeding $500,000 | PS(Tsy) |
| All other fraud / negligence | Exceeding $500,000 | [[finance-committee\|Finance Committee]] |

**Not caused by fraud, suspected fraud, or negligence** (except SPR 1045 cases)

| Value | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Not exceeding $9,000 | Departmental Secretary at or above MPS 45, or equivalent officer designated by the CO to oversee stores |
| Not exceeding $36,000 | Directorate officer of D1/DL1 |
| Not exceeding $180,000 | Deputy / Assistant Head of Department of D2/DL2 or above |
| Not exceeding $900,000 | Head of Department / Bureau or CO personally |
| Exceeding $900,000 | PS(Tsy) |

## Replacement and surcharge

The CO, or a designated directorate officer at least one substantive rank above the officer involved, may allow replacement on repayment if there is no fraud, the total does not exceed $50,000, and replacement is reasonable. No GF 190 write-off is then needed (SPR 1075).

Surcharge under Cap. 2 s.32 is recommended by the CO to PS(Tsy): current replacement (or scrap / depreciated / repair) cost **plus 20% overheads** (SPR 1080). PS(Tsy) processes surcharge under the prevailing Financial Circular (SPR 1085).
