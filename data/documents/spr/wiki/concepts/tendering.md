# Tendering

Tender procedures in Chapter III apply to procurement and disposal of stores, services, construction / engineering works, and revenue contracts, except quotations within SPR 220, statutory or agreed auction-style disposals, land under the Abbreviated Tender System, consultancy (Chapters II and IV), [[direct-engagement]] under SPR 331–333, briefing-out by the Secretary for Justice, and employment of individuals (SPR 300(a)). See [[03-tenders]] and [[03-tenders-process]].

## The value gate

Above the [[quotation]] limits, tender (or consultants selection) is mandatory (SPR 221). [[controlling-officer|COs]] may still choose to tender below those limits. Who accepts is a function of type and value: [[dtc|DTC]] (stores / services / revenue except works ≤ $10 million), [[ctb|GLD Tender Board]] (≤ $60 million), Public Works Tender Board (works ≤ $200 million), or [[ctb|CTB]] advising [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] with no dollar limit (SPR 116, 310, 390). COs personally award some non-WTO GPA works (SPR 310(h)).

## Types (open is the norm)

Departments normally use **open tendering**; any interested contractor / supplier / service provider may bid (SPR 315–316). Alternatives need approval:

- **Selective** — invite from an approved list; still a two-step open process. PS(Tsy) approves non-works lists; PS(W) approves the public-works lists (SPR 320).
- **Single / restricted** (WTO GPA “limited tendering”) — invite one or a few. Not to avoid competition or to protect local firms. Grounds include extreme urgency, exclusive rights, failed open/selective exercise, additional deliveries, prototypes, utility companies, lease-required firms, and [[national-security]] (SPR 325). Initiation: CO personally within the SPR 220(a) departmental limit for stores / non-works services / revenue; [[dgl|DGL]] for GLD tenders; PS(Tsy) otherwise.
- **Prequalified** — PS(Tsy) on CTB advice; list normally valid one year (SPR 330).

Communications from document preparation until the acceptance decision are RESTRICTED (TENDER) (SPR 305). Contract documents themselves are not usually classified; RESTRICTED is enough for disputes and claims, CONFIDENTIAL only if disclosure would prejudice the HKSAR (SPR 305(b)).

[[parallel-tendering]] may start the invitation before funding is secured; it does not change the type of tender. After award, changes are [[contract-variation]], not a new tender, unless the change is itself a new procurement (SPR 520(e)).

## Process in short

Notice and period (SPR 340: normally ≥ 3 weeks; non-GPA works ≤ $52 million and other non-GPA buys may go down to ten days under FC 3/2009; [[wto-gpa]] ≥ 40 days for tenders, ≥ 25 days to prequalify) → documents and specifications (SPR 345–350) → opening → TAP of not less than two persons (SPR 370) → optional negotiations (SPR 385) → acceptance and award notice (SPR 390). Use a [[marking-scheme]] or lowest / highest conforming price. Do not split a requirement to dodge GPA (Appendix III(F)).
