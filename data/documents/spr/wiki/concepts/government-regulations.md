# Government Regulations

Government Regulations regulate the conduct of Government business, appointment terms and conditions of service, financial and accounting operations, management and procurement of Government stores and services, security of Government records, and other miscellaneous matters. They do not provide for necessary and self-evident exceptions (SPR 1). This wiki is Volume 4 only. See [[00-introduction]].

## Making and interpretation

Save for Financial and Accounting Regulations and Stores and Procurement Regulations, which are made under the Public Finance Ordinance (Cap. 2), Government Regulations are made by or with the authority of the Chief Executive. The sole authority for interpretation and application is the Chief Executive, or those to whom he has delegated that authority (SPR 2).

[[sfst|FS / SFST]] makes SPR under Cap. 2 s.11(1) (SPR 100). [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] is authorised to apply, interpret, and make exceptions to SPR and related Financial Circulars (SPR 4 note). SPR applies to all public officers except insofar as it is inconsistent with any enactment (SPR 105).

Government Regulations apply to all government servants except where a contrary intention appears, legislation makes an alternative provision, a regulation is inconsistent with applicable legislation, or the officer’s terms exclude a regulation (SPR 3).

## Circulars of equal force

Regulations may be supplemented by Circulars and Circular Memoranda of equal application and force (SPR 5, 105). For Volume 4 those are Financial Circulars and FSTBCM issued by SFST, and [[dgl|GLD]] Circulars and Circular Memoranda which, with PS(Tsy) approval, also have equal force (SPR 120(c)). Each circular series is numbered by calendar year; the first circular of the year lists which earlier ones remain in force (SPR 6).

Officers other than staff on daily rates must know the conduct-and-discipline regulations and those that appertain to their duties (SPR 10). Disobedience, neglect, or failure may lead to discipline and pecuniary responsibility for loss (SPR 11). Heads of Department must make those texts available and must not let copies walk when an officer transfers (SPR 12–14). Circulars are issued on distribution Scales A–D (SPR 16).
