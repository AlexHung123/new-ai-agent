# Stores classification

Government stores are all articles purchased or acquired on behalf of Government, excluding land and buildings (SPR 200(a)). Chapter VII then splits them twice: **who holds them**, and **whether they go on inventory**. Those classes decide ledgers, inspections, [[write-off]], and [[disposal]]. See [[07-accounting]] and [[compare-stores-classes]].

## GLD unallocated vs departmental

There are two types of stores (SPR 700):

**GLD unallocated stores** are purchased by [[dgl|DGL]] in bulk and held pending sale or issue to departments and other bodies (SPR 705(a)). Cost is first charged to the Unallocated Stores Suspense Account under Cap. 2 s.30, administered by DGL. On issue, DGL tells the Director of Accounting Services to debit the department and credit the suspense account (SPR 705(b)). Serviceable unallocated items that become surplus are **returned to GLD** (SPR 1130, 875), not donated or sold by the department.

**Departmental stores** are acquired by a department solely for its own use. They may be standard items drawn from GLD unallocated stock, or items the department bought direct (SPR 710(a)).

## Inventory vs non-inventory — the $1,000 line

Departmental stores are further classified (SPR 710(b)):

- **Inventory** — generally stores of a permanent or non-consumable nature **with a unit cost at or above $1,000 at the time of purchase**.
- **Non-inventory** — all other stores.

[[dsm|DSM]] accounts for inventory on GF 270 / GF 272 (and GF 273 for quarters or operational need), checks inventories at least once a year, and keeps a control index (SPR 715). Non-inventory held in a store unit is recorded on a Non-inventory Stores Ledger (GF 291); once issued, no further accounting is normally required (SPR 765). If a department wants a non-inventory item treated as inventory for control, it follows SPR 715–760 and then keeps treating it as inventory unless DGL agrees otherwise (SPR 775).

Special inventory (fire-fighting equipment, electrical and gas appliances, books, uniforms and accoutrements) has its own procedures at SPR 735–760.

## Why the class matters

- DSM inspections and surprise checks (SPR 896) run against whatever is on charge.
- Unserviceable **non-inventory** may be dumped by the departmental disposal authority; unserviceable **inventory** first tries a GLD term contractor or the GLD waiver list (SPR 1120–1125).
- A $1,000+ item becomes inventory the moment it is received, even if the buyer never opens Chapter VII.

Books, uniforms, fire-fighting equipment, and electrical or gas appliances have extra accounting rules at SPR 735–760. Transfers between GF 272 records go through DSM on GF 277 (SPR 730). See [[06-stores-records]] for the ledger and voucher set that sits under these classes.
