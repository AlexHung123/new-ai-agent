# Parallel tendering

Parallel tendering means inviting tenders — or starting a consultants selection — **before funding is secured** (SPR 337). It is a funding-sequence device, not a different bid type. The bid is still an open, selective, single/restricted, or prequalified [[tendering]] exercise (or a Chapter IV selection). See [[03-tenders]] and [[finance-committee]].

## When it is required, when it is optional

**Works contracts funded under the Capital Works Reserve Fund.** [[controlling-officer|COs]] **shall** adopt parallel tendering unless, after a risk assessment, they consider the benefits are outweighed by the risks (SPR 337(a)). Benefits cited: time saving, greater certainty in the Approved Project Estimate to be sought. Risks cited: abortive work if the project is not approved or is approved with unexpected conditions; Government being seen to pre-empt [[finance-committee|Finance Committee]] / Legislative Council. For estimated value **exceeding $30 million**, exemption from parallel tendering needs the relevant Director of Bureau (or a Permanent Secretary authorised in writing).

**Other contracts.** COs **may** adopt parallel tendering if, after the same kind of risk assessment, benefits outweigh risks (SPR 337(b)). If estimated value exceeds $30 million, they need the Director of Bureau (or authorised Permanent Secretary) **to adopt** it — the opposite polarity from CWRF works.

**Consultancies.** Works-related consultancies funded under CWRF follow SPR 337(a) and 338. Other consultancies follow SPR 337(b) and 338 (SPR 416). The invitation is still a Chapter IV two-stage (or DCSC) selection, not a Chapter III tender.

## What must be said, and when a contract may be awarded

Invitations must state correctly that funding approval has **not** been obtained, and must remind tenderers that Government is not responsible for their bid-preparation costs (SPR 338). As a general principle, COs should **not** award a contract and should **not** indicate to the successful bidder that Government would accept its bid unless and until funding is secured. Conditions for award are in FC No. 3/2020 (as updated).

## Cancellation if funding fails

Approval to cancel is **not** required when, in a parallel exercise, the relevant funding application is not approved by the Finance Committee before tender validity (or any extension) expires (SPR 380(d)). The same rule applies to a consultants selection (SPR 470(b)).

The same funding rule applies to consultants: CWRF works-related consultancies follow SPR 337(a); other consultancies follow SPR 337(b) (SPR 416).

Parallel tendering does not relax [[wto-gpa]] notice periods, [[national-security]] review, or [[conflict-of-interest]] declarations. It also does not move the accepting authority: a parallel CWRF works tender still goes to the [[ctb|Public Works Tender Board]], CTB / [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]], or the CO under SPR 310(h), as value and type dictate.
