# WTO Agreement on Government Procurement

Where a procurement is covered by the WTO Agreement on Government Procurement (WTO GPA), SPR overlays extra rules on notice, limited tendering, records, and challenge. [[controlling-officer|COs]] shall observe the Agreement (SPR 127). Covered decisions are reviewable by the [[review-body|Review Body on Bid Challenges]] as well as by the ordinary authorities (SPR 108, 160(b)). See [[03-tenders]] and [[01-authority]].

## When GPA applies

In estimating value, departments include the estimated **maximum total value** of the procurement over its **entire duration**, whether awarded to one or more contractors / suppliers / service providers, taking into account all forms of remuneration (SPR 300(b)). Do **not** split a requirement to stay under the threshold (Appendix III(F); overview of this wiki). Current numeric GPA thresholds are not printed in this compilation — confirm against the live FSTB / Trade and Industry text.

Consultancy selection under Chapter IV is **not** bound by WTO GPA, but officers still observe fair play and transparency (SPR 450(e)).

## Extra process rules

- Tender notices and prequalification invitations for covered procurements are published in the **Government Gazette** (SPR 340(c)). Notify listed suppliers on a selective-tender list (SPR 320).
- At least **40 days** for receipt of tenders and no less than **25 days** for applications to be prequalified. Extreme urgency to shorten the tender period needs [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] (delegated to [[dgl|DGL]] for GLD tenders and stores tenders within the departmental limit) (SPR 340(f)).
- Include the Note to Tenderers at Appendix III(E)1 (SPR 345).
- Single / restricted [[tendering]] is GPA “limited tendering”; attach [[doj|D of J]] or LAD(W)/DEVB advice on the GPA perspective (SPR 325(d)(vii)). [[direct-engagement]] on a covered buy: Articles XIII or III (SPR 331).
- Keep tender documents, submissions, reports, SPR 390(c) records, and electronic-traceability data for **not less than three years** from award (SPR 530(d)). Unsuccessful-firm documents on a non-GPA exercise may be destroyed three months after execution (SPR 530(c)).
- A [[contract-variation]] that amounts to a new covered procurement must itself meet GPA (SPR 520(e)).

## Bid challenge

A supplier may lodge a challenge within **ten working days** after he knew or reasonably should have known the basis. The Review Body may take a late challenge but not one filed later than **30 working days** (Appendix III(E)1). Secretariat: Trade and Industry Department.

Tender notices must say whether the intended procurement is covered by WTO GPA (SPR 340(e)). For covered selective tenders, departments notify and provide the notice to contractors already on the approved list (SPR 320). Qualification criteria for those lists are limited to what is essential to legal, financial, commercial, and technical capacity; a previous contract with a GPA entity must not be required (SPR 320(c)).

A three-year file is not optional: SPR 530(d) lists tender documents, all tenderer submissions, reports of procedures and awards including SPR 390(c) records, and electronic-traceability data.
