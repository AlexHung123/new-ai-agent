# Contract variation

After award, a department may need to change the contract. Chapter V applies to stores, services, and revenue contracts whether they were let by [[quotation]], [[tendering]], or [[consultants-selection]] (SPR 500). Variations are a stop-gap, not a way to buy a new requirement without a competition. Payments themselves are not variations: they follow the contract and should normally be made within 30 calendar days of accepting the deliverables (SPR 515(a)). See [[05-contract-admin]].

## Hard stops

- A department may **not** vary a contract so that the approved commitment or approved project estimate is exceeded (SPR 520(a)).
- The **cumulative** value of variations should not normally exceed **50%** of the original contract value. Contingencies and optional items contemplated in the contract do not count as part of that original value (SPR 520(b)–(c)). Excesses need fully documented justifications and should normally be used only as a stop-gap, unless justified under the current [[dgl|GLD]] Circular.

## Who may approve a no-increase variation

A [[controlling-officer|CO]], or a designated officer of appropriate rank, may approve any number of variations — including an extension of a departmental contract, and minor variations to a GLD contract — that are inevitable and do **not** increase the original contract value (or do not affect it, for a revenue contract), provided all relevant factors, including rates, remain no less favourable (SPR 520(b)).

## Who may approve an increase

Any variation that increases the original value (or affects a revenue contract’s value), or any increase in contract sums, must be approved by the authority in **Appendix V(B)** (SPR 520(c)). Officers interpret those limits strictly and must not insert offsetting decreases to stay under a ceiling (SPR 520(d)). Where one change decreases value and another increases it, only the increase counts toward the Appendix V(B) band. Where original items are replaced as part and parcel of one variation, only the **net** value counts.

## GPA and negotiation

A variation, especially one not provided for in the original contract, may amount to a **new procurement**. If that new procurement is covered by [[wto-gpa]], all GPA requirements apply. In doubt, ask [[doj|D of J]] or, for works, LAD(W)/DEVB (SPR 520(e)).

If the department proposes to vary terms, it seeks the SPR 520 / Appendix V(B) authority’s agreement on the variation **and** on the negotiation strategy or bottom line first (SPR 525). For GLD contracts, [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] has delegated contract-negotiation approval to DGL. Conduct follows SPR 385 and Appendix III(J). Copy approved variations and any supplementary agreements to the Director of Accounting Services (SPR 520(f)).

Appendix V(B) ranks are operational and move by circular; do not treat a remembered band as current. Works contracts also follow DEVB guidance on what counts as an optional item (SPR 520(b)). A variation is not a [[write-off]] and not a [[disposal]].
