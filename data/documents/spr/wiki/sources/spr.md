# Source summary — Stores and Procurement Regulations

Volume 4 of the HKSAR Government Regulations. Made by the [[sfst|Financial Secretary / SFST]] under section 11(1) of the Public Finance Ordinance (Cap. 2). [[pstsy]] is authorised to apply, interpret, and make exceptions to SPR and related Financial Circulars. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_spr_e_clean.md` (~3,370 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

Printed effective date: **31 July 2026**.

## Force

[[government-regulations|Government Regulations]] bind public officers except where inconsistent with an enactment (SPR 105). Financial Circulars, FSTBCM, and (with PS(Tsy) approval) GLD Circulars have equal force (SPR 105, 120(c)). See [[00-introduction]].

## Reading the conversion

- Repeated chapter banners are running headers, not new sections.
- SPR 116 and SPR 1110 are images; board limits and the disposal tree are transcribed on [[ctb]], [[ccsb]], and [[11-surplus-disposal]].
- Some article numbers in the conversion are missing or duplicated in headings (e.g. a “5.” before SPR 385; “10.” before SPR 1010). Trust the numbered SPR, not the stray list marker.
- Form numbers (GF 219, GF 230, …) identify stationery; they are not separate legal instruments.
- Dollar limits and rank tables move by Financial Circular. Treat 31 July 2026 figures as the compilation’s snapshot, not a promise they are still current.
