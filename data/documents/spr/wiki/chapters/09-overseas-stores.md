# Chapter IX — Overseas Collection and Delivery

Chapter IX covers stores consigned from suppliers outside Hong Kong. [[dgl|DGL]] is the default receiving agent. Claims that cannot be recovered become [[write-off]] under [[10-audit-writeoff]].

## Receiving agent (SPR 900–915)

DGL may help departments receive specialised imports — the example given is arms and ammunition — consigned by overseas suppliers (SPR 900).

A bill of lading or airway bill addressed to the department is endorsed **“For delivery to the Director of Government Logistics”**, signed on behalf of the [[controlling-officer|CO]], and sent to DGL (SPR 905).

Keep packing materials in separate lots, and keep the related documents, until every item said to have been delivered is actually received. If something is missing, the packing is used to check gross and net weight against the shipping papers (SPR 915).

## Receipt and inspection (SPR 920–930)

On receipt from DGL or direct from the overseas supplier, take on charge the **full invoiced quantity** at once (SPR 920).

Raise Inspection Report of Goods Received (Imported) (**GF 280**) and inspect (SPR 925):

- **general stores — within seven days** of delivery;
- **stores needing special testing — within 30 days**, or the period in the order/contract.

After inspection, complete GF 280 with any difference from the invoice or packing list and any loss or damage. For stores received through GLD, send a copy to DGL (SPR 930).

## Claims (SPR 935–965)

Lodge a claim if the stores are not in good order **and** either:

- the loss or damage exceeds **$1,000**, or
- essential accessories or parts of any equipment are missing or damaged, **regardless of value** (SPR 935).

GLD purchases: send Claims Report (**GF 296**) to DGL, or notify through GLD’s Procurement and Contract Management System, so GLD opens a Claim Record and claims on the liable parties (then SPR 955–965). Direct departmental purchases: the department claims (SPR 950–965).

A claim is **not settled** until the value has been recovered in full, the stores have been replaced, or a write-off has been authorised (SPR 940).

Keep a Claim Register (**GF 207**). An irreparable broken item is treated as a deficiency (SPR 945). After settlement, strike the broken or deficient quantity off charge with GF 277, cross-referenced to the Claim Register (SPR 950).

**GLD accounting (SPR 955).** Debit the department’s subhead for the stores; open a Claim Record Card from the system notice or GF 296. If the claim amount is known, journal-transfer a credit to the department and a debit to “Advances—Claims”, then clear the Advance Account when the liable party pays (same or later year). If the amount is not yet known (broken plant, foreign currency, repair charges still open), credit the subhead if the money arrives in the same year, or revenue if later. Debit subsequent replacements to the department. A free replacement after admitted liability needs no further DGL action except reversing an Advance Account that was opened. Direct purchases follow SPR 955(a), (c), (d) and (e); DAS will open an Advance Account on request (SPR 960).

**No claim** if breakages or deficiencies do **not exceed $1,000** ledger value **and** the stores are not essential. Write those items off under SPR 1040(b) (SPR 965).
