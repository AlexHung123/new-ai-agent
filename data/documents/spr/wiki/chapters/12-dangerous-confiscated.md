# Chapter XII — Dangerous or Confiscated Goods

Two regimes sit here: **immediate [[disposal]] of materials already dangerous**, and **handover of confiscated goods** to the department the law names. Residual categories go to [[dgl|DGL]] (SPR 1220). Ordinary surplus is [[11-surplus-disposal]].

## Dangerous condition (SPR 1200)

When the [[controlling-officer|CO]] considers that materials under control are in a dangerous condition and might endanger life or property, the CO may authorise **immediate disposal**, after consulting the Director of Fire Services, the Government Chemist, or the Senior Physicist in-charge, Radiation Health Unit, Department of Health, as appropriate. Keep records in the [[appendices|Appendix VII]] format.

Storage *before* goods become dangerous is SPR 805 ([[08-stores-management]]).

## Confiscation handover (SPR 1205–1210)

The department that initiates confiscation hands the goods to the department ultimately responsible under the relevant legislation and/or standing instruction of the Financial Secretary (SPR 1215 and 1220) (SPR 1205).

Handover is on Confiscation Order **GF 83**, citing the ordinance or the court/officer who ordered forfeiture (SPR 1210):

- original — officer-in-charge of the case, on the charge sheet;
- duplicate — issuing office;
- triplicate — receiving department, to support the forfeited-goods ledger;
- quadruplicate — initiating department.

## Who disposes of what (SPR 1215–1220)

| Department | Confiscated goods (SPR 1215) |
| --- | --- |
| AFCD | Tree wood; fish |
| CEDD | Category I dangerous goods under the Dangerous Goods (Application and Exemption) Regulations (Cap. 295) **other than ammunition**; minerals confiscated under the Mining Ordinance (Cap. 285) |
| Customs and Excise | Goods (including kerosene, gasoline and diesel) to which the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) applies; rice and dried food not fit for human consumption |
| EMSD | LPG under the Gas Safety Ordinance (Cap. 51) |
| Fire Services | Dangerous goods listed in Cap. 295 **other than** Category I, kerosene, gasoline, diesel, and LPG under Cap. 51 |
| FEHD | Perishable goods for destruction |
| Hong Kong Police Force | Arms and ammunition; counterfeit currency (notes and coins) under Standing Accounting Instruction 1971 |
| Police **or** C&E | Dangerous-drugs paraphernalia |
| Police **or** C&E **or** Department of Health | Dangerous drugs under Cap. 134; the conversion also repeats “dried food not fit for human consumption” here (possible OCR glue with C&E above) |
| Treasury | Currency under Standing Accounting Instruction 1970 |

DGL disposes of any confiscated goods **not** listed in SPR 1215. The initiating department reports to DGL; DGL, after weighing likely transport and storage cost, chooses the method (SPR 1220).

## Destruction powers (SPR 1225)

Named COs may destroy specified confiscated goods:

| CO | Goods and ordinance |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioner of Customs and Excise | (a) Perishable goods, or goods whose sale would harm the public — Import and Export Ordinance (Cap. 60). (b) Obscene books, films, magazines, photographs, etc. — Post Office Ordinance (Cap. 98). (c) Forged trade mark or false trade description where the marking cannot be obliterated — Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362). (d) Copyright-infringing items unsuitable for public sale — Copyright Ordinance (Cap. 528) and Prevention of Copyright Piracy Ordinance (Cap. 544) |
| C&E **and** Commissioner of Police | (e) Controlled chemicals (Cap. 145) — *Note 1*. (f) Obscene publications, video tapes, VCDs, photographs, etc. (Cap. 390) — *Note 2* |
| C&E, Police **and** Director of Film, Newspaper and Article Administration | (g) Indecent articles that do not meet statutory publication requirements (Cap. 390) — *Note 2* |
| Police **and** Director of Food and Environmental Hygiene | (h) Hawker’s equipment, carts, trolleys and stalls — Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance (Cap. 132) |

**Note 1** is printed only as “Exceptions —” with no body *(source unclear; verify against printed SPR)*. **Note 2:** projecting/showing machinery, and printing plates, instruments or utensils, that are suitable for public sale go to DGL under SPR 1220.

## Perishables and destruction paperwork (SPR 1230–1235)

Perishable goods handed to a recognised charitable institution: the institution signs the **triplicate** of GF 83 and returns it to the issuing department; the quadruplicate is not used (SPR 1230).

Goods earmarked for destruction: the officers who destroy them certify on the **triplicate** of GF 83; again no quadruplicate (SPR 1235).
