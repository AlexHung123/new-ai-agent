# Chapter XI — Disposal of Surplus Stores

This chapter is the [[disposal]] tree for stores a department no longer needs — inventory and non-inventory, serviceable or not. Items that can be **traded in** are not surplus (SPR 1100). Return of GLD stock is [[08-stores-management|SPR 875]].

SPR 1110 is a flowchart image in the source. The tree below is the written path in SPR 1115–1151.

## Departmental Disposal Committee (SPR 1105–1108)

Each [[controlling-officer|CO]] appoints a **DDC**: chair at or above senior professional level, and at least two other members at or above Assistant Supplies Officer or Executive Officer II (or equivalent), from inside the department (SPR 1105).

DDC may approve disposal up to the stores [[quotation]] limit in SPR 220(a) (**$1.35 million** in this compilation). It may sub-delegate **non-inventory unserviceable** items to designated officers not below ASO / EO II. Above the DDC limit, the CO or a designated directorate officer approves **on the DDC’s recommendation** (SPR 1106).

**Value** = GLD stock price, or original purchase cost, or replacement cost if neither is easy to fix. The SPR 1106 threshold applies to each item or, if several items, their collective value in each case or one cause (SPR 1107). The CO chooses how the DDC works, provided it is transparent and has checks and balances (SPR 1108).

**DDA** (departmental disposal authority) is the DDC or the designated officer in SPR 1106, depending on value. DDA decides whether a surplus item is serviceable (SPR 1115).

## Disposal tree (SPR 1110–1151)

```
Surplus item
├─ Unserviceable
│  ├─ Non-inventory → dump (SPR 1120)
│  └─ Inventory
│     ├─ on a GLD disposal term contract → sell to the term contractor (SPR 1125(a))
│     ├─ on the GLD waiver list (low value) → dump (SPR 1125(b))
│     └─ otherwise
│        ├─ residual value (display, training, preservation, …)
│        │    → treat as the serviceable path (SPR 1126 → 1135–1151)
│        └─ no residual value → dump (SPR 1126)
└─ Serviceable
   ├─ GLD unallocated → return under SPR 875 (SPR 1130)
   └─ other allocated, inventory or non-inventory
      → CCGO notice ≥ 10 working days (SPR 1135)
         ├─ another department wants it → transfer, first come (SPR 1136)
         └─ not wanted in Government
            → donation / commercial / dump in parallel (SPR 1137)
```

**Unserviceable** means unsuitable because worn out or broken and certified beyond economical repair by the supplier, a competent technician or a technical department, or because it is technically obsolete (SPR 1120).

**CCGO (SPR 1135–1136).** DDA asks [[dgl|DGL]] to post availability on the Central Cyber Government Office. The notice states condition (still serviceable? spare parts?) and a contact. DGL updates the site at least weekly and leaves each notice up **no less than ten working days**. Transfer and account reconciliation follow SPR 865. Trading-fund transfers may be charged; put the proposed charge on the notice. Competing bids: **first come, first served**.

After CCGO, if Government does not want the item, consider donation, commercial disposal and dumping **in parallel**. Residual value, resaleable value and public interest decide; ask DGL about resaleable value where useful (SPR 1137).

## Donation (SPR 1140–1144)

DDA may donate, on its own initiative or on request, if all three hold (SPR 1140):

- the recipient is established solely or principally for **educational, scientific, cultural or charitable** purposes;
- the items will be used only for a **non-profit** purpose;
- there is **no** capital or recurrent cost to Government.

Value above **$300,000** in each case or one cause: prior [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] approval (SPR 1141). A donation that does **not** meet SPR 1140 needs the [[finance-committee|Finance Committee]] (SPR 1142).

Liaise with the NGO directly or through DGL. After approval, the releasing department issues the items and tells DGL who received what (SPR 1143). If applications exceed stock, DGL advises allocations from past donation records so that gifts stay equitable (SPR 1144).

## Commercial disposal and dumping (SPR 1145–1151)

If the item has saleable value, ask DGL to arrange commercial disposal. DGL may send it to a GLD disposal term contractor, or run a quotation, tender or auction, weighing value against cost. Any other method needs PS(Tsy) approval, applied for through DGL with full justifications (SPR 1145).

Dump only when the item is not wanted in Government, is not fit to donate, and commercial disposal is not cost-effective (SPR 1150). Dumping that does not fully meet those conditions needs DGL’s prior consent; if in doubt DGL consults PS(Tsy) (SPR 1151).

Whatever the route, the CO must keep Chapter VII accounts. Dumped items use the [[appendices|Appendix VII]] certificate (SPR 1155).
