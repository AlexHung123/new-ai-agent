# Chapter X — Audit and Write-off

Four bodies may audit procurement and stores management or verify stock on ledger charge (SPR 1000). Losses are [[write-off|written off]] on a rank-and-value table (SPR 1070). High-value fraud or negligence goes to the [[finance-committee|Finance Committee]]. Surcharge sits on Cap. 2 s.32 (SPR 1080).

The conversion prints SPR 1010 as “10.”, and drops the numbers on the no-fault write-off article and the surcharge article. Sequence and later citations identify them as **SPR 1040** and **SPR 1080**.

## Four audit bodies (SPR 1000–1025)

Except for a Special Board, [[dgl|DGL]] sets the checking criteria; [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] may amend or except them (SPR 1000(b)).

1. **GLD Procurement and Stores Management Audit (PSMA) Section** — complete or random verification **at least once every three years**. Terms: procurement compliance (including due diligence); stores-management compliance; ledger vs stock; dormant and excessive stock; security advice. DGL sends the report and recommendations to the [[controlling-officer|CO]], copy D of A (SPR 1005).
2. **Boards of Survey appointed by DGL** — used when PSMA cannot finish within the three-year period. Unless DGL authorises otherwise, no staff of the department under survey sit on the board. Report on GF 236 with GF 241; DGL forwards it to the CO (SPR 1030–1065), copy D of A (SPR 1010).
3. **Departmental stock verification team** appointed by the CO. Unless DGL agrees otherwise: large stores progressively, **each item at least once every three years**; small stores completely **at least once a year**, preferably about six months after the last PSMA or Board check; and completely immediately before and after a store move. Compare ledger to stock (GF 241); examine condition and storage; examine warehouses, locks and fastenings. Reports on GF 237 (SPR 1015–1020).
4. **Special Board of Survey** appointed by the Chief Secretary for Administration where fraud or **gross negligence** is suspected, or as the CS thinks necessary. Report in quadruplicate through DGL; DGL keeps one copy and sends three to the CS with comments (SPR 1025).

## Discrepancies (SPR 1030–1065)

Book a deficiency on GF 277 (citing the adjusting authority) and a surplus on GF 278 (SPR 1030).

**Fraud, suspected fraud or negligence** (SPR 1035). Report immediately in writing to DGL, copies PS(Tsy), DAS and D of A. Report to the police if a criminal act is involved or suspected. Investigate; send the report (and police report if any) to the same addressees, with recommendations, action against the officer, and steps to stop a recurrence; monthly progress reports where appropriate. DGL may enquire and recommend to PS(Tsy).

- Negligence only, value **≤ $50,000**: after investigation, and subject to DGL’s advice, the CO may write off under SPR 1070 or seek replacement under SPR 1075. If DGL disagrees with the CO, PS(Tsy) decides (SPR 1035(d)).
- Otherwise apply to PS(Tsy) on GF 403 (SPR 1070(a)(ii)), any amount, copies DAS, D of A and DGL — except the small-theft track below.
- **Theft or suspected theft** is treated as fraud or negligence, **except** when the loss does **not exceed $50,000**: after departmental investigation the department may write off on GF 190 under SPR 1070(a)(i) (SPR 1035(f)).

**No fault** (SPR 1040). Above **$900,000** in any one case: apply to PS(Tsy) on GF 402, copies DAS, D of A and DGL, with the supporting information in Annex B of FC 5/2017. **≤ $900,000**: departmental write-off on GF 190 under SPR 1070(b)(i)–(iv). The approving officer signs GF 190 personally and certifies: no fraud/suspected fraud/negligence; investigation recorded and recurrence addressed; within delegated authority; stores irrecoverable.

Half-yearly **GF 401** to PS(Tsy) (description and amount of each SPR 1040(b) write-off), within two months of period-end, copies DAS, D of A and DGL; nil return required.

DGL may write off deficiencies in GLD’s Stock Movement Journal up to **$50,000 in any one month** if not caused by fraud, suspected fraud or negligence (SPR 1045).

Surplus of one store above **$50,000**: CO reports to DGL, copies PS(Tsy), DAS and D of A (SPR 1050).

**Value** = GLD stock price, or original purchase cost, or (if neither is easy to fix) replacement cost (SPR 1055). COs must look at protective measures (SPR 1060). Nothing here stops DAS, D of A or DGL investigating a loss they think needs it (SPR 1065).

## Write-off authorities (SPR 1070)

Limits are “in each case or in respect of any one cause”.

**Caused by fraud, suspected fraud or negligence**

| Track | Value | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theft / suspected theft (SPR 1035(f)) | ≤ $10,000 | Deputy/Assistant Head of Department/Bureau, D2/DL2 or above |
| Theft / suspected theft (SPR 1035(f)) | ≤ $50,000 | Head of Department/Bureau or CO **personally** (any rank) |
| All other fraud / negligence | ≤ $500,000 | [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]] |
| All other fraud / negligence | > $500,000 | [[finance-committee\|Finance Committee]] |

**Not caused by fraud, suspected fraud or negligence** (except SPR 1045)

| Value | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| ≤ $9,000 | Departmental Secretary at MPS 45 or above, or another equivalent-rank officer designated by the CO to oversee stores |
| ≤ $36,000 | Directorate officer, D1/DL1 |
| ≤ $180,000 | Deputy/Assistant Head of Department, D2/DL2 or above |
| ≤ $900,000 | Head of Department/Bureau or CO personally |
| > $900,000 | PS(Tsy) |

## Replacement and surcharge (SPR 1075–1085)

The CO, or a designated directorate officer **at least one substantive rank higher** than the officer involved, may approve replacement by that officer if: no fraud or suspected fraud; total value **≤ $50,000**; and replacement is reasonable given the duty to protect public funds and the circumstances. After repayment, no GF 190 write-off. DSM supports the replacement issue with GF 277 quoting the demand-note number (SPR 1075).

After a SPR 1035(b) investigation the CO may recommend to PS(Tsy) a surcharge under **Cap. 2 s.32**. Unless PS(Tsy) authorises otherwise (SPR 1080):

- **new** — current replacement cost + 20% overhead;
- **used** — the greater of scrap value and depreciated value + 20%;
- **repairable** (not beyond economical repair) — repair cost + 20%.

Depreciated value = current replacement cost × (unexpired useful life ÷ useful life). GLD and the specialist department fix replacement cost, life, scrap and repair; if the item is out of production, use the equivalent substitute. An officer who objects may, at own expense, appoint a Government-approved independent valuer; that valuation binds both sides. PS(Tsy) processes surcharge cases under the prevailing Financial Circular (SPR 1085).
