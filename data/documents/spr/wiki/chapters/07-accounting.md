# Chapter VII — Accounting for Stores

Two cross-cutting classes decide how a store is booked: **who holds it** (GLD unallocated vs departmental) and **what it is** (inventory vs non-inventory). Full comparison: [[stores-classification]]. Day-to-day custody is [[08-stores-management]]; discrepancies go to [[10-audit-writeoff]].

The conversion drops several article numbers between SPR 715 and 765. Later text still cites **SPR 720** (general inventory method) and **SPR 740–760** (special inventory). Fire-fighting equipment is numbered SPR 740. Electrical/gas, books, and uniforms are treated below as SPR 745, 750, and 755–760 (verify against printed SPR).

## Two types of stores (SPR 700–710)

**GLD unallocated stores** are bought in bulk by [[dgl|DGL]] and held pending issue or sale. Cost first hits the Unallocated Stores Suspense Account under section 30 of the Public Finance Ordinance (Cap. 2), administered by DGL. On issue to a department, DGL tells DAS to debit that department’s subhead and credit the suspense account (SPR 705).

**Departmental stores** are acquired solely for the department’s own use — either drawn from GLD unallocated stock or bought (or otherwise obtained) by the department (SPR 710(a)).

Departmental stores split again (SPR 710(b)):

- **Inventory** — generally permanent or non-consumable **and** unit cost **≥ $1,000** at purchase.
- **Non-inventory** — everything else.

## General inventory (SPR 715–730)

[[dsm|DSM]] accounts for inventory received and issued: the store-unit officer posts every movement and cross-references Inventory Stores Ledger Sheet (GF 270) and Inventory Sheet and Distribution Record (GF 272) to the order or issue voucher (GF 219 or GF 277); inventories are checked **at least once a year** and discrepancies follow SPR 1030; a control index tracks inventory sheets in use (SPR 715).

**SPR 720 method** (number missing in the conversion; cited by later articles):

- Stock first: on charge to GF 270; on issue, strike off GF 270 and enter in bulk on the entity’s GF 272 (branch, section, floor, …). Quarters or operational need: also the reverse of GF 272 and Room Inventory Record (GF 273), held by both DSM and the inventory holder.
- Immediate use: same as issue, without a stop on GF 270.
- On loan from individuals or NGOs: same method for the loan period.
- Donated items: bring on charge with Departmental Stores Return Note (GF 278) once acceptance is approved, then follow the stock or immediate-use path.

Transfers between GF 272s use GF 277 routed through DSM. Transfers between GF 273s under the same GF 272 need no voucher if both records are updated and the receiving holder signs (SPR 730).

## Special inventory (SPR 735–760)

Fire-fighting equipment, electrical and gas appliances, books, and uniforms follow SPR 740–760 instead of the general path (SPR 735).

**Fire-fighting equipment (SPR 740).** The user department buys portable gear in its buildings, accounts for it on GF 272, and arranges install, test, maintain, inspect and repair. Loss or damage: write-off under SPR 1035–1040.

**Electrical and gas appliances (SPR 745).** Non-specialist items are those listed on Inventory Distribution Sheet of Electrical and Gas Appliances (GF 269); specialist items are not. Office non-specialist: SPR 720 method on GF 269 instead of GF 272. Quarters non-specialist: GF 273, summarised on GF 269. Specialist: SPR 720. EMSD accounts for non-specialist appliances in each non-departmental quarter on EMSD 32 and summarises them on GF 269.

**Books (SPR 750).** Three classes: non-inventory (not taken on charge); books on departmental charge (SPR 720); library books. Each library keeps an Accession Register (GF 39) and allots the next accession number on receipt (on the binding panel, or inside the front cover if that is not practicable). A Loan Register records issues.

**Uniforms (SPR 755–760).** Supply and design follow CSR 570–572 and the approved Uniform Scale. The [[controlling-officer|CO]] may personally exempt low-value, hygiene-sensitive items (socks, stockings, pantyhose) from return on replacement or departure. Items bearing insignia, badges, logo or the department’s name or acronym **must** be returned.

Each officer has a serially numbered Uniform Record Card (GF 203) or an approved departmental equivalent, under a control index. Unless DGL authorises otherwise: initial issue on GF 277; replacement on Exchange Voucher (GF 275) with the old item (except exempted ones). Counter issues go on GF 292 and GF 203; a completed GF 292 is summarised onto GF 277 and struck off GF 270. Unserviceable returns (except exempted) go to the Unserviceable Ledger (SPR 620(a)) for Chapter XI condemnation. Used but serviceable returns from outgoing officers go to the serviceable (used) ledger and may be re-issued as a supplementary issue above scale.

## Non-inventory (SPR 765–775)

Stock in a departmental store unit is recorded on Non-inventory Stores Ledger (GF 291). Once issued, no further accounting is required except under SPR 775. Immediate-use, Working Stores Table, or job issues state the purpose on the voucher (GF 210, GF 219, or a cross-reference to GF 277) (SPR 765).

Where a unit regularly draws large quantities, DSM may set a Working Stores Table with an approved maximum stock level (SPR 770).

A department may treat selected non-inventory items as inventory for control, using SPR 715–760. Once so treated, they stay inventory unless [[dgl|DGL]] agrees otherwise (SPR 775).
