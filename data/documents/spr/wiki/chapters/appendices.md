# Appendices I–VII

Specimen forms and guidelines attached to SPR. This page is a catalogue only — do not treat it as the live form. Specimens live in the source after the Chapter XII text; they are not copied here. A **Glossary** of abbreviations (AACSB, CCGO, CO, CTB, DGL, DSM, PS(Tsy), WTO GPA, …) follows Appendix VII at the end of `MinerU_markdown_spr_e_clean.md`.

Conflict forms: [[01a-conflict-of-interest]]. Tender specimens: [[03-tenders]], [[03-tenders-process]]. Consultants: [[04-consultants]]. Contract execution and variation: [[05-contract-admin]]. Stores registers and dumping certificates: [[06-stores-records]], [[08-stores-management]], [[11-surplus-disposal]], [[12-dangerous-confiscated]].
