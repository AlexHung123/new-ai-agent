# Chapter II — Procurement of stores, services and revenue contracts

SPR 200–298 is the departmental [[quotation]] chapter: definitions, the value gates, segregation of roles, and how to invite and accept quotes. Beyond those gates, use [[tendering]] ([[03-tenders]]) or [[consultants-selection]] ([[04-consultants]]). Route choice is compared on [[compare-procurement-routes]]. [[dgl|GLD]] is the stores agent above the departmental limit (SPR 235).

## Definitions and anti-evasion (SPR 200–217)

For SPR, **government stores** are all articles purchased or acquired on behalf of Government, **excluding land and buildings**. **Services** are tasks performed by firms for Government, including construction and engineering works, consultancy, and other general services. A mixed contract is classified by the larger component: stores-plus-incidental-services is a stores contract if the services do not exceed the stores; the converse is a services contract. **Revenue contracts** generate revenue for Government (SPR 200).

Financial limits are the **total** value of stores or services of a similar nature, or of revenue, that in normal practice is obtained or generated in a single purchase or contract. Do **not** evade limits by splitting into instalments or shortening the usual contract duration. Consolidate similar requirements. A CO who allows or orders a buy without proper authority may be held personally responsible for the amount spent or the revenue lost (SPR 205).

[[national-security|National security]] and the SPR 106–109 principles apply **regardless of value, means, or type**. Escalate national-security issues to directorate officers at the earliest opportunity; those officers steer the response (SPR 205A).

For replacements, consider trade-in. Value the purchase on the **new** stores, not the net after trade-in (SPR 206). Some items need a named endorsement authority (Appendix II) (SPR 210). Procurement is normally at headquarters; the CO may authorise branches (SPR 216).

**Segregation (SPR 217).** Users of the stores or services, and officers who initiate a revenue contract, should not be the authority that invites bids or accepts the offer. The officer who approves or selects whom to invite **shall not** be the officer who authorises acceptance.

## The SPR 220 gates

[[sfst|FS]] has delegated to [[controlling-officer|COs]] authority to procure up to the following limits (SPR 220). “Up to and including.”

| Type | Quotation limit | Departmental limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) Stores; general services (not works); revenue | $1.35 million | $10 million |
| (a) Consultancy (general and works-related) | $3 million | $10 million |
| (b) Construction / engineering works | $7 million | $52 million, for non-[[wto-gpa]] works under the simplified tendering arrangement in FC 3/2009 (as updated) |

Within the quotation limit, follow this chapter — or, if the CO wants more competition, Chapter III or IV. Beyond the quotation limit, Chapter III or IV is mandatory (SPR 220–221). Tender / consultants selection procedures, including a [[marking-scheme]] in general, then apply and the relevant board or committee must advise or approve (SPR 221). A marking scheme may also be used on a quotation; authority and the SMS Framework are in Appendix III(G). As a general rule, **do not** set tenderer’s experience as an essential requirement in non-works, non-consultancy buys (SPR 221A).

**Notes to SPR 220.** Disposal sales of government stores or confiscated goods go through GLD (Note 1). “Works” here means services under UN CPC Division 51 (Note 2). Works classification follows DEVB TC(W) No. 5/2012 and DEVB guidelines (Note 3).

Invite quotes from GLD lists or from a departmental list published (method and admission) on the departmental webpage (SPR 222).

## Stores through GLD or the department (SPR 235–255)

[[dgl|DGL]] is the procurement agent for **stores beyond the SPR 220(a) departmental limit**. Ask DGL for stores that are neither in GLD unallocated stock nor on a GLD bulk contract (SPR 235(a)). Below that limit, departments may ask DGL to prepare documents and invite tenders, or run their own tender (SPR 235(b)). Use GLD’s Procurement and Contract Management System (SPR 235(c)). Use general specifications (SPR 350); a specification that suits one supplier needs exceptional justification (SPR 235(d)). Air delivery is the exception (SPR 235(e)). Attach any Appendix II endorsement to the request (SPR 235(f)).

Departments may buy stores direct up to the departmental limit if the item is not in GLD unallocated stock or on a GLD bulk contract; otherwise obtain DGL’s prior consent on [[value-for-money]] grounds. Except urgent minor purchases (SPR 265), follow SPR 260 or Chapter III (SPR 245).

**Repeated same-item purchases in any 12 months must not, in aggregate, exceed the quotation limit.** Regular demand above that limit goes to tender. For FOB purchases outside Hong Kong the quotation limit is the FOB value; freight, insurance, and incidentals are excluded (SPR 246).

The CO designates an officer, not below Assistant Supplies Officer / Executive Officer II or equivalent (unless DGL approves otherwise), to approve formal orders; that officer reports to the [[dsm|DSM]] (SPR 255).

## Quotation bands and ranks (SPR 260, 280, 295)

The same two bands apply to stores, services, and revenue:

- **≤ $50,000** (except urgent minor): normally more than one quote; accept the lowest conforming offer, or the conforming offer with the highest overall score if a marking scheme is used. Selector not below Supplies Supervisor II or equivalent; accepter not below ASO / EO II, who certifies prices reasonable. Stores and services go on purchasing cards unless the CO (or a designated officer not below CEO) authorises otherwise.
- **> $50,000 up to the quotation limit:** invite **at least five written** quotes (e-mail, fax, or post count as written), unless a higher rank authorises fewer.

**Rank pattern, not a full grid.** Invitation (SPR 260(d) / 280(d)) and acceptance (SPR 260(g) / 280(g)) each have a column **A** (five written quotes invited / received; accept lowest or highest-score conforming) and a column **B** (fewer than five invited or received, or accept a higher / not-highest-score conforming offer with full justification). Thresholds are **20% / 50% / 100% of the relevant SPR 220 quotation limit**. Column B is one rank above column A. For stores, invitation starts at ASO / EO II and acceptance one rank higher (SO / EO I) at the 20% band; the top acceptance cell is D1. For services, non-works ranks track the stores table except that consultancy invitation at the full SPR 220(a)(iii) limit starts at CSO / CEO (B = D1), and consultancy acceptance at that limit is D1 (B = D2). Works (SPR 220(b)) use the Engineer grade (Assistant Engineer → Government Engineer). *Do not treat this summary as the authority table; read SPR 260(d), 260(g), 280(d), and 280(g) in the printed SPR.*

Half-yearly returns of purchases over $50,000 go to DGL (periods ending 31 March and 30 September). Purchases over $500,000 need an explanation if fewer than five written quotes were received or a higher / not-highest-score offer was accepted (SPR 260(c)). Written quotes return in sealed envelopes, via e-Procurement, or by e-mail/fax if the inviting officer so authorised; a two-member opening team (leader not below ASO / EO II, or Assistant Engineer for works) opens them (SPR 260(f), 280(f)). Place stores orders on GF 219 or a Letter of Acceptance; services on GF 220 or a Letter of Acceptance (SPR 260(h), 280(h)).

## Urgent, overseas, revenue, negotiation, cancellation (SPR 265–298)

Urgent minor purchases of stores or services, in cash or by purchasing card, may be made up to **$5,000** if an officer not below ASO / EO II (or Assistant Engineer for works) certifies they are essential and the rates reasonable. Reimburse cash from imprest on GF 51; no covering order (SPR 265(a), 290(a)). An officer not below ASO / EO II may buy stores or services on an official visit outside Hong Kong up to **$25,000**, same reimbursement (SPR 265(b), 290(b)).

**Revenue (SPR 295).** Same quotation limit as SPR 220(a). Accept the **highest** conforming offer, or the conforming offer with the highest overall score. Bands and (for > $50,000) the non-works services procedures apply.

**Negotiation (SPR 296).** Conduct in a non-discriminatory manner if in the Government’s interest. Initiation may be approved by the SPR 260(g) / 280(g) accepter. **Acceptance of the negotiated outcome is one rank higher.** Guidelines: Appendix III(J).

**Cancellation (SPR 297).** The same 260(g) / 280(g) officers may cancel with recorded reasons (no conforming offer; recommended sum exceeds the quotation limit; public interest). No approval is required if **no** offer is received.

**Exception (SPR 298).** In very exceptional cases [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] may authorise a stores purchase outside this chapter. Requests that would depart from SPR 245–265 go **through DGL**, who forwards them with recommendations to PS(Tsy).
