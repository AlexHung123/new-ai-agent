# Chapter VI — Stores Records

Unless [[dgl|DGL]] excepts an item, every store is recorded in the ledgers, vouchers and registers this chapter names. A public officer who certifies a stores form is personally responsible for that certification (SPR 600). Accounting classes sit in [[07-accounting]]; how stock is received, issued and handed over sits in [[08-stores-management]].

## Recording duty (SPR 600–605)

A department that wants computerised stock records or stores vouchers must agree the package’s scope and detail with the Commissioner for Digital Policy, DGL, the Director of Accounting Services (DAS) and the Director of Audit (D of A), so the data are complete and the system meets Government Regulations (SPR 605).

## Ledgers (SPR 610–635)

Keep a complete receipt-and-issue record of every item in a departmental store unit, including livestock, in a manual or preferably computerised ledger (SPR 610).

**Who posts.** Someone other than the officer in direct control of the stores posts the ledger (SPR 615).

**How ledgers are kept** (SPR 620):

- Separate ledgers for **new (unused)**, **serviceable (used)** and **unserviceable** stores.
- Unique folio number per stock type; new items only through the control index.
- Post promptly; every entry cites its supporting voucher.
- Record the unit of issue (number, kilogram, metre, …) and take items on charge in that unit.
- No erasures. Manual corrections: red ink, initialled. Computerised amendments: supporting voucher plus a short note on that voucher.
- Loose-leaf sheets leave the current binder only after D of A has checked the transactions and the balances carried forward. File completed sheets in numerical order and update the index. Computerised ledgers need backup copies.

**Loss or damage.** Notify DGL, DAS and D of A and investigate at once (SPR 625). The [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officer]] appoints a board of survey for a full stock-take. After the CO endorses the board, the [[dsm|DSM]] (SPR 135) opens a new ledger if the stores’ value does not exceed **$250,000**. Above that, DGL’s prior approval is required (SPR 630).

**Destruction.** With D of A approval, DSM may destroy completed manual or computerised ledger sheets **seven years after the end of the financial year of the last entry**, without referring to DGL or DAS. DSM signs the destruction certificate and keeps it for audit (SPR 635).

## Vouchers and forms (SPR 650–675)

DGL is the authority for new and revised stores vouchers and forms. Departments may revise or invent forms only in consultation with DGL (SPR 650).

Use rules (SPR 660): file completed vouchers in numerical order with full particulars; quote the ledger or inventory reference on the receipt copy when the stores go on charge; correct by striking through and writing above, initialled by the signing officer; file cancelled copies (except the last, which stays in the pad) in sequence, marked “Cancelled” and initialled.

Notify DGL, D of A and DAS of any loss of, or damage to, used or unused stores vouchers and control-numbered stores forms (SPR 665).

**Destroying forms** (SPR 670–675):

- DSM may destroy general forms, non-control-numbered stores forms, and unused books of stores vouchers / control-numbered forms that are obsolete or surplus. Surplus destruction needs the authority of the CO who issues the forms. DSM signs the certificate for D of A.
- Used or partly used books: same method after **three years** and D of A audit.
- If a book still holds **original** used vouchers or forms, DAS approval is required unless the book has been kept **seven years** after the financial year of the last entry.
