# Chapter VIII — Management of Stores

The [[controlling-officer|Controlling Officer]] has general supervision of stores. [[dsm|DSM]] sees that the Government mark is used when buying, and that the mark is defaced when stores are sold for private use (SPR 800). Classification and ledgers are in [[07-accounting]]; surplus paths are in [[11-surplus-disposal]].

The conversion drops the article numbers on handover. The block after SPR 885 and before SPR 896 is treated as **SPR 890–895**.

## Storage (SPR 805–810)

Quantities of highly inflammable or explosive items above Dangerous Goods Ordinance exemption levels, and the adequacy of fire-fighting facilities, are referred to the Director of Fire Services. Corrosive, radioactive or other dangerous materials need special arrangements with the Government Chemist or the Senior Physicist in-charge, Radiation Health Unit, Department of Health (SPR 805). Draft Schedules of Accommodation must specify proper storage (including dangerous, perishable, valuable, attractive or delicate items) (SPR 810). Immediate disposal of goods already in a dangerous condition is [[12-dangerous-confiscated|SPR 1200]].

## Receipt (SPR 815–835)

DSM ensures supply matches the contract or order; the receiving officer reports sub-standard stores to DSM at once (SPR 815). The officer who signed the order voucher owns delivery location and timing, and must sign any amendment or cancellation agreed in writing with the supplier (SPR 820).

Inspect within a reasonable time. If extensive acceptance testing is required, pay only after it is satisfactory, unless the contract says otherwise. Book accepted stores immediately and take up any prompt-payment discount; pay under the Financial and Accounting Regulations (SPR 825).

Empty non-returnable drums of **200 litres or more** go on GF 270; keep a receipt-and-return record of containers returnable to or on loan from suppliers (SPR 830).

Reject local stores **in writing**. Copy [[dgl|DGL]] if the order or contract was GLD’s (SPR 835).

## Issues (SPR 845–870)

**GLD unallocated.** DGL may issue to departments for government use (the department vote is debited under SPR 705). Issues on payment to NGOs or individuals, of stock DGL treats as surplus to government need, are at **stock price + 20% overhead**. Only [[pstsy|PS(Tsy)]] may waive the overhead. If market price has risen significantly above stock price, DGL consults PS(Tsy) on the issue price (SPR 845).

**Departmental issues** for works or services, depot transfers, conversion, or sale follow [[appendices|Appendix VI]] (SPR 850).

**Loan, ≤ six months**, DSM approval (SPR 855):

- Own officers: Loan Register; retrieve at the end of the period or before the officer leaves.
- Other departments: same, plus the borrower raises GF 277 (issue) and GF 278 (return). Charge stays with the issuing department.
- Government-works contractors: issuing department raises GF 277/278 on the contractor’s behalf; the contractor gives a written agreement with financial safeguards.
- Private individuals or NGOs: only with the **personal** authority of the CO or deputy; same contractor paperwork.

**Tools** (SPR 860). Long-term (daily work): standard scale where possible; GF 272 or a per-employee card; physical check **at least once every six months**. Short-term (a specific job outside normal work): Loan Register; retrieve when the job ends. Return all loaned tools before leave of **seven or more consecutive days**.

**To another department** (SPR 865). CO or a designated directorate officer may approve **without limit** the issue of stores the department no longer needs. The receiver raises GF 277 citing the approving authority. Half-yearly return to PS(Tsy) (description and value of each case), within two months of period-end, copy DGL; nil return required.

**On payment** of surplus departmental stores to NGOs or individuals needs DGL approval. Pricing matches SPR 845: stock + 20% (PS(Tsy) may waive); consult PS(Tsy) if market price has moved significantly (SPR 870).

## Return (SPR 875–880)

Serviceable items originally issued from GLD unallocated stock may go back on GLD Unallocated Serviceable Stores Return Note (**GF 224**) if they are not obsolete or surplus to government, are in a condition DGL will re-issue, and are in complete units. DGL takes them on charge at current stock price. DAS then debits the Unallocated Stores Suspense Account and credits the returning department’s vote (same financial year) or General Revenue (a later year) (SPR 875).

Serviceable departmental stock is returned to departmental stock on GF 278, marked new (unused) or serviceable (used) (SPR 880).

## Custody, handover, security (SPR 885–896)

Officers are personally responsible for stores received in the course of duty. Failure to follow SPR or DGL custody instructions can lead to surcharge (SPR 885; see [[10-audit-writeoff]]).

**Handover (SPR 890–895).** Outgoing and incoming officers verify ledger against stock (DSM may allow a sample that includes valuable and highly attractive items if a full check is impracticable). Surpluses and deficiencies go on GF 241 and paragraph 1 of GF 237 to the CO via DSM; adjust under SPR 1030–1070 with GF 277/278. Both officers sign Handing Over Certificate **GF 239**. If the outgoing officer cannot hand over in person, the CO appoints a Handing Over Board under SPR 1015(a)–(c)(i) and the board signs GF 240. The outgoing officer may be held for reported deficiencies; **unreported shortages later discovered fall on the incoming officer**.

**Inspection (SPR 896).** DSM inspects each store unit under direct control **at least once every two years** (including security) and reports to the CO on GF 238 (and GF 241 if needed). Surprise stock and security checks at irregular intervals **at least once every three months**, unless DGL authorises otherwise. If inspecting every unit is impractical or not cost-effective, the unit head or deputy does the check. Record findings in Surprise Inspection Book (GF 235).
