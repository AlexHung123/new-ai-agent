# Compare — stores classes

Chapter VII splits **who holds** the stock from **how it is accounted**. Chapter XI then splits **serviceable** from **unserviceable** and sets the surplus tree. Detail: [[stores-classification]], [[07-accounting]], [[disposal]], [[11-surplus-disposal]], [[dgl]].

## Who holds the stock (SPR 700–710)

| | GLD unallocated stores | Departmental stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What they are** | Bought in bulk by [[dgl\|DGL]] and held pending sale or issue to departments and other bodies (SPR 705(a)). | Bought by a department for its own use — either drawn from GLD unallocated stock or bought direct (SPR 710(a)). |
| **Money** | First charged to the Unallocated Stores Suspense Account (Cap. 2 s.30), administered by DGL. On issue, DAS debits the department and credits the suspense account (SPR 705(b)). | Charged to the department’s own subheads. |
| **When surplus and still serviceable** | Return to GLD on GF 224 if not obsolete, acceptable to DGL for re-issue, and in complete units (SPR 875, 1130). | Follow SPR 1135–1151 (CCGO notice, then donate / sell / dump). |

## How it is accounted (SPR 710(b), 715–775)

| | Inventory | Non-inventory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Test** | Permanent or non-consumable **and** unit cost **≥ $1,000** at purchase (SPR 710(b)). | Everything else (SPR 710(b)). |
| **May a department upgrade it?** | — | Yes, for control; then keep treating it as inventory unless DGL agrees otherwise (SPR 775). The SPR 1110 tree treats SPR 775 items as inventory. |
| **Ledgers** | GF 270 (stock); GF 272 (entity distribution); GF 273 (rooms / quarters) (SPR 715–720). Special rules for fire gear, electrical/gas appliances, books, uniforms (SPR 735–760). | GF 291 while in a store unit. After issue, no further accounting except SPR 775 cases (SPR 765). |
| **Check** | Inventories at least once a year; discrepancies under SPR 1030 (SPR 715(b)). | Stock control through the ledger and Working Stores Tables (SPR 770). |

## Serviceable vs unserviceable (SPR 1115–1126)

The departmental disposal authority (DDA) — the Departmental Disposal Committee or a designated officer (SPR 1106) — decides serviceability (SPR 1115).

| | Unserviceable | Serviceable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meaning** | Worn out or broken and certified beyond economical repair by the supplier, a competent technician, or a technical department; **or** technically obsolete (SPR 1120). | Still usable. Trade-in items are **not** surplus (SPR 1100). |
| **Non-inventory** | DDA may dump (SPR 1120). | If GLD unallocated → return (SPR 875). Otherwise CCGO path (SPR 1135). |
| **Inventory** | If on a GLD disposal term contract → sell to that contractor. If on GLD’s published waiver list (low value) → dump (SPR 1125). Else, if it has residual value (display, training, preservation) → SPR 1135–1151; if not → dump (SPR 1126). | Same fork as the row above. |

## Surplus paths (SPR 1110–1151)

Value for the DDC ceiling is GLD stock price, original purchase cost, or replacement cost (SPR 1107). DDC may approve disposal at or below the stores quotation limit ($1.35m); the CO or a designated directorate officer approves above that, on DDC advice (SPR 1106).

| Order | Path | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Return to GLD** (GF 224) | Serviceable GLD unallocated items that DGL will take back (SPR 875, 1130). |
| 2 | **CCGO transfer** | DGL posts a notice ≥ 10 working days. First come, first served. Trading-fund transfers may be charged (SPR 1135–1136). |
| 3a | **Donate** | In parallel with sale, on merits (residual / resale value and public interest) (SPR 1137). NGO must be educational, scientific, cultural, or charitable; non-profit use; no Government capital or recurrent cost (SPR 1140). **> $300k:** [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]]. Off-conditions: [[finance-committee\|Finance Committee]] (SPR 1141–1142). |
| 3b | **Sell** | DGL arranges term-contractor sale, quotation, tender, or auction (SPR 1145). Other methods need PS(Tsy) via DGL. |
| 4 | **Dump** | Last resort: not wanted in Government, not fit to donate, and sale not cost-effective (SPR 1150). Off-tree dumping needs DGL (and PS(Tsy) if DGL is in doubt) (SPR 1151). |

Whatever the path, movement stays on charge under Chapter VII; dumped items use Appendix VII (SPR 1155).
