# Compare — approving authorities

Unless another officer is named, [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]] signs. Dollar figures are HKD. Board ceilings are from the SPR 116 diagram.

## Board acceptance limits (SPR 116)

| Board / committee | Remit | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[ctb\|Central Tender Board]] | All procurement types; advises PS(Tsy) above subsidiary limits (SPR 310(b), 390) | No $ limit |
| GLD Tender Board | Stores, non-works services, revenue | ≤ $60m |
| Public Works Tender Board | Works and related services | ≤ $200m |
| [[dtc\|Departmental Tender Committee]] | Stores, services, revenue — **except works** | ≤ $10m |
| [[ccsb\|CCSB / AACSB / EACSB]] | Consultant appointment (CCSB advises PS(Tsy); AACSB / EACSB approve) | No $ limit |
| DCSC | Departmental consultancies | ≤ $10m |

Quotation gates on the same diagram: works ≤ $7m; other procurement ≤ $1.35m; consultancy quotations ≤ $3m (SPR 220).

## Who signs

| Action | Who | SPR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Make SPR** | [[sfst\|FS / SFST]] under Cap. 2 s.11(1) | 100 |
| **Apply, interpret, except SPR** (and related FCs) | PS(Tsy). Ch II stores bought off-procedure: request via [[dgl\|DGL]] to PS(Tsy) | Intro. 4; 298 |
| **Invite single / restricted tenders** | CO **personally** — stores, services (not works or consultancy), revenue within the $10m departmental limit. DGL or delegates — GLD tenders. PS(Tsy) otherwise (stores above $10m via DGL). Directorate endorsement, any value | 325(b) |
| **Initiate direct engagement** | PS(Tsy). Delegated to CO **personally** only for stores, services (not works or consultancy), and revenue ≤ $10m. Directorate endorsement | 332 |
| **Accept a tender** | PS(Tsy) unless delegated. Subsidiary boards and [[dtc\|DTCs]] accept inside the SPR 116 ceilings. CTB **advises** PS(Tsy) above those ceilings | 310, 390 |
| **Accept works ≤ $30m** (not GPA) | [[controlling-officer\|CO]] personally | 310(h)(i) |
| **Accept works $30–52m** (not GPA) | CO personally, **only** if lowest conforming bidder or highest overall scorer | 310(h)(ii) |
| **Appoint a consultant** | PS(Tsy) is the default. CCSB advises PS(Tsy). AACSB / EACSB approve. CO / DCSC up to $10m (list, brief, marking scheme, pre-contract negotiation, appointment, cancellation) | 410, 425–426 |
| **Direct one-firm consultant** (no competition) | **PS(Tsy) only** — not delegated to COs at any value. Then a combined Stage 1+2 paper to CCSB (> $10m) or DCSC (≤ $10m) | 430(b) |
| **Accept a quotation** | Rank bands in SPR 260(g) / 280(g). Column (A) if ≥ 5 written quotes and lowest / highest-score offer; column (B) if fewer quotes or a non-default offer. **220(a) stores / general services / revenue / consultancy (20% and 50% bands):** ≤ 20% of the relevant quote limit — SO/EO I (A) / SSO/SEO (B); ≤ 50% — SSO/SEO / CSO/CEO. **Top band:** stores / general services / revenue up to $1.35m — CSO/CEO / D1; consultancy up to $3m — D1 / D2. **Works (220(b)) up to $7m:** Engineer / SE; SE / CE; CE / Government Engineer. ≤ $50k: accept at ASO/EO II (or AE for works) after more than one quote | 260, 280 |
| **Negotiate** | **Quotes:** officers in SPR 260(g)/280(g) may start; accept the outcome at least one rank higher (SPR 296). **Tenders:** PS(Tsy); delegated to subsidiary boards, DGL (GLD tenders), COs or uninvolved directorate officers (non-GPA works ≤ $52m), and DTCs within $10m (SPR 385(c)). COs / uninvolved directorate also for the SPR 385(d) cases (approved single tender; only one or few open tenders; price out of market; disadvantageous counter-proposals; tied conforming tenders). Negotiators at directorate level unless PS(Tsy) allows otherwise. **CCSB consultancies:** PS(Tsy) unless delegated (SPR 455) | 296, 385, 455 |
| **Vary a contract** | CO or a designated officer may approve variations that do **not** increase original value (rates no less favourable). Any increase: **Appendix V(B)** rank/board table — do not copy it here. Cumulative increases should not normally exceed 50% of original value. See [[05-contract-admin]] and [[contract-variation]] | 520, 525 |
| **Write-off** | **Fraud / suspected fraud / negligence** other than the theft exception: PS(Tsy) ≤ $500k; [[finance-committee\|Finance Committee]] > $500k. **Theft / suspected theft** with no other fraud/negligence path (SPR 1035(f)): D2/DL2 ≤ $10k; Head of Department / CO personally ≤ $50k. **No fraud or negligence:** Dept. Secretary at MPS 45+ (or CO-designated equivalent) ≤ $9k; D1/DL1 ≤ $36k; D2/DL2 ≤ $180k; HoD / CO personally ≤ $900k; PS(Tsy) > $900k | 1070 |
| **Donate surplus > $300k** | PS(Tsy), if SPR 1140 conditions are met (educational / scientific / cultural / charitable NGO; non-profit use; no capital or recurrent cost to Government). **Finance Committee** if those conditions are not met | 1140–1142 |
| **Cancel a tender** | Relevant tender board or DTC, with reasons and whether a re-tender will follow. CO personally for non-GPA works ≤ $30m. **No approval** if no tenders arrive, or if parallel-tendering FC funding is refused before validity expires. Consultants: relevant board; DCSC ≤ $10m (SPR 470) | 380, 470 |
