# Compare — procurement routes

Four ways to buy. Value and type pick the route (SPR 220–221). Open competition is the norm; [[direct-engagement]] is an exception (SPR 109, 315, 331). See [[quotation]], [[tendering]], [[consultants-selection]], and chapter hubs [[02-procurement]], [[03-tenders]], [[04-consultants]].

Limits are the total value of similar stores/services, or total revenue, in one purchase or contract. Do not split a requirement or shorten contract duration to stay under a gate (SPR 205).

| | Quotation (Ch II) | Tender (Ch III) | Consultants selection (Ch IV) | Direct engagement (SPR 331–333) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **When to use** | Value within the quotation limit. COs may still use tender or consultants procedures for more competition (SPR 220–221). | Beyond the quotation limit (except consultancy). Default competitive route for stores, services, works, and revenue (SPR 221, 300, 315). | When tendering is not apt: Government lacks the expertise; quality turns on the firm’s method and reputation; the qualities cannot be specified precisely; or price-only competition would cut quality (SPR 400). | Only when open, selective, restricted, or single tendering would not work, with very strong justifications (SPR 331). Not a first-choice route (SPR 109). |
| **Value gates** (SPR 220) | **Stores / general services / revenue:** ≤ $1.35m. **Consultancy:** ≤ $3m. **Works** (UN CPC Div. 51): ≤ $7m. | **Departmental limit:** $10m for stores, general services, consultancy, and revenue. **Works:** $52m for non-[[wto-gpa]] works under the simplified arrangement in FC 3/2009. Above those limits, GLD/board routes apply (SPR 235, 116). | Quotation ≤ $3m (Ch II). Departmental (DCSC) ≤ $10m. Above $10m: CCSB / AACSB / EACSB (SPR 220(a), 425–426). | Same subject limits as the delegated initiator: COs personally only for stores, services (not works or consultancy), and revenue ≤ $10m. Otherwise [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]] (SPR 332). |
| **Who invites** | Department officers by rank band (SPR 260(d), 280(d)). Normally more than one supplier ≤ $50k; at least five written quotes above $50k. Lists: GLD or a published departmental list (SPR 222). | The procuring department (or [[dgl\|GLD]] for stores beyond the departmental limit, SPR 235). Open tender is the default (SPR 315). Notices: Gazette and/or internet per SPR 340. | The department, after Stage 1 approval of the list, brief, and marking scheme (SPR 440–445). EOI then proposals, or direct invitation of proposals. | No bidding. Approach the named firm after initiation is approved (SPR 331–333). |
| **Who accepts** | Rank bands in SPR 260(g) / 280(g): lowest conforming offer, or highest overall score if a marking scheme is used. Higher band if fewer than five quotes or a non-lowest / non-highest-score offer. | [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]] unless delegated. [[dtc\|DTCs]] and subsidiary boards accept within SPR 116 limits. [[ctb\|CTB]] advises PS(Tsy) above those limits (SPR 310, 390). COs personally: non-GPA works ≤ $30m; and $30–52m if lowest conforming or highest overall scorer (SPR 310(h)). | PS(Tsy) is the default (SPR 410). [[ccsb\|CCSB]] advises PS(Tsy). AACSB / EACSB approve. DCSC up to $10m (SPR 425–426). | Initiation **and** acceptance: PS(Tsy); COs personally only in the SPR 332 carve-out. Directorate endorsement on every request. |
| **Marking scheme** | Optional (SPR 221A). Works and consultancy: a directorate officer before invitation. Stores / services / revenue: directorate approval if the scheme leaves the SMS Framework. | Non-works: SMS Framework (FC 2/2019); board/DTC approval for deviations (SPR 350). Works: relevant tender board unless a delegated or standard DEVB scheme. | Required board/DCSC approval (SPR 427). Technical weighting normally about 60–70%. | No competition. Include contracting and negotiation strategy, with a cost/revenue baseline (SPR 333). |
| **WTO GPA overlay** | Chapter II has no separate GPA code. If the estimated maximum total value over the whole duration is GPA-covered, Chapter III extras apply (SPR 300(b)). Do not split to dodge GPA (SPR 205). | Gazette notice; 40 days (25 days to prequalify); limited-tender grounds; three-year file retention; Review Body (SPR 340, 390, 530(d), 160(b)). | “Not bound by the WTO GPA” (SPR 455(e)). Still apply fair play and transparency. | If GPA-covered: GPA Arts. XIII / III and legal advice from D of J and/or LAD(W)/DEVB (SPR 331). |
| **Typical output** | Departmental Order for Supply of Stores (**GF 219**), Departmental Order for Provision of Services (**GF 220**), or a Letter of Acceptance (SPR 260(h), 280(h)). | Standard forms GF 230 / 231 / 232 or works Articles of Agreement, then a letter of acceptance or a signed contract (SPR 345, 390). | Consultancy agreement, cleared with D of J; signed at directorate level unless PS(Tsy) authorises otherwise (SPR 460). | A contract without a bidding process (SPR 331, 390). Keep a justification record (SPR 390(c)). |
