# Compare — tendering types

[[tendering|Open tendering]] is the norm (SPR 109, 315). The other modes need a named approving officer and a paper trail. Chapter hub: [[03-tenders]]. [[direct-engagement]] sits beside the four tender modes because it skips bidding altogether (SPR 331–333).

| | Open (SPR 316) | Selective (SPR 320) | Single / restricted (SPR 325) | Prequalified (SPR 330) | Direct engagement (SPR 331–333) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Who may bid** | Any interested contractor, supplier, or service provider. | Those on the approved list for that article or service. A firm that has applied to join the list is not shut out merely because there is no time to finish the application, except in complex cases (SPR 320(b)). | One firm (single) or a short list (restricted). | Only the CTB-endorsed prequalified list for that project or product. | No bidding. One named contractor, supplier, or service provider. |
| **Approving officer to initiate** | Default. No special initiation approval (SPR 315). | [[pstsy\|PS(Tsy)]] to **establish** the list (qualification criteria and assessment method). Works lists: Permanent Secretary for Development (Works) (SPR 320(c)–(d)). Updating a list to the approved criteria needs no further PS(Tsy) approval. | **COs personally** for stores, services (not works or consultancy), and revenue within the SPR 220(a) departmental limit (also if the department uses this mode inside the quotation limit). **[[dgl\|DGL]]** or delegates for GLD tenders. **PS(Tsy)** otherwise; stores above the departmental limit go via DGL (SPR 325(b)). Directorate endorsement, any value. Follow FC 2/2019. | PS(Tsy), on [[ctb\|CTB]] advice, for use of prequalified tendering **and** the evaluation criteria (SPR 330(b)). List of prequalified applicants then goes back to CTB (SPR 330(e)). | PS(Tsy). Delegated to **COs personally** only for stores, services (not works or consultancy), and revenue ≤ the SPR 220(a) departmental limit (SPR 332). Directorate endorsement. Follow FC 2/2019 (SPR 333). |
| **How invited** | Government Gazette (normally two consecutive issues); internet, press, or journals as well (SPR 340). For non-[[wto-gpa]] works ≤ $52m and other non-GPA procurements, internet alone suffices (SPR 340(d); FC 3/2009). | Gazette, internet, and/or letter to **all** firms on the relevant list. GPA: also notify those on the list (SPR 320(b), 340). Publish a standing internet notice inviting applications to the list (SPR 320(e); Appendix III(A)). | **Letter** to the invited firm(s) only (SPR 325(c)). Same submission manner as open or selective. | Prequalification: Gazette (and internet, press, journals as appropriate). Then **letter** to the prequalified tenderers (SPR 330(c), (f)). GPA Gazette rule: SPR 340(c). | No invitation. Approach the firm after initiation is approved. Include contracting and negotiation strategy (SPR 333). |
| **GPA limited-tender note** | Competitive mode GPA expects. GPA: Gazette; at least 40 days for tenders (SPR 340(c), (f)). | Competitive two-step mode. GPA qualification criteria limited to legal, financial, commercial, and technical ability; no “must have had a GPA contract” test (SPR 320(c)). | SPR 325(a) **includes** limited tendering under [[wto-gpa]]. Grounds must match GPA. Attach D of J or LAD(W)/DEVB advice on the GPA angle (SPR 325(d)(vii)). Record the GPA limited-tender condition (SPR 390(c)(iii)). | Competitive after a Gazette prequalification. GPA: Gazette; at least 25 days to apply for prequalification (SPR 340(c), (f)). | If GPA-covered: GPA Art. XIII (limited tendering) or Art. III, plus legal advice (SPR 331). Record the justification (SPR 390(c)). |
| **Secrecy** | Communications RESTRICTED (TENDER) until an award decision (SPR 305(a)). | Same. List-administration correspondence follows SPR 305. | Same, **and** do **not** tell tenderers that the exercise is single or restricted (SPR 325(c)). | Prequalification correspondence is also RESTRICTED (TENDER) (SPR 305(a)). | Keep a separate record of each direct engagement (SPR 390(c)(ii)). |
| **Typical justifications** | The norm: open and fair competition (SPR 109, 315). | Frequent need for the same article or service; not every firm in the market can supply it (SPR 320(b)). | Only when open competition would not work, and **not** to avoid competition or to favour local firms (SPR 325(a)). Catalogue: extreme urgency from unforeseeable events; work of art / patents / copyrights / exclusive rights with no reasonable substitute; failed or collusive previous open or selective tender (same essential requirements); additional deliveries that cannot change contractor; prototype or original development; utility companies; lease requires a named firm; [[national-security]] (SPR 325(a)(i)–(viii)). | Pre-testing of equipment; extremely complex, high-value, or rigidly programmed projects; high coordination or a non-standard form (e.g. BOT, specific design-and-build); products critical to the user (SPR 330(a)). List normally valid one year, for that project or product (SPR 330(h)). | Very strong case that **no** tender mode would work. Examples in SPR 331: national security, extreme urgency, patents / copyrights / other exclusive rights. |
