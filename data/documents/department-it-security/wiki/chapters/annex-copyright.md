# Appendix D — Copyright Ordinance and computer software

Added in **v1.2 (July 2008)**. DITSP s.19.5.1: “Compliance with the Copyright Ordinance and relevant guidelines on the use of computer software” attached at Appendix D **must** be observed. Concept page: [[copyright-compliance]]. Software usage: [[06-network-malware-patch]]. Catalogue: [[annexes]].

## Circulars (recirculate every six months)

Colleagues must observe the Copyright Ordinance and prevailing Government guidelines when installing software on office workstations, and familiarise themselves with:

- **Intellectual Property Department General Circular No. 3/2005** — salient provisions of the Copyright Ordinance and areas to note for compliance;
- **[[ogcio|OGCIO]] Circular No. 3/2008** — “Intellectual Property Rights Protection through Proper Management and Use of Software”: proper management and use of software, and rules/guidelines on IPR protection.

The two documents are **re-circulated to colleagues for reference every six months**.

## I. Software installation on office workstations

### What “unauthorised” means

Under the prevailing OGCIO circular, **unauthorised software** means:

- software which is **not licensed** for use; **or**
- software which **is** licensed for use but is **not authorised for use by the Bureau/Department**.

Installation of any unauthorised or illegal (pirated) software on office computer workstations is **strictly prohibited**.

### Freeware, shareware, and personally licensed copies

- Freeware/shareware of any type or nature obtained from the Internet — including screen savers, wallpaper, pictures and images — must **not** be downloaded to run on office computers without the **express permission of the relevant copyright owners** **and** the **approval of the Bureau**.
- Use of freeware/shareware risks virus attacks and information leakage.
- Detailed guidelines: “Guidelines for SAM implementation” in OGCIO Circular No. 3/2008.
- Where a division has a justified, genuine need, it makes a case to [[itmu-security-team|ITMU]], which considers the request **case by case** and, where appropriate, seeks OGCIO advice.
- Installation of any **personally licensed** software on any office workstation is **not allowed**.

### ITMU before procure or install

It is the **personal responsibility** of colleagues to ensure their workstations do not contain unlicensed or unauthorised software. Do not install software without the **technical advice and endorsement of ITMU**. As a rule, divisions should seek **prior advice of ITMU before procuring** any hardware or software for office use. Market software may not be fully compatible with internal technical/security settings of office workstations or the network. In all circumstances, prior technical consultation with ITMU is essential before installation.

**SM2** is the Bureau Intellectual Property Compliance Officer for intellectual property protection in connection with **software asset management** in CSB. Contact SM2 on any matter connected with procurement and installation of computer software.

## II. Photocopying of copyrighted works

If you need to photocopy copyrighted works, refer to the licence agreement between CSB and the **Hong Kong Reprographic Rights Licensing Society Limited (HKRRLS)**. The licence gives the right to photocopy books, journals and other publications **as covered in the agreement and in accordance with its conditions**. Terms of the licence are on the CSB Information Portal.

**SEO(Adm)3** is the Bureau Intellectual Property Compliance Officer for intellectual property protection in connection with **photocopying** of copyrighted works in CSB. Contact SEO(Adm)3 for those enquiries.
