# Security risk assessment

A **security risk assessment** is a process of evaluating security risks related to the use of information technology. It can be used as a baseline for showing how much has changed since the last assessment, and how much more change is required to meet the security requirement (DITSP s.4.1(g)). Policy: s.12. Guidelines: s.20. Hub: [[07-risk-audit]]. Method detail is in OGCIO **Security Risk Assessment & Audit Guidelines [G51]** v2.1 (cited, not in this file).

[[ditso|Division DITSO]] shall ensure assessments and audits are performed at least every two years and as necessary (s.5.3). The DITSO and [[itmu-security-team]] use that cycle for ongoing review of policy compliance and of safeguards (s.14.1.3).

## Every two years, and before a major change (s.12.1.1, s.20.1)

Security risk assessments for information systems and production applications **shall** be performed **once every two years**. An assessment **shall also** be performed **prior to major enhancements and changes** associated with those systems or applications (s.12.1.1) [M]. Examples of major change: major software/hardware upgrade, computer-room relocation (s.20.1).

Information Systems shall be evaluated once every two years by auditors of an **independent and trusted party** to determine the minimum set of controls required to reduce risk to an acceptable level (s.12.2.1). Compliance auditing of computer and network security policies shall be performed periodically (s.12.2.2).

In general the booklet **recommends** that security risk assessment **and** audit be conducted by an **external trusted third party** (s.20 intro).

## Who does which assessment (s.20.1)

| Cycle | Who performs it |
| --- | --- |
| **Bi-yearly** SRA | **External contractors.** It is the Division DITSO’s responsibility to ensure it is carried out. |
| **Pre-major-change** SRA | Deemed less complex than the bi-yearly exercise. Should be conducted by the **CSB ITMU Security Team** or the **IT Staff** of the information system. |

## Tools restricted (s.12.1.2, s.12.2.3)

Use of software and programs for security risk assessment analysis **shall** be restricted and controlled [A]. The same restriction applies to software and programs for security **audit** analysis. That is a policy **shall**, not a guideline preference.

## One-month follow-up; one-week audit (s.20.2)

A list of improvement actions is typically recommended in the SRA report. Those actions **should be performed within one month** after the assessment is completed, **unless [[dso|DSO]] has endorsed the exception with reasons**. A **security audit should then follow within one week** to ensure the safeguards are properly implemented.

| After bi-yearly SRA/audit | After system-specific SRA/audit |
| --- | --- |
| Division DITSO and CSB ITMU Security Team ensure follow-up is timely | Follow-up led by the [[information-system-owner|system owner]] |

Present results and recommended safeguards to DSO, IS Owners, and support IT Staff; seek DSO endorsement for policy/procedure revision and for bureau-level safeguards; seek IS Owner endorsement for system-specific safeguards; evaluate effectiveness of what was implemented.

Wireless and mobile usage should be in the periodic SRA (s.19.6.1.2, s.19.6.2). After a serious incident, SRA/audit of the affected system may be part of aftermath ([[incident-handling]]).
