# Information security

DITSP defines **information security** as the preservation of Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability (DITSP s.4.1(e)). Those three words are the CIA triad. They sit on an **Information System**: an electronic system that processes data through information technology, including computer systems, servers, workstations, terminals, storage media, communication devices, and network resources (s.4.1(a); SR 350(a) in [[annex-security-regulations]]).

Part II is the policy every CSB user shall observe (Part I s.1; Part II intro). How to read the verbs and the role tags: [[compare-shall-should-may]]. The eight control areas and the CSB organisation are in [[00-introduction]].

## CIA (DITSP s.4.1)

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Confidentiality** | Only authorised persons are allowed to know or gain access to information stored or processed by Information Systems, in any aspect (s.4.1(d)). |
| **Integrity** | Only authorised persons are allowed to make changes to that information, in any aspect (s.4.1(c)). |
| **Availability** | Information Systems should be available to users at any given or specified period of time (s.4.1(b)). |

A **security incident** is any event or vulnerability that could threaten CIA of a system or of information resources (s.4.1(h); [[incident-handling]]). A **security risk assessment** measures how far the system still is from the requirement (s.4.1(g); [[security-risk-assessment]]). **Classified information** is whatever Security Regulations Chapter III grades TOP SECRET, SECRET, CONFIDENTIAL, or RESTRICTED (s.4.1(f); [[classified-information]]).

[[information-system-owner|Information System Owners]] shall ensure CIA — and all other security aspects — of systems under their control, including outsourced ones (s.6.1.7). That residual duty is the outsourcing rule, not a waiver ([[outsourcing-security]]).

## Shall / should / may (s.4.2)

| Verb | Force |
| --- | --- |
| **Shall** | Mandatory requirement. |
| **Should** | Good practice; follow whenever possible. |
| **May** | Desirable requirement. |

Part II is written mostly in **shall**. Part III is how to deploy it ([[compare-policy-guidelines]]). Where the two parts disagree on the same control (password age; CIG versus departmental gateway), keep both texts and treat the detail pages as `contested` — do not pick a winner in this compilation.
