# Compare — roles

S17 requires a Departmental IT Security Officer. CSB splits that work across **three site-specific Division DITSOs**; when DITSP says “DITSO”, it means them (DITSP s.5.3 note). [[dso]] is the SR-appointed executive. Incident machinery is a different stack: [[isirt]] plus government-wide [[giro]]. Tagging of policy statements: [[compare-shall-should-may]]. Framework narrative: [[00-introduction]].

| | Who | Mandate | Typical approvals | Incident role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[[dso|DSO]]** | Departmental Security Officer appointed under Security Regulations; overall IT-security executive for CSB (s.5.1). | Direct and enforce security measures; provide resources; ensure participation at all levels; discharge all aspects of CSB security; advise on set-up and review of policy (s.5.1). Endorses DITSP (s.14.1.1). | Endorses the booklet; endorses urgent safeguards proposed by ITMU with Division DITSO (s.14.1.4); may endorse exception to the one-month SRA follow-up (s.20.2); endorses bureau-level safeguards after SRA; **must** approve TCD referral and any public statement (s.21.4). | Serious / public-aware: notified within **60 minutes** (after Division ISIRTC approval). Notifies senior management. Aftermath of serious incidents: ITMU leads under DSO escalation (s.21.5). |
| **[[ditso|Division DITSO]]** | One officer per site-specific division (Headquarters & Others, OLD, CSTDI) (s.5.3). “DITSO” in CSB IT-security documents **refers to them**. | Protection programme; lead policy/standards/guidelines/procedures; awareness; coordinate with other bureaux and CSB divisions; ensure SRA/audit at least every 2 years; initiate investigation and rectification (s.5.3). Leads booklet development with ITMU; seeks DSO endorsement (s.14.1.1). Dissemination channels (s.14.1.2). | **Day-to-day bottleneck:** shared/group user-IDs (s.8.4.1); password sharing (s.8.6.2); inter-departmental system connections (s.8.7.1); simultaneous LAN + external link (s.11.1.4); unauthorised devices (s.11.1.5); privately owned kit on the LAN (s.11.1.8); unauthorised software (s.11.5.2); IM (s.19.2.3); P2P (Appendix C); system-specific IT security policy (s.14.1.1); log profiling of a user (s.8.8.5). Approves shortening of the 3-month inactive-account period (s.8.5.1). | Not the ISIRT Commander by this booklet (that role is named separately). **Minor-incident aftermath:** collaborates with the IS Owner (s.21.5). Virus alerts go out centrally through DITSO or ITMU ([[malware]]). |
| **[[information-system-owner|IS Owner]]** | Collator and owner of information stored in databases and data files (s.5.4). | Determine security requirements, **classifications**, usage, and protection; lead system-specific policy and procedures (s.5.4, s.14.1.1). Residual CIA duty including outsourced systems (s.6.1.7; [[outsourcing-security]]). | Endorses **working-level procedures** for their own system (account, password, change/patch, backup, incident) (s.14.1.1). Endorses system-specific safeguards after SRA (s.20.2). Authorises access to their information (s.8.1.1). Sets DRP drill frequency ([[disaster-recovery]]). | Named as information owner on the 15-minute (serious) / 60-minute (other) clock (s.21.4). Writes the Post-Incident Report (s.21.5). Leads system-specific post-incident follow-up. |
| **IT Staff** | CSB technical staff operating Information Systems: security, network, and system administrators; application development and maintenance; CSB ITMU (s.5.5). Tagged **[A]**. | Implement and maintain safeguards in accordance with procedures set by Division DITSO and/or IS Owners (s.5.5). | Operational implementation, not policy endorsement. ITMU advises DSO/Division ISIRTs and, with DITSO, proposes urgent safeguards (s.5.2, s.14.1.4). Pre-major-change SRA may be done by ITMU or the system’s IT Staff (s.20.1). | **IT System Support Officer** starts the clock (15 min serious / 60 min other). Division **IRM** is the next step. ITMU is on the 60-minute serious list and advises DSO. ITMU leads aftermath of serious incidents and notifies other divisions’ IRMs (s.21.4–21.5). |
| **Users** | Staff who actually use information on Information Systems (s.5.6). Tagged **[U]**. | Know, understand, follow, and apply available security mechanisms to the maximum extent; prevent unauthorised access to their computers at best effort (s.5.6). Accountable for all activities under their user-IDs (s.8.4.2). | None of policy. They request access; they do not grant it. Shared passwords and shared IDs still need Division DITSO, not user self-approval. | Report malfunctions, alerts, warnings, suspected vulnerabilities, and suspected network problems **immediately only to CSB ISIRT** (s.13.2.3). Record symptoms for the Support Officer / IRM (s.21.3). Isolate and report suspected malware ([[malware]]). |
| **[[isirt|ISIRT]]** | CSB Information Security Incident Response Team: DSO + “Bureau Information,” (OCR fragment in s.21.2; title not reconstructed) + ITMU + three **Division ISIRTs** (each: ISIRTC, IRM, Support Officers) (s.21.2). Formation detail is in the ISMF, not this file. | Focal point for **all** information-security incidents in CSB (s.21.2). Immediate response on suspected intrusion (s.13.2.4). | Division ISIRTC (or DSO) approves disclosure to outside parties (s.21.3). Division ISIRTC approves the 60-minute notify-up of ITMU / DSO / GIRO. DSO approves TCD and public lines. | Owns the 15 / 30 / 60-minute (and every-30-minute) clocks for serious/public-aware incidents, and the 60-minute / every-2-hour clocks for other incidents ([[incident-handling]], [[08-incident]]). |
| **[[giro|GIRO]]** | Government Information Security Incident Response Officer — **government-wide**, not a CSB post (s.4.2). | Central **recording and coordination** of incidents that meet the GIRO criteria (s.21.4). | Not a CSB approver. CSB notifies GIRO; GIRO does not endorse DITSP. | Serious/public-aware: on the **60-minute** list, then every 30 minutes, and at close. Copy GIRO on every TCD referral. Notify GIRO if the incident hits life/safety, public-facing service, sensitive data (e.g. CMS), mission-critical systems, or highly undesirable impact (e.g. defacement). Other incidents: only after recovery, if Division ISIRTC so decides. |

[[government-security-officer]] and [[ogcio]] are not CSB line roles; they approve isolated LAN / CMS and issue S17 / G3 / G51 / G54. [[tcd]] investigates computer crime after DSO approval.
