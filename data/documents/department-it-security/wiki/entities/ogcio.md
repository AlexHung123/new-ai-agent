# Office of the Government Chief Information Officer

[[ogcio|OGCIO]] is the Office of the Government Chief Information Officer — the 2008 name used throughout this compilation. DITSP Part II is adapted from OGCIO’s Baseline IT Security Policy [S17]; Part III follows OGCIO guidelines. OGCIO was later absorbed into the Digital Policy Office; this wiki is the July 2008 snapshot and was not rewritten for that change. See [[00-introduction]] and [[ditsp]].

## Technical endorsement, CIG, and helpdesk

TOP SECRET / SECRET may be transmitted only under encryption **inside an isolated LAN** approved by the [[government-security-officer]] **subject to OGCIO technical endorsement** (DITSP s.11.1.10; Appendix A, SR 352). Email of classified information may go only on an Information System so approved — the Confidential Mail System (s.11.3.5; SR 365, 369). Physical-access rules stay with the Government Security Officer; logical access control and encryption methods must follow OGCIO (SR 350(d)).

Part II requires Internet access from the CSB departmental network through the Central Internet Gateway (CIG) (s.11.2.1). Part III also allows a departmental Internet gateway or CIG (s.19.2.1) — keep both; see [[06-network-internet-email]]. OGCIO anti-spam on the Central Internet System prefixes “[Possible SPAM]”. The Central Computer Centre Helpdesk (`ccc_hd@ogcio.gov.hk`) can assist investigation of suspected [[malware]] (s.19.4). Virus alerts issued by OGCIO are to be disseminated promptly. LAN/system administrators should also observe OGCIO security alerts when applying patches.
