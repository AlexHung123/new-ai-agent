# Technology Crime Division

[[tcd|TCD]] is the Technology Crime Division of the Hong Kong Police Force’s Commercial Crime Bureau (DITSP s.4.2). It is the police contact when the [[isirt|CSB ISIRT]] suspects a **computer crime**. DITSP does not describe TCD’s internal organisation; it only sets the reporting path. See [[incident-handling]] and [[08-incident]].

## When to contact TCD (DITSP s.21.4)

The Technology Crime Division should be contacted if the ISIRT suspects a computer crime has been committed. The gate is:

1. [[isirt|ISIRT]] suspects computer crime.
2. Seek **advice and endorsement from the [[dso|DSO]]** before reporting to TCD.
3. After DSO approval, the authorised ISIRT officer reports to TCD for case investigation (phone or email).
4. Notify [[giro]] as well, for central recording and coordination support.

On the serious / public-aware clock this is an “as when necessary” step, not the 15/30/60-minute chain. Ordinary “other incidents” are not sent to TCD unless crime is suspected and the DSO has endorsed.

## Aftermath and first investigation

Aftermath follow-up includes leading investigation and prosecution in case of computer crime (s.21.5). Appendix A, SR 382 says breaches on Information Systems should be investigated initially by the bureau or department following OGCIO incident-handling requirements; compromise of classified information also follows SR 333–334. Examples of Information System security breaches (unauthorised access to classified information, unattended keys or authentication devices, tampering in transit, loss of a portable computer or removable media containing classified information) are listed at DITSP s.21.2 and SR 383. Those examples are not themselves a decision that TCD must be called.
