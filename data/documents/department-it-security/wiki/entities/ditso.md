# Division IT Security Officer

Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] requires each bureau or department to appoint a Departmental IT Security Officer. CSB appoints a **Division DITSO** for each of three site-specific divisions to lead IT security at that site (DITSP s.5.3). “When DITSO is mentioned in CSB IT Security documents, it refers to the Division DITSOs” (s.5.3 note). See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-management]].

## Programme duties (DITSP s.5.3)

The Division DITSO:

- establishes and maintains an information protection programme for staff in the division;
- leads establishment, maintenance, and implementation of information security policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures;
- promotes IT security awareness in the division;
- coordinates with other bureaux/departments and CSB divisions on IT security;
- ensures [[security-risk-assessment|security risk assessments and audits]] at least every two years and as necessary;
- initiates investigations and rectification after a security breach.

The Division DITSO leads development of this booklet with technical help from the [[itmu-security-team|CSB ITMU Security Team]], consults [[information-system-owner|Information System Owners]] and IT support staff on the guidelines, and seeks [[dso|DSO]] endorsement (DITSP s.14.1.1). Dissemination is the Division DITSO’s job: file DITSP in the CSB Circulation Folder (every **six months**, with a signed acknowledgement), publish it on the CSB Intranet, issue virus alerts and circulars, and run awareness training (s.14.1.2). Virus alerts should be disseminated centrally through the Division DITSO or ITMU, not forwarded by individuals (s.19.4).

## Approvals

Explicit Division DITSO approval is required for:

- shared or group user-IDs (DITSP s.8.4.1);
- shared passwords (s.8.6.2);
- connecting a CSB Information System to one under another bureau/department — the security level of the connected system shall not be downgraded (s.8.7.1);
- privately owned computer resources on the Government internal network; the same IT security policy still applies (s.11.1.8);
- unauthorised application software on a Government Information System (s.11.5.2);
- Instant Messaging, restricted to business use (s.19.2.3);
- P2P / file-sharing technology, case by case after a risk assessment ([[annex-file-sharing|Appendix C]]).

Also: public release of internal network addresses (s.11.1.1); simultaneous LAN plus external/broadband connection (s.11.1.4); unauthorised devices on a Government Information System (s.11.1.5); log profiling of a particular user (s.8.8.5); shortening the usual three-month inactivity revocation (s.8.5.1).

The Division DITSO ensures the two-year SRA (by external contractors) and, with ITMU, timely follow-up (ss.14.1.3, 20.1–20.2). For **minor** incidents the Division DITSO works with the Information System Owner on aftermath; serious cases escalated to the DSO are led by ITMU (s.21.5).
