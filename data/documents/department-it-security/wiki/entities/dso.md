# Departmental Security Officer

Security Regulations require a [[dso|Departmental Security Officer]] (DSO) to be appointed for security duties in CSB. In DITSP the DSO is the overall IT security executive for the bureau (DITSP s.5.1). Part II tags management statements **[M]** for DSO, [[ditso|Division DITSO]], and [[information-system-owner|Information System Owners]] together. See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-management]].

## Executive duties (DITSP s.5.1)

The DSO:

- directs and enforces the development of security measures;
- provides the resources those measures need;
- ensures participation at all levels of management, administrative, technical, and operational staff, and supports them;
- discharges responsibility for all aspects of CSB security;
- advises on the set-up and review of security policy.

The DSO is responsible for establishment of the Departmental IT Security Policy and Guidelines. The Division DITSO leads drafting with [[itmu-security-team|CSB ITMU Security Team]] help, then seeks **DSO endorsement** so the booklet is enforced (DITSP s.14.1.1). Day-to-day approvals (shared IDs, privately owned kit, IM, P2P) sit with the Division DITSO, not the DSO. Urgent virus outbreaks and intruder attacks: ITMU and the Division DITSO propose safeguards and seek DSO endorsement for prompt action (s.14.1.4).

## Risk follow-up and incidents

Recommended actions from a [[security-risk-assessment]] should be done within one month unless the DSO has endorsed an exception with reasons; a security audit should follow within one week (DITSP s.20.2). Bureau-level safeguards and revisions of IT security policy also need DSO endorsement. The DSO sits on the CSB [[isirt|ISIRT]] (s.21.2).

On a serious or public-aware incident, the Division IRM notifies CSB ITMU, the DSO, and [[giro]] within **60 minutes** after Division ISIRTC approval (s.21.4). The DSO notifies senior management; CSB ITMU advises the DSO if needed. Subject to the DSO’s decision, an authorised ISIRT officer reports a suspected computer crime to [[tcd]] after DSO approval, and the Bureau Information Officer may disseminate to the public after DSO approval. For serious cases escalated to the DSO, the ITMU Security Team leads the aftermath and coordinates the three divisions (ss.14.1.3, 21.5). See [[incident-handling]].
