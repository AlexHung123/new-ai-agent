# Government Information Security Incident Response Office

To handle information security incidents occurring in B/Ds, an Information Security Incident Response Team ([[isirt|ISIRT]]) **shall** be established in each B/D. The Government Information Security Incident Response Office (GIRO) provides **central coordination and support** to the operation of individual ISIRTs. The GIRO Standing Office serves as the executive arm of GIRO (S17 s.5.1.3). See [[incident-handling]] and [[compare-roles]].

## Major functions (S17 s.5.1.3)

- maintain a **central inventory** and oversee the handling of all information security incidents in the Government;
- prepare periodic statistics reports on government information security incidents;
- act as a central office to co-ordinate the handling of **multiple-point** security attacks (simultaneous attacks on different government information systems); and
- enable experience sharing and information exchange related to information security incident handling among ISIRTs of different B/Ds.

Core members: [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]], [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]], Hong Kong Police Force.

[[govcert|GovCERT.HK]] collaborates with the GIRO Standing Office in co-ordinating information and cyber security incidents within the Government (S17 s.5.1.4). [[ditso|DITSO]] oversees dissemination of GIRO security alerts to responsible parties and project teams (S17 s.5.2.1). The [[isirt|ISIRT]] Commander collaborates with GIRO in reporting incidents for central recording and necessary follow-up (S17 s.5.2.4).

S17 does **not** print G3’s 60-minute phone / 48-hour form clocks. Reporting in S17 is **immediately** to the responsible party (s.18.1.5).
