# Information Owner

Information Owners **shall** be the collators and the owners of information stored in information systems (S17 s.5.3.2). Their primary responsibility is to **determine the data classifications, the authorised data usage, and the corresponding security requirements** for protection of the information. See [[classified-information]] and [[compare-roles]].

## Approvals in S17

| Subject | S17 |
| --- | --- |
| Access to information shall not be allowed unless authorised by the relevant information owners | s.11.1.2 |
| Application development and system support staff shall not access classified information in **production** systems unless the Owner approves | s.16.2.7 |
| Use of classified data from production as test data: process shall be reviewed, documented, and approved by the Owner | s.16.3.1 |

The Application Development & Maintenance Team **shall** liaise with the Information Owner in order to define and implement system security requirements during development and maintenance (S17 s.5.3.4). See [[12-development]] and [[security-by-design]].

System **tier** classification (Tier 1/2/3) is endorsed by the Head of B/D or an explicitly delegated directorate officer, not by the Information Owner (S17 s.7.2.2). That is a different fork: [[classified-protection]].
