# IT Security Working Group

The IT Security Working Group (ITSWG) serves as the **executive arm of the [[ismc|ISMC]]** in the promulgation and compliance monitoring of government IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines (S17 s.5.1.2). ITSWG was established in **May 2000**. Its responsibilities are to:

- co-ordinate activities aimed at providing guidance and assistance to B/Ds in the enforcement of IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines;
- **monitor the compliance with the Baseline IT Security Policy** at B/Ds;
- define and review the IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines; and
- promote IT security awareness within the Government.

Core members comprise representatives from [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]], [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]], Hong Kong Police Force (HKPF), and Chief Secretary for Administration’s Office (CSO). Representative(s) from other B/Ds will be co-opted on a need basis.

ITSWG monitors S17 compliance; incidents go to [[giro|GIRO]], not ITSWG. Compare [[compare-roles]].
