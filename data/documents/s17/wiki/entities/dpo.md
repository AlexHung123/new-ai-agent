# Digital Policy Office

The Digital Policy Office (DPO) issues the Government IT Security Policy and Guidelines — Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] and IT Security Guidelines [G3] — and the supporting practice guides (S17 s.2.3.2). This compilation is S17 **v8.2 (April 2025)**. Version 8.1 (July 2024) renamed the issuer from the Office of the Government Chief Information Officer (OGCIO) to DPO; do not treat OGCIO as the current issuer. See [[compare-documents]] and [[s17]].

DPO is a **core member** of [[ismc|ISMC]], [[itswg|ITSWG]], and [[giro|GIRO]] (S17 ss.5.1.1–5.1.3). DPO assists in reviewing and clarifying the documents B/Ds submit as required in S17 (s.5.1.1). Together with [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]], DPO provides designated training that [[ditso|DITSOs]] shall attend (s.5.2.1).

## Technical endorsement

The Government Security Officer (a Security Bureau post) approves the **isolated LAN** used to transmit information classified higher than CONFIDENTIAL, and the **information system** used for classified email; both are **subject to the technical endorsement of DPO** (S17 ss.15.2.1, 15.2.3). See [[classified-information]] and [[11-communications]].
