# Security Bureau

Security Regulations, authorised by Security Bureau, provide directives on what documents, material, and information need to be classified and to ensure that they are given an adequate level of protection in relation to the conduct of government business (S17 s.2.3.1). S17 defers the categories of [[classified-information]] to SR; it does not reprint the four grades. See [[compare-documents]].

Security Bureau is a **core member** of [[ismc|ISMC]], [[itswg|ITSWG]], and [[giro|GIRO]] (S17 ss.5.1.1–5.1.3). Together with [[dpo|DPO]], SB provides designated training that [[ditso|DITSOs]] shall attend (S17 s.5.2.1).

## Government Security Officer

S17 names the **Government Security Officer** (an SB post, not the departmental [[dso|DSO]]) as the approver, with DPO technical endorsement, of:

- the **isolated LAN** used to transmit information classified **higher than CONFIDENTIAL** (S17 s.15.2.1); and
- the **information system** on which classified **email** may be transmitted (S17 s.15.2.3).

See [[11-communications]] and [[compare-classification-controls]].

The departmental [[dso|DSO]] is the B/D’s executive for all aspects of security and may also be the DITSO (S17 s.5.2.3). Compare [[compare-roles]].
