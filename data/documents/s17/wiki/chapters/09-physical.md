# Physical and environmental security

S17 s.13: B/Ds **shall** prevent unauthorised physical access, damage, theft, or compromise of assets, and interruption to the office premises and information systems. Data-centre / computer-room definitions: S17 s.4.1(k–l).

## Secure areas (S17 s.13.1)

Careful site selection and accommodation planning of a purpose-built computer installation **shall** be conducted. Reference to the security specifications for construction of special installation or office as standard **should** be made (s.13.1.1).

Data centres and computer rooms **shall** have good physical security and strong protection from disaster and security threats, whether natural or caused by other reasons, in order to minimise the extent of loss and disruption (s.13.1.2). They **shall** comply with the relevant government requirements on physical security according to the **classification of the information system housed** (s.13.1.3). That is system classification ([[classified-protection]]), not only data grade.

A list of persons authorised to gain access to data centres, computer rooms, or other areas supporting critical activities, where computer equipment and data are located or stored, **shall** be kept up-to-date and be reviewed periodically (s.13.1.4). All access keys, cards, passwords, etc., for entry to any of the information systems and networks **shall** be physically secured or subject to well-defined and strictly enforced security procedures (s.13.1.5).

All visitors to data centres or computer rooms **shall** be monitored at all times by authorised staff. A visitor access record **shall** be kept and properly maintained for audit purposes (s.13.1.6).

All staff **shall** ensure the security of their offices. Offices that can be directly accessed from public areas and contain information systems or information assets **should** be locked up when not in use or after office hours (s.13.1.7).

## Equipment (S17 s.13.2)

All information systems **shall** be placed in a secure environment or attended by staff to prevent unauthorised access. Regular inspection of equipment and communication facilities **shall** be performed to ensure continuous availability and failure detection (s.13.2.1).

Staff in possession of mobile devices or removable media for business purposes **shall** safeguard the equipment in their possession and **shall not** leave the equipment unattended without proper security measures (s.13.2.2). IT equipment **shall not** be taken away from sites without proper control (s.13.2.3). Mobile/remote usage policy: [[07-access-control]] s.11.5.

If there has been no activity for a pre-defined period of time, to prevent illegal system-access attempts, re-authentication **shall** be activated, or the logon session and connection **shall** be terminated. Also, the user workstation **shall** be switched off, if appropriate, before leaving work for the day or before a prolonged period of inactivity (s.13.2.4).

The display screen of an information system on which [[classified-information]] can be viewed **shall** be carefully positioned so that unauthorised persons cannot readily view it (s.13.2.5).
