# IT security aspects of business continuity

S17 s.19: B/Ds **shall** ensure the availability of information systems and security considerations embedded in disaster recovery plans. Depth: [[disaster-recovery]]. Principle of resilience for crucial systems: [[02-principles]].

## IT security continuity (S17 s.19.1)

B/Ds **shall** plan, implement, and regularly review disaster recovery plans to ensure adequate security measures under such situations (s.19.1.1). S17 does not split BCP from DRP in this clause; it requires DR plans **with security measures**, planned, implemented, and regularly reviewed.

Off-site disconnected backups of business-essential / crucial information: S17 s.14.3.5 ([[10-operations]]). ISIRT Commander may trigger the departmental disaster-recovery procedure depending on impact (S17 s.5.2.4).

## Resilience (S17 s.19.2)

B/Ds **shall** ensure adequate resilience to meet the availability requirements of IT services and facilities (s.19.2.1). Core principle: all **crucial** information systems **shall** be resilient to stand against major disruptive events, with measures to detect disruption, minimise damage, and rapidly respond and recover; damage containment shall be considered (S17 s.6). “Crucial” aligns with [[classified-protection|Tier 2]] language (systems crucial to Government or society operations) but S17 s.19.2.1 is framed as availability of IT services and facilities generally.
