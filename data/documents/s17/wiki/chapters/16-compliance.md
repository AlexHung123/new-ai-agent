# Compliance — legal obligations, PDPO, SRA, audit

S17 s.20: B/Ds **shall** avoid breaches of legal, statutory, regulatory, or contractual obligations related to security requirements. Security measures **shall** be implemented and operated in accordance with the respective security requirements. Depth: [[security-risk-assessment]]. Personal-data floor: [[classified-information]].

## Legal and contractual (S17 s.20.1)

B/Ds **shall** identify and document all relevant statutory, regulatory, and contractual requirements applicable to the operations of each information system (s.20.1.1). B/Ds **shall** keep records to evidence compliance with security requirements and support audits of effective implementation of corresponding security measures (s.20.1.2). Same record-keeping duty: S17 s.6 (auditability).

B/Ds **shall** comply with relevant government requirements in relation to the security of information systems, including, but not limited to, storage, transmission, processing, and destruction of [[classified-information]]. Information **without** any security classification **should** also be protected from unintentional disclosure (s.20.1.3).

**Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) shall be observed** when handling personal data. All personal data **should** be classified as **RESTRICTED information or above**. Depending on the nature and sensitivity of the personal data concerned and the harm that could result from unauthorised or accidental access, processing, erasure, or other use of the personal data, a higher classification and appropriate security measures **may** be required (s.20.1.4). B/Ds also **shall** reserve the right to examine all information stored in or transmitted by government information systems in compliance with PDPO (S17 s.7.1.5).

## Security reviews (S17 s.20.2)

[[security-risk-assessment|Security risk assessments]] for information systems and production applications, as well as privacy impact assessments for information systems and production applications **involving personal data**, **shall** be performed **at least once every two years**. An SRA for information systems, as well as a PIA for information systems involving personal data, **shall** also be performed **before production rollout** and **prior to major enhancements and changes** associated with these systems or applications (s.20.2.1). v8.2 aligns this clause with General Circular No. 6/2024; do not reconstruct the circular.

Audit on information systems **shall** be performed **at least once every two years** to ensure the compliance of IT security policies and effective implementation of security measures. The selection of auditors and conduct of audits **shall** ensure objectivity and impartiality of the audit process. **Auditors shall not audit their own work** (s.20.2.2). See [[segregation-of-duties]]. [[ditso|DITSO]] oversees satisfactory completion of security-audit exercises (S17 s.5.2.1).

Use of software and programs for performing security risk assessment or security audit **shall** be restricted and controlled (s.20.2.3).
