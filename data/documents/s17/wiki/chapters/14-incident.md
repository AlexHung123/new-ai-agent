# Security incident management

S17 s.18: B/Ds **shall** ensure a consistent and effective approach to the management of information security incidents. Depth: [[incident-handling]]. Organisation: [[isirt]], [[giro]].

## Management of security incidents (S17 s.18.1)

B/Ds **shall** establish an incident detection and monitoring mechanism to detect, contain, and ultimately prevent security incidents (s.18.1.1). Threat monitoring: [[10-operations]] s.14.7. B/Ds **shall** ensure that system logs and other supporting information are retained for the proof and tracing of security incidents (s.18.1.2). Logging policy: S17 s.14.4.

B/Ds **shall** establish, document, **test**, and maintain a security incident response plan for their information systems (s.18.1.3). Staff **shall** be made aware of the security incident response plan that is in place and **shall** observe and follow it accordingly (s.18.1.4).

Any observed or suspected security incidents or security problems in information systems or services **shall** be reported **immediately** to the responsible party and handled according to the incident-handling procedure (s.18.1.5). S17 does **not** print a 60-minute GIRO phone clock or a 48-hour preliminary-form clock. Those implementation floors live in G3.

Each B/D **shall** establish an ISIRT; GIRO provides central coordination (S17 s.5.1.3). The Head of B/D **should** designate a senior-management [[isirt|ISIRT]] Commander (S17 s.5.2.4). [[ditso|DITSO]] initiates investigation and rectification on breach and coordinates required incident reports to the Director of Bureau (S17 s.5.2.1).

Staff **shall not** disclose information about the individuals, B/Ds, or specific systems that have suffered from damages caused by computer crimes and computer abuses, or the specific methods used to exploit certain system vulnerabilities, to any people other than those who are handling the incident and responsible for the security of such systems, or authorised investigators involving in the investigation of the crime or abuse (s.18.1.6).
