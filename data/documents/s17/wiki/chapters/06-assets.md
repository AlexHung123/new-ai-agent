# Asset management — inventory, classification, media

S17 s.10: B/Ds **shall** maintain appropriate protection of all hardware, software, and information assets and ensure all information systems and assets receive an appropriate level of protection. Classification grades: [[classified-information]]. Encryption: [[encryption]].

## Responsibility for assets (S17 s.10.1)

B/Ds **shall** ensure that an inventory of information systems, hardware assets, software assets, valid warranties, service agreements, and legal/contractual documents are properly owned, kept, and maintained (s.10.1.1).

Information about information systems **shall not** be disclosed where that information may compromise the security of those systems, except on a need-to-know basis and **only if authorised by the DITSO** (s.10.1.2). Staff **shall not** disclose to any unauthorised persons the nature and location of the information systems and the information-system controls that are in use, or the way in which they are implemented (s.10.1.3).

At the time that a member of the staff is transferred or ceases to provide services to the Government, the outgoing officer or staff of external parties **shall** handover and return computer resources and information to the Government (s.10.1.4). Exit of privileges: [[07-access-control]]. Surviving duties: [[05-human-resource]] s.9.1.9.

## Information classification (S17 s.10.2)

B/Ds **shall** comply with the government security requirements in relation to information classification, labelling, and handling (s.10.2.1). Categories are those of the Security Regulations (S17 s.4.1(i)); S17 does not reprint the four grades.

**All classified information shall be encrypted in storage irrespective of the storage media** (s.10.2.2). That is an S17 *shall* on **classified** information, not only on a named grade. Personal data **should** be classified RESTRICTED or above (S17 s.20.1.4). Control table: [[compare-classification-controls]].

## Storage media handling (S17 s.10.3)

B/Ds **shall** manage the use and transportation of storage media containing classified information (s.10.3.1). Storage media with classified information **shall** be protected against unauthorised access, misuse, or physical damage (s.10.3.2). All classified information **shall** be completely cleared or destroyed from storage media before disposal or re-use (s.10.3.3).

Removable-media definition: S17 s.4.1(o). Backup-media protection: [[10-operations]] s.14.3. Outsourced erasure on exit: [[13-outsourcing]] s.17.2.3.
