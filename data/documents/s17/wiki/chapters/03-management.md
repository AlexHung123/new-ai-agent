# Management responsibilities — organisation, SoD, classified protection

S17 s.7: Heads of B/Ds **shall** put in place effective security arrangements to ensure information systems and data assets of the Government are safeguarded, and IT services are delivered securely. Roles: [[01-organisation]]. Tiers: [[classified-protection]].

## General management (S17 s.7.1)

B/Ds **shall** define their departmental IT security organisational framework and the associated roles and responsibilities (s.7.1.1). They **shall** ensure that security protection is responsive and adaptive to changing environments and technology (s.7.1.2). They **shall** apply sufficient [[segregation-of-duties|segregation of duties]] to avoid the execution of all security functions of an information system by a single individual (s.7.1.3). They **shall** ensure that the provision for necessary security safeguards and resources is covered in their budgets (s.7.1.4).

B/Ds **shall** reserve the right to examine all information stored in or transmitted by government information systems in compliance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (s.7.1.5). Personal-data floor: [[16-compliance]].

## Security risk management (S17 s.7.2)

B/Ds **shall** adopt a **risk-based approach** to information security to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets and all other security aspects of information systems under their control, **including outsourced systems**, and monitor compliance with the security policies, guidelines, etc., by their staff and contractors (s.7.2.1). See [[security-risk-assessment]] and [[outsourcing-security]].

B/Ds **shall** adopt **classified protection of IT security** by assessing the classifications of **all** their information systems, including infrastructure facilities and departmental shared IT services, **regardless of the source of their funding**, and implementing **tiered** security controls according to the system classifications (s.7.2.2). The assessment details of system classification of all information systems **shall** be properly documented. The information-system classifications **shall** be endorsed by the Heads of B/Ds or their **explicitly delegated officer at directorate level**.

S17 defines the three tiers in s.4.1. It does **not** reprint G3’s assessment worksheet or extra *shalls* for Tier 2/3. Compact comparison: [[compare-tiers]].
