# Outsourcing security — providers, audit rights, cloud

S17 s.17: B/Ds **shall** ensure the protection of information systems and assets that are accessible by external service providers. Depth: [[outsourcing-security]]. Risk on outsourced systems: S17 s.7.2.1.

## IT security in outsourcing (S17 s.17.1)

External service providers **shall** observe and comply with B/Ds’ departmental IT security policy and other information security requirements issued by the Government (s.17.1.1).

B/Ds utilising external services or facilities **shall** identify and assess the risks to the government data and business operations. Security measures, service levels, and management requirements of external services or facilities commensurate with the data classification and business requirements **shall** be documented and implemented. Security responsibilities of external service providers **shall** be defined and agreed upon (s.17.1.2).

S17 does **not** print G3’s prohibition on unsupervised or remote day-to-day production access. Do not invent that floor here; it lives in G3.

## Service delivery management (S17 s.17.2)

B/Ds **shall** monitor and review with external service providers to ensure that operations by external service providers are documented and managed properly. Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements **shall** be properly managed and reviewed when changes occur that affect the security requirement (s.17.2.1).

B/Ds **shall** reserve **audit and compliance monitoring rights** to ensure external service providers have implemented sufficient controls on government information systems, facilities, and data. Alternatively, the external service providers **shall** provide security audit reports periodically to prove the measures put in place are satisfactory (s.17.2.2).

B/Ds **shall** ensure all government data in external services or facilities are **cleared or destroyed** according to government security requirements at the expiry or termination of the service or upon request of the Government (s.17.2.3). Media erasure: [[06-assets]] s.10.3.3.

## Cloud computing security (S17 s.17.3)

**Information classified as RESTRICTED or above shall not be stored in or processed by public cloud services** (s.17.3.1). Before signing an agreement with a cloud service provider, B/Ds **shall** ensure that the shared responsibilities of both parties are clearly defined, documented, and understood (s.17.3.2).
