# Source summary — Baseline IT Security Policy [S17]

Government-wide mandatory minimum. Title as printed: *Baseline IT Security Policy* [S17]. Issued by the **Digital Policy Office**. This compilation is **version 8.2 (April 2025)**. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_S17_EN_2090718517630623744.md` (~998 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

S17 is **not** a volume of the Government Regulations. It sits under Security Regulations (Volume 5). IT Security Guidelines [G3] elaborate it. B/Ds shall comply with S17; they shall also apply enhanced measures commensurate with determined risks (S17 ss.1, 2.1). See [[00-introduction]] and [[compare-documents]].

## Force

S17 “outlines the **mandatory minimum** security requirements for the protection of all HKSAR government information systems and data assets” (S17 s.1). B/Ds shall develop, document, implement, maintain, and review appropriate security measures by: establishing IT security policy, planning, and governance in line with this document (adopting all frameworks and requirements); implementing the measures detailed here; reviewing continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness; and improving those measures. The requirements are **technology-neutral** and focus on protecting information during processing, storage, and transmission. **Shall** is mandatory; **should** is best practice whenever possible; **may** is desirable (S17 s.4.2). Staff, vendors, contractors, and consultants who provide IT services to the Government are in the target audience (S17 s.2.2).

## Reading the conversion

- Repeated running headers and TOC dots are not new sections.
- Section 5 is printed as “GOVERNMENT ORGANISATION STRUCTURE ON INFORMATION SECURITY” without the number “5.”
- Section 9 is printed as “HUMAN RESOURCE SECURITY” without the number “9.”
- The Tier 1 definition wraps as “Tier 1 Information A related set… Systems” — reconstruct as “Tier 1 Information Systems”.
- Three figures are images: document-relationship diagram (s.2.3); government Information Security Management Framework (s.5.1); sample departmental organisation chart (s.5.2, caption footnote 1 missing). Roles are taken from surrounding captions, not invented boxes.
- Footnote 2 at s.15.1.9 (“mode of use”) is missing. Do not reconstruct it.
- Lotus Notes mail is still listed as a contact path in s.21 alongside CMMP.
