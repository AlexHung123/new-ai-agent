# Outsourcing security

B/Ds **shall** ensure the protection of information systems and assets that are accessible by external service providers (S17 s.17). The risk-based approach covers information systems under B/D control **including outsourced systems**, and monitors compliance by staff **and contractors** (S17 s.7.2.1). Hub: [[13-outsourcing]].

S17 does **not** print G3’s “no unsupervised / remote day-to-day production access” rule. Do not invent it here.

## Provider compliance and residual risk (S17 s.17.1)

External service providers **shall** observe and comply with B/Ds’ departmental IT security policy and other information security requirements issued by the Government (s.17.1.1). B/Ds utilising external services or facilities **shall** identify and assess the risks to government data and business operations. Security measures, service levels, and management requirements commensurate with data classification and business requirements **shall** be documented and implemented. Security responsibilities of external service providers **shall** be defined and agreed upon (s.17.1.2).

## Monitor, audit, destroy (S17 s.17.2)

B/Ds **shall** monitor and review with external service providers so operations are documented and managed properly. Confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements **shall** be properly managed and reviewed when changes affect the security requirement (s.17.2.1).

B/Ds **shall** reserve **audit and compliance monitoring rights**, or alternatively the providers **shall** provide security-audit reports periodically (s.17.2.2). All government data in external services or facilities **shall** be cleared or destroyed according to government security requirements at expiry or termination of the service, or upon request of the Government (s.17.2.3). Media erasure: [[06-assets]].

Handover of computer resources when external-party staff cease to provide services: S17 s.10.1.4.

## Public cloud (S17 s.17.3)

**Information classified as RESTRICTED or above shall not be stored in or processed by public cloud services** (s.17.3.1). Before signing an agreement with a cloud service provider, B/Ds **shall** ensure that the shared responsibilities of both parties are clearly defined, documented, and understood (s.17.3.2).
