# Classified protection of IT security

Classified protection means assessing **every** information system — including infrastructure facilities and departmental shared IT services, **regardless of funding** — as Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3, and implementing **tiered** security controls according to the system classifications (S17 s.7.2.2). A risk-based approach **shall** implement measures according to this classified protection to mitigate security risks to an acceptable level (S17 s.6). Compact comparison: [[compare-tiers]].

This is a **system** classification. Data classification under Security Regulations is a different fork ([[classified-information]]).

S17 does **not** reprint G3 Appendix B (assessment worksheet), Appendix C (extra *shalls* for Tier 2/3), or Appendix D (DPO lists). Those live in G3. Do not invent them here.

## Definitions (S17 s.4.1; reconstructed from the wrapped s.4.1(a) heading)

| Tier | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Tier 1** | A related set of hardware and software organised for the collection, processing, storage, communication, or disposition of information, regardless of the source of funding and project type. This is S17’s definition of an information system; every government information system is at least Tier 1. |
| **Tier 2** | Tier 1 systems which are **crucial** to the operations of the Government or society and whose failure or disruption will result in a **serious impact** on government operations or may cause **public turmoil and catastrophes**. |
| **Tier 3** | Tier 2 systems which are **directly related to the provision of an essential service** concerned and whose disruption or destruction may cause **serious harm** to the economy, people’s livelihood, public safety, etc. |
| **Essential services** | Services that are critical to the functioning and security of a society and its economy. |

Core principle: all **crucial** information systems shall be resilient (S17 s.6) — language that tracks Tier 2. Physical security of data centres and computer rooms shall comply with government requirements according to the classification of the **information system housed** (S17 s.13.1.3).

## Who endorses

The assessment details of system classification of **all** information systems **shall** be properly documented. The information-system classifications **shall** be endorsed by the **Heads of B/Ds or their explicitly delegated officer at directorate level** (S17 s.7.2.2). S17 does not time this assessment at “project initiation” in this clause.

[[information-owner|Information Owners]] determine **data** classifications, not system tiers (S17 s.5.3.2).
