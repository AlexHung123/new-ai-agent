# Incident handling

S17 requires a consistent and effective approach to managing information security incidents (S17 s.18). Prevent / detect / respond / recover is a core principle (S17 s.6). Hub: [[14-incident]]. Organisation: [[isirt]], [[giro]].

S17 does **not** print G3’s 60-minute GIRO phone, 48-hour preliminary form, one-week post-incident report, or GC 6/2024 two-day/seven-day Director-of-Bureau track. Reporting in this file is **immediately**. Do not import those clocks.

## Detect, retain, plan (S17 ss.18.1.1–18.1.4)

B/Ds **shall** establish an incident detection and monitoring mechanism to detect, contain, and ultimately prevent security incidents (s.18.1.1). They **shall** ensure that system logs and other supporting information are retained for the proof and tracing of security incidents (s.18.1.2). They **shall** establish, document, **test**, and maintain a security incident response plan for their information systems (s.18.1.3). Staff **shall** be made aware of the plan and **shall** observe and follow it (s.18.1.4). Threat monitoring: [[10-operations]] s.14.7.

## Report immediately (S17 s.18.1.5)

Any observed or suspected security incidents or security problems in information systems or services **shall** be reported **immediately** to the responsible party and handled according to the incident-handling procedure.

Each B/D **shall** establish an [[isirt|ISIRT]]; [[giro|GIRO]] provides central coordination and a government-wide inventory (S17 s.5.1.3). The Head of B/D **should** designate a senior-management ISIRT Commander (s.5.2.4). [[ditso|DITSO]] initiates investigation and rectification on breach and coordinates required incident reports to the Director of Bureau (s.5.2.1).

## Non-disclosure (S17 s.18.1.6)

Staff **shall not** disclose information about the individuals, B/Ds, or specific systems that have suffered damages caused by computer crimes and computer abuses, or the specific methods used to exploit certain system vulnerabilities, except to those handling the incident and responsible for the security of such systems, or authorised investigators.
