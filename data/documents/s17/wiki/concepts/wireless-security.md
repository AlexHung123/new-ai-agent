# Wireless security

Wireless communication without protection is S17’s worked example of why classified information in transit needs security measures (S17 s.6). Hub: [[11-communications]]. Encryption overlay: [[encryption]].

S17 does **not** print G3’s grade-by-grade wireless table, “WPA3 is not sufficient,” or Head of B/D approval for CONFIDENTIAL wireless. Do not invent those floors here.

## Controls on the internal network (S17 ss.15.1.7–15.1.8)

B/Ds **shall** **document, monitor, and control** wireless communications with connection to the government internal network (s.15.1.7). Proper **authentication and encryption** security controls **shall** be employed to protect data communication over wireless communications with connection to the government internal network (s.15.1.8).

## Simultaneous connections

Staff **shall not** connect workstations and mobile devices to an external network by means of a communication device, such as a dial-up modem, **wireless interface**, or broadband link, if the workstations or mobile devices are simultaneously connected to a government internal network, unless with the approval from the [[ditso|DITSO]] (S17 s.15.1.10). Unauthorised (including privately-owned) resources on the internal network also need DITSO approval (s.15.1.6).

Mobile and IoT usage: [[07-access-control]] ss.11.5–11.6. Classified over un-trusted networks: [[classified-information]] s.15.2.2.
