# Security risk assessment

A **risk-based approach shall** be adopted to identify, prioritise, and address the security risks of information systems in a consistent and effective manner (S17 s.6). B/Ds **shall** apply that approach to information assets and all other security aspects of information systems under their control, **including outsourced systems**, and monitor compliance by staff and contractors (S17 s.7.2.1). Hub: [[16-compliance]]. Tiers: [[classified-protection]].

## Cadence (S17 s.20.2.1)

Security risk assessments for information systems and production applications, as well as **privacy impact assessments** for information systems and production applications **involving personal data**, **shall** be performed **at least once every two years**.

An SRA for information systems, as well as a PIA for information systems involving personal data, **shall** also be performed:

- **before production rollout**; and
- **prior to major enhancements and changes** associated with these systems or applications.

v8.2 aligns this clause with General Circular No. 6/2024; cite the circular, do not reconstruct it. S17 does **not** print G3’s annual SRA for Tier 3, nor “self-checklist is not a substitute.”

## Audit (S17 s.20.2.2–20.2.3)

Audit on information systems **shall** be performed **at least once every two years** to ensure compliance of IT security policies and effective implementation of security measures. Selection of auditors and conduct of audits **shall** ensure objectivity and impartiality. **Auditors shall not audit their own work**. Use of software and programs for performing SRA or security audit **shall** be restricted and controlled. See [[segregation-of-duties]].

[[ditso|DITSO]] oversees the overall IT risk-management process, including satisfactory completion of information security risk assessments and privacy impact assessments as well as subsequent rectifications (S17 s.5.2.1), and oversees satisfactory completion of security-audit exercises.

Security planning for systems under development according to the systems’ security requirements **shall** be included (S17 s.16.1.1). See [[security-by-design]].
