## Entities

- [[ditso|DITSO]] — D3 (or highest directorate); departmental IT-security executive.
- [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]] — issues S17/G3; core member of ISMC, ITSWG, GIRO.
- [[dso|Departmental Security Officer]] — all aspects of B/D security; may also be DITSO.
- [[giro|GIRO]] — government-wide incident inventory; supports departmental ISIRTs.
- [[govcert|GovCERT.HK]] — alerts, HKCERT bridge (since Apr 2015).
- [[information-owner|Information Owner]] — data classification, authorised use, protection requirements.
- [[isirt|ISIRT]] — departmental incident team; Commander is senior management.
- [[ismc|ISMC]] — government-wide IT security oversight (since Apr 2000).
- [[itswg|ITSWG]] — executive arm of ISMC; monitors S17 compliance.
- [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]] — Security Regulations; co-trains DITSOs; GSO approvals.
