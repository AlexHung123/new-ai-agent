# Director of Administration

The [[director-of-administration]] **issues** the Records Management Manual to provide guidance and instructions for proper and coordinated management of government records (RMM 100). The same office **may update and issue amendments** from time to time (RMM 114). Comments by B/Ds on the Manual should be addressed to the [[grs-director]], not to the Director of Administration directly (RMM 114).

The Director of Administration is the **issuing authority**, not the operational records agency. Day-to-day central functions sit with [[grs]]. Departmental ownership sits with the [[head-of-bd]] and the [[drm]].
