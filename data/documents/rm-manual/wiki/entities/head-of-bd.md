# Head of bureau or department

Heads of B/Ds are reminded that it is their **duty and responsibility** to accord appropriate priority and resources to implement a proper records management programme throughout the organisation (RMM 104). Through officers designated with records-management and/or custodian responsibilities, the head should ensure the eight outcomes in RMM 209 — programme and policy; capture into a [[recordkeeping-system]]; create and retain as needed; content/structure/context; authenticity and usability; protection from unauthorised access, alteration, removal or destruction; protection of vital and historical records; trained records staff. Full list: [[01-policy-responsibilities]].

The [[drm]] is appointed **to assist** the head (RMM 210). Disposal schedules and disposal decisions are exercised by the DRM **as approved by** the head (RMM 604). Designation of [[vital-records]] is by the DRM with approval of the head or deputy head as appropriate (RMM 710).

On closed archival records, it is the responsibility of the **head of the transferring or responsible agency** to decide access status when the public applies, regardless of security classification (RMM 469). Climate standards for long-term and permanent storage that current facilities may not meet should be noted by heads and included in newly furnished or constructed facilities (RMM 523).
