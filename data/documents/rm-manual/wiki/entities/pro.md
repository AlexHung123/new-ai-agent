# Public Records Office

The [[pro]] is the **central archives of the Government** (RMM 318; Appendix B “Archives”). Records known to have permanent value should be created in a format that permits transfer to and preservation by PRO (RMM 318). Records appraised as possessing archival value by the **Archivist of the PRO** should be transferred to and preserved by PRO of [[grs]] (RMM 626).

## Access

Under the Public Records (Access) Rules, PRO has a general responsibility for granting public access to archival records of **not less than 30 years old**, or those whose contents have been published or wholly disclosed (RMM 465). Unclassified archival records reaching 30 years, held and accessioned by PRO, are released automatically; classified ones are reviewed by the creating B/D (RMM 466–468). The [[grs-director]] may permit inspection of closed archives or withhold for preservation (RMM 470–471). See [[records-access]].

## Transfer conditions (RMM 636–639)

B/Ds systematically transfer archival records to PRO under schedules approved by the GRS Director. All government records reaching 30 years should be appraised by PRO. Include supporting documentation or indexes. Acceptance requires: Archivist confirmation of sufficient value; transfer as an appropriate means of preservation and future access; preparation as PRO requires.

## Other PRO functions in this Manual

- Advice on newly furnished storage that should meet the climate bands in RMM 522 (RMM 523).
- **Central Preservation Library for Government Publications**, an integral part of PRO: contact before disposing of library collections or printed materials; send a copy of newly published reports, books, posters or other printed material unless otherwise specified (General Circular 1/2001) (RMM 631).
- Consult PRO on preservation, administration and disposal of long-term (30 years or over) or permanent records still in B/D control (RMM 632).
- Archivist appraisal when functions are deleted (RMM 807).
