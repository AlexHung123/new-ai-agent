# Government Records Service Director

The [[grs-director]] is the named officer who holds the Manual’s **hard bars** and several discretions. Comments on amendments to the Manual are addressed here (RMM 114). New B/Ds (and any change of [[drm]]) report the DRM’s name, title and contact number here (RMM 213).

## Destruction and schedules

- **No government records are to be destroyed** without the GRS Director’s prior agreement (RMM 605).
- Unauthorised removal, defacing, alteration, destruction or loss is reported here **immediately** (RMM 606).
- Disposal authority for [[programme-records]] schedules is obtained from the GRS Director through the [[rmo]] (RMM 615). All changes to schedules are agreed with the GRS Director (RMM 616).

## Access to closed archives

In discretion, and in accordance with general instructions of the Chief Secretary for Administration, the GRS Director may permit any persons to inspect **closed archival records** held by [[pro]] (RMM 470). The GRS Director may also **withhold** access to archival records to ensure safe custody and proper preservation (RMM 471).

## Transfer outside Government

No records go into legislation, a contract of sale, or an agreement on privatisation or transfer of a function outside Government unless the GRS Director has issued authority to transfer custody or ownership (RMM 822). Transfer agreements are endorsed here (RMM 823). See [[custody-ownership]].

## Monitoring

May conduct records management studies and give instructions and advice (RMM 906). Coordinates service-wide surveys with heads of B/Ds (RMM 907). See [[records-review]].
