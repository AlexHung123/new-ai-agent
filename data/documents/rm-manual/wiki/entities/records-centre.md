# Records Centre

The [[records-centre]] is the low-cost off-site facility, run by the [[rmo]] of [[grs]], for housing and servicing **inactive records** whose reference rate does not warrant expensive office space (Appendix B; RMM 629). B/Ds should use it as far as practicable. Procedures: Records Management Publication No. 1.

Destruction of inactive records stored there is **not** arranged unless the transferring B/D consents in writing. If the B/D fails to respond to a request for confirmation of destruction within **3 months** after the records are due for disposal, RMO **reserves the right to destroy** them (RMM 630). That silence rule does not waive the GRS Director destruction bar in RMM 605 for records still under B/D control; it is a Records Centre operating rule for holdings already transferred there.

One of the [[drm]]’s printed duties is to use the Records Centre as far as practicable ([[annex-drm-duties]] item f).
