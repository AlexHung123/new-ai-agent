# Government Records Service

[[grs]] is the **central records management and service agency** of the Government (RMM 201, 220). Responsibilities for proper management of government records are **shared** between B/Ds that create and collect records and GRS (RMM 201).

## Main responsibilities (RMM 220)

- Development and implementation of government-wide records management policies.
- Development of records standards, guidelines and procedures.
- Records-related advisory services and training.
- Administration of [[records-centre|records centres]], records disposal and centralised microfilming.
- Survey, inspection, study and evaluation of departmental programmes.
- Selection, administration and preservation of government archives and valuable publications.
- Access and reference services to government archives and selected publications.
- **Authority** in advising and instructing on administration of records, archives and publications of **long-term (30 years or over) or permanent** value.

GRS authorises disposal by approving requests and schedules and issuing disposal authority or agreement (RMM 602). Units named in this Manual: [[grs-director]], [[pro]] (including the Central Preservation Library), [[rmo]] (including the Records Centre).

B/Ds and GRS should work closely so records are effectively managed, operations are accountable, and records of long-term and permanent value that form part of the community’s cultural heritage are protected (RMM 221). Publications: [[annex-publications]].
