# Records Management Office

The [[rmo]] is the unit of [[grs]] through which B/Ds obtain **disposal authority** for [[programme-records]] schedules from the [[grs-director]] (RMM 615). Requests to **amend** schedules should be lodged with RMO in the first instance (RMM 616).

RMO operates the [[records-centre]] facilities B/Ds should use as far as practicable for inactive records (RMM 629). Destruction of Records Centre holdings needs the transferring B/D’s written consent; if the B/D does not respond within **3 months** after the records are due for disposal, RMO reserves the right to destroy them (RMM 630).

On **deletion of functions**, RMO receives amended disposal authority or a complete list of records to be disposed of, and arranges temporary storage, appraisal by the Archivist of [[pro]], or authorisation of destruction as appropriate (RMM 807).

Appendix B defines a records centre as a low-cost centralised area for housing inactive records; “The Records Management Office of the Government Records Service provides centralized records centre services.”
