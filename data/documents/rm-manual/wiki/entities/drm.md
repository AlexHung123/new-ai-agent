# Departmental Records Manager

The [[drm]] is appointed to assist the [[head-of-bd]] to establish and implement a proper departmental records management programme (RMM 210). That programme provides efficient handling of government records by controlling creation, organisation, maintenance and disposal (RMM 211).

**Expected rank:** Departmental Secretary or an officer holding equivalent position (RMM 212). The DRM oversees the programme according to government policy, encourages cooperation in daily handling and use of records, and coordinates with [[grs]] and other B/Ds. Eleven major duties: [[annex-drm-duties]].

Newly established B/Ds should forward the DRM’s name, title and contact number to the [[grs-director]]. Any change or replacement should be reported as soon as possible (RMM 213).

## Named DRM tasks in the body

| Task | RMM |
| --- | --- |
| Review classified documents at least every 5 years to downgrade | 443 |
| Develop disposal schedules and decide disposal action, as approved by the head | 604 |
| Designate [[vital-records]], highly selectively, with head or deputy-head approval | 710 |
| On terminated or defunct operations, arrange transfer/disposal with GRS prior approval | 803 |
| Internal evaluation of the records function every 5 years or more often | 915 |

The DRM is **not** the destruction authority. Destruction still needs the GRS Director (RMM 605). The DRM is not a substitute for the head’s ownership of priority and resources (RMM 104, 209).
