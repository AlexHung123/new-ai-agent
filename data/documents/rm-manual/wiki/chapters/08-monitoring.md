# Monitoring records management

RMM 900–917: GRS may review, study and survey B/D programmes; each [[drm]] should evaluate the local programme at least every five years. Concept: [[records-review]]. Yardsticks: this Manual and Publication No. 7.

## GRS records review (RMM 900–902)

Where circumstances warrant, [[grs]] will review the records management function of B/Ds to ensure compliance with policies, procedures, directives and disposal authorities (RMM 900). Selection is based on perceived needs by GRS or specific requests by B/Ds (RMM 901). GRS will notify the selected B/D **in writing at least 3 months in advance** (RMM 902).

## Study and survey (RMM 906–908)

The [[grs-director]] may conduct records management studies and give instructions and advice to ensure accurate and complete documentation, safe retention, and efficient and cost-effective management (RMM 906). The GRS Director will from time to time coordinate with heads of B/Ds in conducting **service-wide surveys** of records and practices (RMM 907). B/Ds should implement recommendations as far as practicable to promote economy and efficiency in staff, space, technology, systems, equipment and supplies (RMM 908).

## Internal evaluation (RMM 915–917)

In addition to GRS work, departmental management should examine the function periodically. It is recommended that the DRM, as far as possible, conduct an evaluation **every 5 years or more often** (RMM 915). Use the provisions of this Manual as yardsticks (RMM 916). Publication No. 7 — *Checklist for Proper Records Management Practices* provides a framework and essential tips and should be adopted whenever applicable (RMM 917).
