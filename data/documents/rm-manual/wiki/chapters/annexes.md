# Appendices — catalogue

Three appendices. The conversion prints them without the letters A/B/C; the TOC identifies them. The closing **INDEX** is a subject-to-paragraph table, not a fourth appendix.

| Appendix | Title | Wiki |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | List of Records Management Publications by the Government Records Service | [[annex-publications]] |
| B | Records Management Glossary | [[annex-glossary]] |
| C | Major Duties of the Departmental Records Managers | [[annex-drm-duties]] |

The publications are **named, not transcribed**. Use the current GRS editions for procedure. Glossary definitions that have their own pages are filed as concepts; the catalogue keeps one-liners for the rest.
