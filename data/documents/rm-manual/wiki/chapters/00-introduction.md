## Validity and objective (RMM 100–104)

Issued by the [[director-of-administration]] to provide guidance and instructions for proper and coordinated management of government records (RMM 100). Purpose: a comprehensive programme that documents policies and transactions, controls quality and quantity, streamlines the records lifecycle, identifies records of value, and maintains supporting systems (RMM 101).

B/Ds should make records management an integral part of business routine and manage records as a **vital resource** for decision-making, operations, and legal/financial/public interests (RMM 102). They should follow the Manual **as far as possible** (RMM 103). Heads of B/Ds are reminded it is their duty to accord appropriate **priority and resources** (RMM 104). See [[head-of-bd]].

## Scope and key provisions (RMM 105–108)

“Records management” is the planning, directing, organising, controlling, reviewing, training and other managerial activities covering creation through disposal, to document government policies, decisions and transactions and to run B/Ds efficiently (RMM 105). Unless specified otherwise, the Manual applies to **all government records** in official business (RMM 106).

Key provisions (RMM 108): make and keep full and accurate records; protect records in custody; establish a programme to government policy and best practice; monitor and report; manage records to meet statutory and regulatory requirements.

RMM 107: with growth of computer technology, the Government “is in the process of formulating a new strategy” for electronic information systems and records; detailed e-mail and electronic-records instructions “will be issued in due course”. Treat as a **2001/2020 snapshot** — see [[electronic-records]] and [[synthesis]].

## Distribution, publications, amendments (RMM 111–114)

Intended for all officers, particularly those with records management or custodian responsibilities (RMM 111). Use **in conjunction with** the GRS Records Management Publications, which give explanations, procedures, and options to suit individual B/Ds (RMM 112–113). List: [[annex-publications]]. The Director of Administration may update the Manual; comments to the [[grs-director]] (RMM 114).

## Definitions (RMM 115–121)

A [[government-records|government record]] is any recorded information or data in any physical format or media created or received by a B/D during official business and kept as **evidence** of policies, decisions, procedures, functions, activities and transactions (RMM 115). To be evidence it must have **content, structure and context** and sit in a [[recordkeeping-system]] (RMM 116–117).

- **Recordkeeping** — making and maintaining complete, accurate and reliable evidence (RMM 118).
- **Recordkeeping system** — manual or automated system that collects, organises and categorises records (RMM 119).
- **Recordkeeping requirements** — what a B/D must keep to protect legal, financial and public rights (RMM 120).

Other definitions: [[annex-glossary]], with ISO as normative reference (RMM 121).

## Regulatory framework (RMM 122)

Apart from this Manual, B/Ds need to identify requirements that affect records work. The printed list includes: Copyright Ordinance; Electronic Transactions Ordinance; Evidence Ordinance; Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; Code of Practice on Human Resources Management; Code on Access to Information; Civil Service Regulations; Financial and Accounting Regulations; Standing Accounting Instructions; General Regulations; Stores and Procurement Regulations; Security Regulations; CSB Circulars; Financial Circulars; General Circulars; ITSD Circulars; Public Records (Access) Rules.

## Electronic records (RMM 123)

Records created, collected or generated electronically as evidence of official business **are government records** and should be managed like other official records. For management detail, see RMM 107.
