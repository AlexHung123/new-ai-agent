# Managing records for administrative changes

RMM 800–820: when a function is created, deleted, or moved **inside** Government, records must move with it — or be disposed of under Chapter 6 — without losing context. Transfers **outside** Government: [[07-outsourcing-transfer]]. Concept: [[administrative-changes]].

## What changes (RMM 800–803)

An administrative change is a change in the administration of government functions in a B/D. It may involve (RMM 800):

- establishment or deletion of a function within the organisation;
- transfer of a function from one unit to another within the organisation;
- transfer of a function to another government B/D;
- outsourcing of a function to a contractor;
- sale or transfer of a function to a private organisation; or
- corporatisation of functions into a government business enterprise.

Proper transfer and/or disposal is needed so the inheriting agency can operate and the function-losing agency is not left maintaining unneeded records (RMM 801). Records not transferred and not required for further use should be disposed of as other government records (RMM 802). It is the duty of the [[drm]] in the B/D that **terminates an operation or holds records of a defunct operation** to arrange transfer and/or other disposal **with the prior approval of [[grs]]** (RMM 803).

## Creation and deletion (RMM 806–807)

New functions: prepare a new or expanded [[recordkeeping-system]] — at least a new or expanded classification scheme, related index, updated records inventory, disposal schedules, and a list of vital records (RMM 806).

Deleted functions: schedule the related records for disposal. This may mean amending existing disposal authority with the [[rmo]], or sending a complete list of records to be disposed of to RMO. RMO will arrange temporary storage, appraisal by the Archivist of [[pro]], or authorisation of destruction as appropriate (RMM 807).

## Transfer within Government (RMM 810–815)

When functions move to another unit or B/D, the function-losing agency **relinquishes control** and transfers related records to the agency to which the functions devolve (RMM 810). Transfer **only** those records relating to the transferred functions that will help continued operation (RMM 811). If the records are in an automated system, agree formal arrangements on access, security, compatibility and servicing contracts (RMM 812).

**Function-losing agency** should (RMM 813):

- consult action officers to identify records the inheritor needs;
- inform GRS of the function and records transfer, and arrange disposal with GRS of records needed by neither side;
- pass the transferred records **and their control records (including the records disposal authority) in their entirety**; and
- keep a list of records handed over, or at least record types and quantity.

**Function-inheriting agency** should (RMM 814):

- contact the losing agency to discuss the arrangement;
- check completeness against the lists and ensure retrieval via the control records;
- decide the recordkeeping arrangement for the new functions, especially if automated;
- **retain the original reference** of the transferred records and raise **new** records for continued action — transferred records should **not be altered or top-numbered** into current or new series, which would change original context; and
- ensure documentation of charges, contracts and responsibilities is also received.

Where both agencies need the same categories, decide whether special access arrangements or **duplication** of the relevant portion is required (RMM 815).
