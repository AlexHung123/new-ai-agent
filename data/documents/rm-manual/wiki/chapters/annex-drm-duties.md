# Appendix C — major duties of the Departmental Records Manager

Appointment and expected rank: [[drm]] (RMM 210–213). These eleven duties are the printed list. They do not replace the head of B/D’s ownership of the programme ([[head-of-bd]], RMM 209).

The DRM should:

1. Ensure that the design of the [[recordkeeping-system]], the choice of equipment, and the use of resources and space are as efficient and cost-effective as possible.
2. Establish, implement and document departmental records management practices and procedures according to [[grs]] guidelines and instructions.
3. Provide records management staff with appropriate expertise, training and guidance.
4. Appoint Assistant Departmental Records Manager(s) (ADRM), where necessary, to monitor records management routines. *(ADRM has no separate page — passing mention.)*
5. Introduce technology in a cost-beneficial manner to any aspect of the records function that improves operation and helps achieve government records management policy.
6. Use the [[records-centre]] run by the [[rmo]] as far as practicable to store, service and dispose of inactive records.
7. Facilitate records appraisal and records access review conducted by the [[pro]], and ensure prompt and systematic transfer of [[archival-records]] to PRO for preservation.
8. Identify and protect [[vital-records]] so they are available in any emergency or disaster.
9. Foster cooperation between records management personnel and other staff.
10. Cooperate with GRS in evaluating and improving the effectiveness of the programme on a regular basis ([[records-review]]).
11. Consult GRS when records management advice is required.
