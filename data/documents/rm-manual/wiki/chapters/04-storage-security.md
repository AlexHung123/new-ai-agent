# Records storage and security

RMM 500–523: store records so they remain usable for as long as they are needed, and keep them from unauthorised access, alienation, water, fire and climate damage. Classified overlay: Security Regulations ([[security-classification]]). Long-term / permanent climate: [[archival-records]], [[pro]].

## General rules (RMM 500–504)

Store information of continuing relevance in a medium appropriate to the information, user access, and the time needed for business, accountability, legal, policy and archival requirements (RMM 500). Offer proper storage and protection to records needed for decision-making, programme operations, service delivery, and regulatory/policy requirements (RMM 501).

Protect records from unauthorised access, use, disclosure, removal, **alienation** (transfer outside Government control), deterioration, loss or destruction (RMM 502). Store and protect in ways that reflect security/sensitivity classification, importance and retention (RMM 503). Classified records: also follow the Security Regulations (RMM 504).

## Housekeeping (RMM 510–521)

- Protect from dirt, insects and rodents, smoke, chemical exhausts; away from direct sunlight (RMM 510).
- Keep storage clean; avoid food, drinks and plants (RMM 511).
- Avoid underground level, under water pipes, or near unblocked windows (water damage) (RMM 512).
- Designated records storage areas should be used for **records only** (RMM 513).
- Smoking strictly prohibited in storage and adjacent area (RMM 514).
- No chemicals or inflammable/combustible materials in or near storage (RMM 515).
- Effective fire detection and suppression system **must** be installed (RMM 516).
- Do not place records on top of equipment so as to obstruct sprinklers (RMM 517).
- Do not stack on the floor; keep in equipment preferably **at least 7 to 10 cm off the floor** (RMM 518).
- Shelves, cabinets or racks appropriate to format and size; clear passageway (RMM 519).
- Observe **floor loading**; densely stored records, especially on mobile shelving, are heavy (RMM 520).
- Staff must know existence and current locations of records used and stored in various places (RMM 521).

## Climate for long-term or permanent records (RMM 522–523)

Records appraised as **long-term (30 years or over) or permanent** should be stored in a 24-hour air-conditioned, dust-free environment with stable, controlled temperature and humidity:

| Medium | Temperature | Relative humidity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | 20°C ± 2°C | 50% ± 5% |
| Microfilm | 14°C ± 2°C | 40% ± 5% |
| Electronic | 18°C ± 2°C | 40% ± 5% |
| Audiovisual | 4°C to 16°C | not higher than 40%, minimum fluctuations |

These requirements “may not currently be met by all government records storage facilities”. Heads of B/Ds should note them and include them in **newly furnished or constructed** facilities. Advice: Public Records Office of GRS (RMM 523).
