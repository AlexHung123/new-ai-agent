# Records retrieval and access

RMM 450–479: find the record, then decide who may see it. Two public-access tracks — the Code on Access to Information (current records) and the Public Records (Access) Rules (archives). Concept: [[records-access]]. Tracking sits on the hub: [[03-classification]].

## Retrieval (RMM 450–452)

Adopt an effective means to retrieve records — reference manuals, processing forms, index cards or a computer-assisted system (RMM 450). Each B/D should establish and maintain effective indexes (alphabetic, numeric or other) with enough detail that information can be made available when required (RMM 451). Process and transmit classified records under the Security Regulations (RMM 452).

## Life-cycle access (RMM 460)

Having regard to [[security-classification]], records should be made accessible throughout their life cycle to **authorised users only** (RMM 460).

## Code on Access to Information (RMM 461–464)

The Code, and the Public Records (Access) Rules, provide public access and set out exemptions (RMM 461).

Where practicable, records holdings except exempted categories should be identified and described so they can be made readily accessible **within 10 days** of a written request, and for more complicated cases **not exceeding 30 days** (RMM 462). B/Ds are to publish or make available a list of categories of records, a list of information published or otherwise available, and procedures and any charges for access to information not routinely published (RMM 463).

Under the Code, **the fact that a record is classified may not of itself be a valid reason for refusing public access** to the information it contains (RMM 464).

## Public Records (Access) Rules (RMM 465–471)

[[pro]] has a general responsibility for granting public access to archival records of **not less than 30 years old**, or archival records whose contents have been published or otherwise wholly disclosed (RMM 465).

**Unclassified** archival records reaching 30 years, held and accessioned by PRO, should be released **automatically** (RMM 466). **Classified** archival records held by PRO and reaching 30 years are reviewed by the creating/responsible B/D to determine access status (RMM 467). B/Ds should fully cooperate with PRO in the **annual classified records review**, given the policy of openness and liberal access (RMM 468).

Members of the public can apply for access to **closed** archival records **regardless of security classification**. The head of the transferring or responsible agency decides access status (RMM 469). The [[grs-director]], in discretion and in accordance with general instructions of the Chief Secretary for Administration, may permit inspection of closed archival records held by PRO (RMM 470). The GRS Director may withhold access to ensure safe custody and proper preservation (RMM 471).

## Personal data (RMM 472)

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, data subjects should be given access to their own personal data. Access by data users for purposes other than those for which the data were collected requires consent. Exemptions: sections 51–63 of the Ordinance.
