# Vital records protection

RMM 700–730: identify a short list of records without which the B/D cannot operate through an emergency, protect them, and keep the set current. Concept: [[vital-records]]. How-to publication: Records Management Publication No. 6. Disposal of time-expired items follows [[05-scheduling-disposal]].

## Purpose (RMM 700–702)

[[vital-records]] contain information **essential to the continued and effective operation** of a B/D during and after an emergency or disaster (RMM 700). Identify and protect them to manage crisis, keep major functions running, mitigate loss and damage, and protect the legal and financial rights of the Government and the people it serves (RMM 701). Detailed procedures: Publication No. 6 — *Manual on Vital Records Protection* (RMM 702).

## Identification and categories (RMM 706–710)

Categorise as follows (RMM 706):

| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Essential** | Emergency operating records for conducting emergency services |
| **Important** | Records documenting legal and functional status, and protecting financial assets and ownership, essential to re-establish functions and responsibilities |
| **Useful** | Records documenting the rights and claims of individuals, organisations, and members of the public |

The set may comprise any combination of copies, synopses or extracts, on any media (RMM 707). Finding aids and indexes to the selected records are themselves vital records (RMM 708). For electronic media, also protect hardware documentation, system and application software, technical and procedural documentation, data sets and files, hardcopy and softcopy of inputs, indexes and output, and processing files needed for integrity and contextual relationships (RMM 709).

Designation **must be highly selective**, by the [[drm]] with approval of the head or deputy head as appropriate. Choose only records that are absolutely essential to proper functioning, or that are irreplaceable, costly or time-consuming to replace or reconstruct (RMM 710).

## Protection methods (RMM 716–718)

Provide adequate protection and make the records accessible and usable when required (RMM 716). Methods should be economical, simple and suited to the organisation. Seek advice from the Security Bureau, Fire Services Department, Information Technology Services Department (as printed; now DPO in later government organisation) and [[grs]] as appropriate (RMM 717). Ensure that equipment to **read** vital records or their copies would be available in emergencies (RMM 718).

## Programme maintenance (RMM 726–730)

Regularly update the set at designated secure sites (RMM 726). Only authorised personnel should deal with access and transmittal (RMM 727). For vital records stored at GRS, the transferring B/D must document essential information, register the vital records schedule(s) with GRS, and periodically review or update by removing obsolete items and replacing them with the most recent version (RMM 728). Issue written procedures, train responsible staff, and **test and review** procedures regularly (RMM 729). Disposing of time-expired vital records follows Chapter 6 like other government records (RMM 730).
