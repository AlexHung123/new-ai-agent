# Source summary — Records Management Manual

Manual issued by the [[director-of-administration]] to prescribe the code of practices for a comprehensive records management programme in B/Ds (RMM 100–101). Printed title: *Records Management Manual of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region*, **2001 (with minor updates in November 2020)**. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_2.3.1_RM_Manual_(Eng)_2090718349128654848.md` (1,148 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

The Manual is a **procedure and policy booklet**, not a volume of the Government Regulations. It is to be read with the GRS Records Management Publications listed in [[annex-publications]] (RMM 112–113). Comments on amendments go to the [[grs-director]] (RMM 114).

## Force

Applies, unless specified otherwise, to the management of all government records in the conduct of official business (RMM 106). B/Ds should follow it **as far as possible** (RMM 103). Heads of B/Ds are reminded it is their duty to accord priority and resources (RMM 104). Hard bars in the text include no destruction without GRS Director agreement (RMM 605) and no records in a privatisation instrument without GRS Director transfer authority (RMM 822). Companion instruments cited in RMM 122 (Copyright Ordinance, Electronic Transactions Ordinance, Evidence Ordinance, Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, Code on Access to Information, CSR, Financial and Accounting Regulations, Standing Accounting Instructions, General Regulations, SPR, Security Regulations, circulars, Public Records (Access) Rules) have their own force. See [[00-introduction]].
