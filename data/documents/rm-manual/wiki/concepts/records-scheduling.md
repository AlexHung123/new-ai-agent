# Records scheduling

**Records scheduling** is developing **records retention and disposal schedules** that stipulate how long records should be retained and how they are to be disposed of (RMM 610; Appendix B). Schedules of government agencies should be drawn up with the concurrence of the [[grs-director]] (Appendix B).

Procedures differ for [[administrative-records]] (follow GARDS) and [[programme-records]] (B/D-built schedules authorised through the [[rmo]]) (RMM 611–615). See [[compare-admin-vs-programme]]. How-to: Publication No. 1.

## Upkeep

Most schedules will periodically need change. All changes should be agreed with the GRS Director; lodge requests with RMO first (RMM 616). Review **at least every 5 years** (RMM 617). Where practicable, assign definite retention periods **before** any disposal action (RMM 618). Permanent retention **in a B/D** is usually valid only when required by legislation (RMM 619) — archival permanent retention is a [[pro]] function, not an office-cupboard function.

Each B/D should keep a master set of schedules and disposal authorities as agreed with the GRS Director, plus documentation of records transferred, destroyed or migrated (RMM 620). Archival transfers follow schedules approved by the GRS Director (RMM 636). Development of schedules is exercised by the [[drm]] as approved by the [[head-of-bd]] (RMM 604).
