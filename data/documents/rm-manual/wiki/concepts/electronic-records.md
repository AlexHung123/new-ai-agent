# Electronic records

Electronic records are any information recorded in a form that only a computer or other electronic devices can retrieve and process, **and that satisfies the definition of record** in this Manual (Appendix B). Records created, collected or generated electronically as evidence of official business **are government records** and should be properly managed like other official records (RMM 123).

## What this compilation actually says

RMM 107: with the growth of computer technology, the Government “is in the process of formulating a new strategy and relevant policies” for electronic information systems and records. Detailed guidelines for e-mail and other electronic records “will be issued in due course.” That is a **2001/2020 snapshot**. Do not treat it as a live statement that no e-records policy exists. Later GRS issuances are not in this file. See [[synthesis]].

A complete electronic record should have **links to subject matter, function identification or related records** (RMM 313(g)). Long-term or permanent electronic records should be stored at 18°C ± 2°C and RH 40% ± 5% in 24-hour air-conditioning (RMM 522(c)). For [[vital-records]] on electronic media, protect hardware, software, technical documentation, data sets, inputs/outputs and processing files so contextual relationships survive (RMM 709). On function transfer, automated systems need agreed access, security, compatibility and servicing arrangements (RMM 812).
