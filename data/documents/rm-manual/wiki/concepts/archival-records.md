# Archival records

**Archives / permanent records** are documents created or received and accumulated in the course of affairs and preserved because of **continuing or permanent value** (Appendix B). Government policy includes identifying, preserving and providing access to archives for research, educational, cultural and other purposes (RMM 200(f)). [[grs]] selects, administers and preserves government archives and valuable publications, and is the authority on records of **long-term (30 years or over) or permanent** value (RMM 220). The [[pro]] is the designated central archives.

## Creation and storage

Create records known to have permanent value in a format that permits transfer to PRO (RMM 318). Long-term or permanent records should meet the climate bands in RMM 522 ([[04-storage-security]]). Consult PRO on such records still in B/D control (RMM 632).

## Appraisal and transfer

Records appraised as archival by the **Archivist of the PRO** should be transferred to and preserved by PRO (RMM 626). Each B/D should systematically transfer archival records under schedules approved by the [[grs-director]] (RMM 636). **All government records reaching 30 years old** should be appraised by PRO for archival value (RMM 637). Include supporting documentation or indexes (RMM 638).

PRO accepts for permanent preservation only if the Archivist confirms sufficient value, transfer is an appropriate means of preservation and future access, and the records have been prepared as required (RMM 639).

## Access

Public Records (Access) Rules: unclassified 30-year archives at PRO released automatically; classified ones reviewed by the creating B/D; public may apply for closed records; GRS Director may permit or withhold. See [[records-access]].

Permanent retention **in a B/D** is usually valid only when required by legislation (RMM 619) — that is not a substitute for PRO transfer.
