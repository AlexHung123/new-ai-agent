# Programme records

**Programme (or function) records** are created or received while carrying out the **primary functions, activities or missions** for which the B/D was established. They are **unique** to each B/D (RMM 417, 611; Appendix B).

## Classification and disposal

B/Ds should develop **their own** classification scheme for programme records. How-to: Records Management Publication No. 3 — *Subject Filing* (RMM 417). They should also develop their own retention and disposal schedules (RMM 613) and **must obtain disposal authority** from the [[grs-director]] through the [[rmo]] (RMM 615). All changes to those schedules are agreed with the GRS Director; lodge amendments with RMO first (RMM 616).

This is the opposite path from [[administrative-records]], which ride GRS’s common scheme and GARDS. See [[compare-admin-vs-programme]]. Mixing the two is how offices either over-retain or destroy the wrong series.
