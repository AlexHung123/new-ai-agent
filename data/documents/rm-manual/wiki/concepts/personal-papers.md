# Personal papers

**Personal papers** are documentary materials of a private nature that **do not relate to or have an effect upon** the conduct of official business. They are **excluded** from the definition of [[government-records]] (RMM 410; Appendix B).

## Examples (RMM 410)

- Materials accumulated by an officer **before** joining the service that are not subsequently used in government business.
- Materials relating solely to an individual’s private affairs (outside business, professional affiliations, private associations) that do not relate to official business and **do not require declaration** to the Government.
- Diaries, journals, personal correspondence or other personal notes that are **not** prepared or used for, or circulated or communicated in, the course of government business.

## Separation and mixed documents

Personal papers should be clearly designated and **at all times separated** from official records (RMM 411). If private matters and official business appear in the **same document**, copy it at receipt and treat the copy as an official record with personal information deleted; with the officer’s agreement the original may be put on file (RMM 412).

**The label is not the test.** Materials labelled “personal”, “confidential”, “private” or similarly **and used in the transaction of government business are official records** and are managed and disposed of as such (RMM 413).
