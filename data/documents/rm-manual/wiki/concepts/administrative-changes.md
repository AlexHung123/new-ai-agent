# Administrative changes

An **administrative change** is a change in the administration of government functions in a B/D (RMM 800). It may be internal (create, delete, or move a function between units), across B/Ds, or out of Government (outsourcing, sale/transfer to a private organisation, corporatisation). Intra-government procedure: [[07-administrative-changes]]. Extra-government: [[07-outsourcing-transfer]].

The point of the chapter is continuity of the function **and** an exit from unneeded records, without ad hoc destruction. Records not transferred and not required for further use are disposed of as other government records (RMM 802). The [[drm]] of the B/D that terminates an operation or holds records of a defunct operation must arrange transfer and/or disposal **with prior approval of [[grs]]** (RMM 803).

## Inside Government

New function → new or expanded [[recordkeeping-system]] (RMM 806). Deleted function → schedule for disposal via [[rmo]] (RMM 807). Transferred function → losing agency relinquishes control and passes related records **and control records in their entirety**; inheritor checks completeness, keeps original references, and **does not top-number** transferred records into a new series (RMM 810–814). If both sides still need the series, arrange access or duplicate (RMM 815).

## Outside Government

Nothing goes into a sale, contract or ordinance unless the [[grs-director]] has issued transfer authority. Custody ≠ ownership. See [[custody-ownership]] and [[compare-custody-ownership]].
