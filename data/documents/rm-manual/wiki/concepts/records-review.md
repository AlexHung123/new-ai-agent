# Records review, study and evaluation

Three monitoring layers. Chapter: [[08-monitoring]].

## GRS records review

Where circumstances warrant, [[grs]] reviews a B/D’s records management function for compliance with policies, procedures, directives and disposal authorities (RMM 900). Selection is by perceived GRS need or a B/D request (RMM 901). Written notice **at least 3 months** in advance (RMM 902).

## Study and survey

The [[grs-director]] may conduct studies and give instructions and advice on documentation, retention and cost-effective management (RMM 906), and from time to time coordinates **service-wide surveys** with heads of B/Ds (RMM 907). B/Ds should implement recommendations as far as practicable (RMM 908). Appendix B: a **records survey** gathers quantity, type, functions, locations, storage, accumulation rate and uses for planning.

## Internal evaluation

The [[drm]] should, as far as possible, evaluate the departmental function **every 5 years or more often** (RMM 915), using this Manual as yardstick (RMM 916) and Publication No. 7 — *Checklist for Proper Records Management Practices* — whenever applicable (RMM 917). Cooperating with GRS on evaluation is also a printed DRM duty ([[annex-drm-duties]] item j).

Do not confuse this with the **annual classified records access review** with [[pro]] (RMM 467–468) or the DRM’s **five-year security-downgrade** review (RMM 443). Those are different clocks.
