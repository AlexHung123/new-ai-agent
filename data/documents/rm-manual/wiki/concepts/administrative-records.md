# Administrative records

**Administrative records** document **common service activities relating to the internal administration** of B/Ds (RMM 418, 611). Appendix B adds: records created or received during day-to-day administrative activities dealing with **finance, accommodation, procurement and supply, establishment, personnel and other general administrative activities**, irrespective of medium. Records of this nature are **common** across government B/Ds.

## Classification and disposal

B/Ds should **adopt** the GRS subject classification scheme for administrative records in Records Management Publication No. 3 — *Subject Filing* (RMM 418). That government-wide scheme also feeds disposal sentences in Publication No. 4 — **GARDS** (*General Administrative Records Disposal Schedules*) (RMM 419). B/Ds should follow GARDS when disposing of administrative records of common nature (RMM 612).

This is the opposite path from [[programme-records]], which are unique and need a B/D-built scheme and GRS-authorised schedules. See [[compare-admin-vs-programme]].

GARDS itself is **not transcribed** in this compilation. Use the current GRS edition for retention periods.
