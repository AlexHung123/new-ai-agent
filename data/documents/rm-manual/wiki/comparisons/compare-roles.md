# Compare — records management roles

Shared responsibility (RMM 201): B/Ds create and collect; [[grs]] is the central agency. The [[director-of-administration]] issues the Manual but does not run the programme.

| Actor | Owns | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[director-of-administration]] | Issues and amends the Manual (RMM 100, 114) | Day-to-day RM or archives |
| [[head-of-bd]] | Programme, priority, resources; approves DRM’s disposal decisions; decides access to closed archives of records the B/D transferred; head/deputy-head approves vital-record designation (RMM 104, 209, 469, 604, 710) | Central standards; destruction authority |
| [[drm]] | Day-to-day programme; normally Departmental Secretary; five-year classified review; five-year internal evaluation; function-exit transfers (RMM 210–213, 443, 803, 915) | Destruction without GRS Director; issuing the Manual |
| [[grs]] | Government-wide policy, standards, training, inspection, records centres, archives (RMM 220) | Creating a B/D’s programme records |
| [[grs-director]] | Destruction bar; programme-schedule authority; closed-archive access discretion; extra-government transfer authority (RMM 605–606, 615, 470–471, 822) | Substituting for the HoD |
| [[pro]] | Central archives; 30-year appraisal; public access to archives; Central Preservation Library (RMM 318, 465–468, 626, 631, 637) | GARDS authoring (that is GRS publications) |
| [[rmo]] | Programme-schedule gateway; [[records-centre]] operations; deleted-function disposal logistics (RMM 615–616, 629–630, 807) | Archivist appraisal (PRO) |

Comments on the Manual go to the GRS Director (RMM 114). DRM nomination also goes there (RMM 213). See [[annex-drm-duties]] for the eleven DRM duties.
