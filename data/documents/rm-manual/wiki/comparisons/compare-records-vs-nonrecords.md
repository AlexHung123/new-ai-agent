# Compare — records, non-records, and personal papers

The test is **evidence of official business in a [[recordkeeping-system]]**, not uniqueness, not paper, and not the word on the cover. Chapter: [[03-classification]].

| | [[government-records]] | Non-records (RMM 406) | [[personal-papers]] (RMM 410) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Definition | Created/received in official business **and kept as evidence** (content, structure, context) | Documentary materials that do **not** meet record status | Private nature; **no relation to or effect upon** official business |
| In the recordkeeping system? | **Yes** — official record copy (RMM 404) | **No** | **No** — keep separated (RMM 411) |
| Extra copies | **Are records** if created/received to document official functions **and kept as evidence** (RMM 405) | Extra copies **for convenience or reference** only | — |
| Drafts / working papers | If they are the evidence | Drafts **if the official copy is properly filed** | — |
| Library / museum stock, blank forms, routing slips | — | Typical non-records | — |
| Label “personal” / “private” | If **used in official business**, they are official records (RMM 413) | — | Only if they truly do not affect official business |
| Mixed private + official in one document | Copy and file as official record, personal information deleted; original may be filed with officer’s agreement (RMM 412) | — | The private remainder, if separable |

Uniqueness of information is **not** the test (RMM 405). [[electronic-records]] that meet the evidence definition are government records (RMM 123).
