# Payment and record keeping

AP s.5 is short and strict: products are not payable before the Acceptance Certificate; maintenance is not payable during [[warranty]]. Tests: [[03-acceptance]]. Maintenance charging: [[05-maintenance]].

## Payment procedures (AP s.5.1)

The Contractor sends invoices **directly to the B/D that placed the purchase order**, or sends **electronic invoices via [[e-ps]]** to B/Ds that have adopted the e-Invoice service (as available under the Full Function of e-PS).

Payment becomes payable to the Contractor:

- for **hardware and software**: only **after** issue of the **Acceptance Certificate**;
- for **Maintenance Services**: **quarterly in arrears** commencing from the **expiry of the Warranty Period**.

Pay within **30 days** after such payment is payable **and** the B/D has received and accepted the Contractor’s invoice, **whichever is the later**.

The Government is not liable for any Maintenance Charge during the Warranty Period (AP s.6.2.1; restated here because it is a payment rule).

## Record keeping (AP s.5.2)

All communications with Contractors and acceptance test reports should be properly recorded and maintained and available for inspection. Classification until award/rejection: **RESTRICTED** (AP s.8.1; [[07-control]]).
