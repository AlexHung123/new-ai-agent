## Preamble (AP s.1.1)

The booklet sets out procedures for acquiring information technology products and related services by Government bureaux/departments under the Government Procurement of IT Products scheme. The acquisition procedures are summarised in Annex 1.

**Definitions.** Capitalised terms and expressions take the meanings in Clause 1 of the [[conditions-of-contract|Conditions of Contract of the GITP scheme]]. That contract text is not in this compilation.

## Eight features (AP s.1.2)

[[dpo|Digital Policy Office]] (or the then OGCIO) introduced GITP in **October 2023** to increase flexibility and efficiency, encourage competition among IT suppliers, and accelerate procurement of current products for digital government services.

1. IT products are grouped under major [[product-categories]] and sub-categories.
2. **Related services** include, but are not limited to, one-off support (system configuration, network cabling, network configuration, data erasing, support staff for IT-product tasks), **maintenance** of offered IT products, and **[[trade-in]]** services.
3. A list of [[gitp-providers]] is maintained by DPO. Providers have undertaken to comply with the GITP Scheme Participation Terms and Conditions.
4. A set of common terms — Conditions of Contract for Individual Procurement — is prepared for B/Ds to use as the basis of contracts with GITP Providers.
5. Sample IT products offered by providers are consolidated as a **reference IT products list** under the providers and published on DPO’s **ITG InfoStation**. For B/Ds’ reference only.
6. B/Ds are **not obliged to buy**, and GITP is **not obliged to sell**, the IT products on the reference lists.
7. DPO publishes **reference technical requirements** and **reference maintenance service requirements** for some products/services. They are for reference. B/Ds may tailor or add technical specification **within the scope of the sub-category**.
8. A procurement workflow in DPO’s [[e-ps|e-Procurement System]] supports invitation of quotations from GITP Providers. **There shall not be any other separate avenue** for issuing invitation to quotation under GITP. e-PS is the only avenue.
