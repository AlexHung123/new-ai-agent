# Contractor’s performance monitoring

AP s.7 is a single procedure: the [[cpar|Contractor Performance Appraisal Report]]. Unlike a standing SPR performance-reporting duty for every contract, GITP only asks for a CPAR when the B/D rates performance **adverse**. Form: Annex 14 ([[annexes]]; not transcribed). Escalation: [[gitp-support-team]].

## Adverse CPAR only (AP s.7.1)

If deemed necessary, B/Ds **can** submit an adverse CPAR at **completion (or termination)** of each individual contract to evaluate the [[gitp-providers|GITP Provider]]. An adverse CPAR is a CPAR rated as **“Not satisfactory”**. Otherwise, B/Ds are **not required** to submit CPARs.

For an adverse CPAR, indicate the unsatisfactory area and provide supplementary information/justification where applicable.

Forward the completed CPAR to the GITP Provider for **acknowledgement and signing**. The provider may provide clarifications and/or **express disagreement** on the assessment. If there is disagreement, B/Ds may escalate through the GITP Support Team.
