# Invitation and receipt of Proposals

AP ss.3.3–3.4 cover who is invited, who may approve issue of the invitation, what the approval request must contain, dispatch **only** through [[e-ps]], and receipt of Proposals. Rank tables compared: [[compare-approving-authorities]]. Ceiling: [[quotation-limit]]. Hub: [[02-acquisition]].

## Who is invited (AP s.3.3.1)

For purchases with estimated value **up to** the prevailing quotation limit in SPR 220(a), unless otherwise justified and approved, invite **all** [[gitp-providers]] of the respective sub-category.

Over that limit: **open tender** under SPR Chapter III — not this scheme. SPR 205 (quoted in the booklet) forbids splitting or shortening to evade the limit. See [[quotation-limit]].

## Authority to issue (AP s.3.3.2)

Officers **not lower than** the stated rank approve issue of invitation. Two columns: all relevant GITP Providers invited, versus not all invited.

| Estimated value | All relevant providers invited | Not all relevant providers invited (Note 5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not exceeding **$50,000** as in SPR 260(a) | Supplies Supervisor II or equivalent | Same |
| Not exceeding **20%** of the prevailing quotation limit | Assistant Supplies Officer / Executive Officer II or equivalent | Supplies Officer / Executive Officer I or equivalent |
| Not exceeding **50%** of the prevailing quotation limit | Supplies Officer / Executive Officer I or equivalent | Senior Supplies Officer / Senior Executive Officer or equivalent |
| Not exceeding the **prevailing quotation limit** | Senior Supplies Officer / Senior Executive Officer or equivalent | Chief Supplies Officer / Chief Executive Officer or equivalent |

**Note 5** (reconstructed from a split OCR paragraph): a GITP Provider of the sub-category may be omitted where that provider has been awarded a related **system implementation** contract and is **debarred** from the subsequent computer-equipment procurement by a debarment provision in that contract.

## Request for approval (AP s.3.3.3)

When seeking approval to issue an invitation with estimated value not exceeding the prevailing SPR 220(a) quotation limit, include **at least**:

1. estimated purchase value;
2. sub-category against which providers will be invited;
3. confirmation whether **all** GITP Providers under that sub-category are invited; if not, names of those not invited and **reasons**;
4. the [[brief]];
5. confirmation that product/service specifications are general, based on functional and performance requirements, and SPR 350(b);
6. [[conflict-of-interest]] declaration;
7. confirmation of **availability of funds**.

Sample form: Annex 5 ([[annexes]]). B/Ds may amend it.

## Dispatch (AP s.3.3.4)

On approval, issue the invitation with the Brief **via e-PS only**. Refer to e-PS documentation.

Minimum time normally allowed for Proposals:

- [[product-categories|Categories A and B]]: at least **two weeks**
- Category C: at least **one week**

B/Ds may adjust according to project requirement. **Funds must be available** before initiating acquisition.

An invitation to submit Proposals **does not constitute a commitment** by the Government to order or purchase any hardware, software, or services.

## Receipt (AP s.3.4)

Require GITP Providers to submit Proposals via e-PS — already specified in **Clause 7.3 of the Sample Brief**. Receipt handling: e-PS documentation.

From receipt/opening until a decision, correspondence is **RESTRICTED** (AP s.8.1; [[07-control]]).
