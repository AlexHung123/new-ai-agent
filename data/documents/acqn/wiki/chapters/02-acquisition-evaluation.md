# Evaluation, acceptance, and cancellation

AP ss.3.5–3.9 run from opening Proposals to a letter of acceptance or a letter of cancellation. Criteria must already be in the [[brief]] (AP s.3.2.4). Bases compared: [[compare-evaluation-bases]]. Who may accept: [[compare-approving-authorities]]. Hub: [[02-acquisition]].

## Evaluation panel (AP s.3.5)

Evaluate in accordance with the assessment criteria in the Brief. If needed, or when the estimated purchase amount is high, consider a panel of **end-user** representatives and [[itmu]] staff or the Client IT Services Manager of [[dpo]].

## Technical evaluation (AP s.3.5.1)

Preferably conducted by staff with IT knowledge. Consider:

- written commitment on compliance of technical/service requirements in the Proposal; and
- whether details meet the pre-defined technical/service requirements in the Brief.

Written clarifications on technical aspects as necessary. Seek technical advice from ITMU or the Client IT Services Manager.

**Only Proposals which conform fully to the stipulated mandatory technical/service requirements at the time of proposal opening** can be considered for acceptance.

- **Overall** basis: partial and incomplete proposals **must not** be considered.
- **Group** basis: partial and incomplete proposals **within a particular group** shall also be rejected.

SPR **365** applies when seeking clarification and/or missing information.

## Price evaluation (AP s.3.5.2)

Conduct price evaluation in accordance with the **Estimated Contract Price** as defined in Clause 1 of the [[conditions-of-contract]]. Other than correction of **arithmetical errors**, do **not** accept any change in quoted price during evaluation.

## Recommendation (AP s.3.6)

Normally accept the **lowest conforming offer** (no marking scheme) or the conforming offer with the **highest combined score** (marking scheme), unless otherwise justified. Negotiation with the selected Contractor for better terms: **SPR 385** and **SPR Appendix III(J)** if considered necessary.

## Approval to accept (AP s.3.7)

Officers **not lower than** the stated rank approve acceptance. The right-hand column also applies when the recommendation is **not** the lowest conforming offer (no marking scheme) or **not** the highest combined score (marking scheme), under exceptional circumstances with full justifications.

| Value of purchase | All relevant providers invited | Not all invited (Note 5) **or** not the default offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not exceeding **$50,000** as in SPR 260(a) | Assistant Supplies Officer / Executive Officer II or equivalent | Same |
| Not exceeding **20%** of the prevailing quotation limit | Supplies Officer / Executive Officer I or equivalent | Senior Supplies Officer / Senior Executive Officer or equivalent |
| Not exceeding **50%** of the prevailing quotation limit | Senior Supplies Officer / Senior Executive Officer or equivalent | Chief Supplies Officer / Chief Executive Officer or equivalent |
| Not exceeding the **prevailing quotation limit** | Chief Supplies Officer or Chief Executive Officer or equivalent | **D1** or equivalent |

At least include in the approval request:

1. a price summary;
2. details of the recommended offer(s);
3. confirmation that recommended items comply with the Brief;
4. if not the lowest conforming / highest combined score, **reasons for rejecting** that offer;
5. comparison with last purchase prices, if available;
6. confirmation of availability of funds;
7. declaration of conflict of interests.

Sample: Annex 6. B/Ds may amend it.

## Letter of acceptance (AP s.3.8)

On approval, issue a letter of acceptance (sample Annex 7) to the selected GITP Provider and **copy to `gitp_admin@digitalpolicy.gov.hk`** as a firm commitment of the Government. Order(s) may be placed within the period specified in the Brief under **“Price Validity”**, counting from the Date of Acceptance of Proposal.

## Cancellation (AP s.3.9)

On approval to cancel, issue a letter of cancellation of procurement (sample Annex 8) to the selected GITP Providers and **copy to `gitp_admin@digitalpolicy.gov.hk`**.
