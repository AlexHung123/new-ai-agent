# On-going maintenance

AP s.6 covers the [[warranty]] year and optional paid maintenance afterwards. Which scheme (M1–M3 / software) is chosen in the [[brief]] (AP s.3.2.3). Charging: [[04-payment]]. Scheme catalogue: [[maintenance-schemes]] (Annexes 3–4 not transcribed).

## Warranty (AP s.6.1)

The Contractor warrants that delivered hardware and/or software will be **new** and **free from defect** in design, material, workmanship, and installation during the Warranty Period. **Warranty Period** is the **12-month period**, or such extended period as agreed between B/Ds and the Contractor, starting from the **Acceptance Date**.

During the Warranty Period the Contractor provides **free-of-charge Maintenance Services** to keep the hardware and/or software in proper order. AP s.3.2.3: that service follows the Maintenance Scheme specified in the Brief; a longer warranty must be specified in the Brief.

## After-warranty maintenance (AP s.6.2.1)

Specify the required **Maintenance Period** (during which a Maintenance Item — hardware and/or software — continues to receive Maintenance Services after the Warranty Period) in the Brief. B/Ds have **sole discretion** whether to acquire maintenance from the Contractor after the free Warranty Period.

Maintenance Charges are payable **quarterly in arrears**. The Government is **not liable** for any Maintenance Charge during the Warranty Period.

Maintenance Services for a Maintenance Item continue throughout the Maintenance Period after the Warranty Period unless B/Ds give the Contractor **not less than 1 month’s notice** that Maintenance Services for that item shall be terminated.

## Extension of Maintenance Period (AP s.6.2.2)

B/Ds may extend the Maintenance Period for period(s) of **not more than 12 months in aggregate**. Exercise the option **not less than 3 months** before expiry of the Maintenance Period, by written notice specifying the extension; the period is then deemed extended on the **same terms and conditions**.

If the initial extension is **less than 12 months**, the Government may make **further extensions** (no limit on the number of times) on **not less than 1 month’s** notice before expiry as last extended, always subject to the **12-month aggregate**.

Before issuing the written notice, obtain proper approval from the appropriate B/D authority in accordance with **SPR 520** (variation of the Individual Contract).
