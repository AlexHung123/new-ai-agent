## Overview (AP s.3.1)

B/Ds issue a “requirement specifications document” (the [[brief]]) to invite Proposals from [[gitp-providers]] in the relevant [[product-categories|sub-categories]], **via [[e-ps]] only**. The Brief describes requirements on hardware, software, and/or service. B/Ds may issue a Brief to one or more sub-categories of one or more major categories, and **are recommended to issue the Brief to all GITP Providers under the sub-category(ies)**.

Two Outline of Brief templates:

- **Annex 2a** — evaluation on **overall** basis
- **Annex 2b** — evaluation **by group or item** basis

B/Ds may amend the document as deemed necessary. On receipt, invited providers prepare and submit a Proposal via e-PS before the proposal closing time/date in the Brief: proposed configuration of hardware/software and/or related implementation services, and/or Trade-in Items and/or Maintenance Services, and prices. For service requirements, include work approach, estimated manpower, and implementation schedule as the Brief instructs.

## The SPR 220 gate (AP s.3.3.1)

GITP is a quotation-band channel. Full invitation rules: [[02-acquisition-invitation]] and [[quotation-limit]].

- Estimated value **up to** the prevailing quotation limit in SPR 220(a): unless otherwise justified and approved, invite **all** GITP Providers of the respective sub-category.
- Estimated value **over** that limit: arrange an **open tender** for the products/services under **SPR Chapter III**. Not GITP.

SPR 205 is quoted in the booklet and applies when choosing GITP versus Chapter III tendering: limits are the **total value** of stores or services of a similar nature (or total revenue) in a single purchase or contract; do not evade limits by dividing requirements or shortening usual contract duration; consolidate similar requirements; Controlling Officers may be held personally responsible if they allow or order procurement without proper authority.

## Urgent minor purchases ≤ $5,000 (AP s.3.10)

For an **urgent** purchase with estimated value **not exceeding HK$5,000**, B/Ds may arrange **direct local purchase** under SPR 265 or SPR 290, whichever is appropriate. **Such procurement will not be covered by the GITP scheme.** SPR 205 still applies: do not divide requirements into instalments.

## Financial limits auto-update (AP s.3.11)

The financial limits in AP ss.3.3.1, 3.3.2, 3.3.3, 3.7, and 3.10 **will be automatically updated** in accordance with SPR 260(a), 260(b), 220(a), and SPR 265/290 if those SPR figures are revised. Printed dollars in this compilation (including the Jan 2024 **$1.36 million** quotation-limit snapshot in the amendment history) are a snapshot. Rank tables: [[compare-approving-authorities]].
