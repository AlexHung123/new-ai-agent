## Product / service requirements (AP s.3.2.1)

Include details of product/service requirements in the Brief. Reference technical and maintenance requirements on ITG InfoStation are for reference; B/Ds may tailor or add specification **within the sub-category**. Sample products on the reference list are for reference; [[gitp-providers]] **can propose products not on the list**.

Services through GITP should only relate to set-up, configuration, and support of day-to-day operation of related IT products. **Not included:** development and/or maintenance of custom program(s); on-going support of office systems and/or networks by staff stationed at user site.

Technical information to include (Brief parts as numbered in the booklet):

| Part | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Part 2 — General information | User department; existing equipment to be connected |
| Part 3 — General requirements | Site location, connectivity, implementation schedule, specific project requirements (e.g. security) |
| Part 4 — Equipment requirements | Hardware and software specifications; warranty/maintenance requirements |
| Part 5 — Service requirements | Service specifications (e.g. server hardening) |

Specifications must observe **SPR 350(b)**: general enough, based on **functional and performance** requirements.

If an item is on EPD’s **Green Procurement List**, extract the Green Requirements and associated Verification Methods from the EPD site (`https://epd.host.ccgo.hksarg/ERPurchase/index.html`) and put them in the Brief.

Advice: departmental [[itmu]] or DPO Client IT Services Manager. If B/Ds approach GITP Providers for product information, **no perceived advantage** in the subsequent invitation. ITMU contact list and sample completed Briefs: ITG InfoStation (`itmucontactlist.htm`; `content/gitp/`). e-PS documentation: `content/e-ps/eProcure/index.htm`. e-PS matters: [[08-enquiries]].

## Implementation plan (AP s.3.2.2)

Unless prior agreement, the selected provider (Contractor) completes work to this default:

| Activity | Completion |
| --- | --- |
| Hardware, software and documentation delivery | Within **6 weeks** from the date of written order |
| Hardware and software installation | Same |
| Installation tests | Same |
| Function tests | Same |
| Reliability tests | Within **12 weeks** from the date of written order |
| Completion | Same |

Notes:

1. A different Completion Date for a particular project **must** be specified in the Brief at invitation.
2. Products needing an export licence (e.g. security / encryption): allow time; B/Ds may need to complete/sign an end-user certificate.
3. Delivery during **normal office hours**. Installation in the same **prime maintenance period** of the [[maintenance-schemes|Maintenance Scheme]] (M1 Option A / M1 Option B / M2 / M3) the Government would subscribe.
4. At least **4 weeks** is provided for reliability tests (see [[acceptance-tests]]).

## Warranty / maintenance (AP s.3.2.3)

Normally **one-year free-of-charge** Maintenance Service, to the Maintenance Scheme specified in the Brief, during the [[warranty|Warranty Period]] for IT products (hardware and software). Longer warranty: specify in the Brief. Four hardware schemes and eight software schemes: Annexes 3 and 4 ([[annexes]]; bodies not in this conversion). Specify the required scheme in the Brief.

## Basis of acceptance (AP s.3.2.4)

State clearly in the Brief:

- whether a **marking scheme** will be used, or evaluation on **total offer price** only (B/Ds may adopt a marking scheme under **SPR 221A**);
- whether the Proposal is considered on an **overall**, **group**, or **itemised** basis;
- price assessment methodology — e.g. **5-year cost** (one-off plus maintenance charges / periodic licence fee for the **4-year period after the one-year free warranty**) or **one-off cost**;
- any other assessment criteria.

## Trade-in, validity, liquidated damages (AP ss.3.2.5–3.2.7)

Before opting for [[trade-in]] under GITP, consider alternative disposal in **SPR Chapter XI** and **GLD Circular No. 3/2022** (as updated). If using trade-in, stipulate requirements in the Brief; quoted trade-in price is taken into account in price evaluation.

Stipulate the required **validity period** after assessing how long evaluation will take (AP s.3.2.6).

Specify the **daily rate of liquidated damages** for delay of Add-on Hardware and/or Add-on Software ready for use (e.g. failing Reliability Tests, or failing to deliver to the required Location) or delay on completion of Support Services, as in **Clause 14** of the [[conditions-of-contract]]. The percentage should reflect the Government’s **genuine pre-estimated loss**; in doubt, seek Department of Justice advice. References include administrative cost of monitoring the Contract over the delay, and maintenance price difference between existing items and replacement items under the Contract.
