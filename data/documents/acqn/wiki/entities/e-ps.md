# e-Procurement System

DPO’s **e-Procurement System (e-PS)** is the **only** avenue for issuing invitation to quotation under GITP. “There shall not be any other separate avenue” (AP s.1.2(h)). The [[brief]] goes out through e-PS; Proposals come back through e-PS (AP ss.3.1, 3.3.4, 3.4).

## What must go through e-PS

- Invitation with the Brief, after issue-approval (AP s.3.3.4).
- Submission of Proposals by [[gitp-providers]] — Sample Brief **Clause 7.3** already states this (AP s.3.4).
- Optional **e-Invoice** to B/Ds that have adopted that service under e-PS Full Function (AP s.5.1).

How to operate the screens is **not** in this booklet; B/Ds are told to use e-PS documentation on ITG InfoStation (`content/e-ps/eProcure/index.htm`). Operational contact: e-Procurement Programme Management Office, helpdesk 8107 0608 (AP s.9; [[08-enquiries]]).

## What e-PS is not

e-PS does not itself approve invitation or acceptance — B/D ranks do ([[compare-approving-authorities]]). It does not replace SPR. An invitation via e-PS is **not** a commitment to order (AP s.3.3.4). Over the [[quotation-limit]], the buy leaves GITP for SPR Chapter III open tender; this booklet does not say that those tenders run in e-PS.
