# GITP Providers

Listed IT suppliers maintained by [[dpo]] who have undertaken to comply with the **GITP Scheme Participation Terms and Conditions** (AP s.1.2(c)). Invitation is by [[product-categories|sub-category]] through [[e-ps]] only. They are not a standing contractor panel with an exclusive right to supply (AP s.8.4(b)).

## What membership means

- Sample products they offer are consolidated as a **reference IT products list** on ITG InfoStation (AP s.1.2(e)). B/Ds are not obliged to buy, and GITP is not obliged to sell, those products (AP s.1.2(f)).
- Providers **may propose products not on the reference list** (AP s.3.2.1).
- Common contract basis: [[conditions-of-contract|Conditions of Contract for Individual Procurement]] (AP s.1.2(d)).
- A Brief should be issued to providers in the relevant sub-category(ies); B/Ds **are recommended to invite all** under those sub-categories (AP s.3.1). Below the [[quotation-limit]], inviting fewer than all needs justification and a higher approving rank (AP ss.3.3.1–3.3.2).

## Debarment (Note 5)

A provider of the sub-category may be **not invited** where that provider has been awarded a related **system implementation** contract and is debarred from the subsequent computer-equipment procurement by a debarment provision in that contract (AP s.3.3.2 Note 5; OCR reconstructed). Names of those omitted, and reasons, go in the invitation-approval request (AP s.3.3.3(c)).

## After selection

The selected provider is the **Contractor**. Default implementation timetable and tests: [[02-acquisition-brief]], [[acceptance-tests]]. Performance: optional adverse [[cpar]]. Approaching a provider for product information **before** invitation must not confer a perceived advantage (AP s.3.2.1).
