# GITP Support Team

DPO team named for **scheme enquiries** (AP s.9) and for **escalation when a [[gitp-providers|GITP Provider]] disagrees with an adverse [[cpar]]** (AP s.7.1). It is not an approving authority for invitation or acceptance.

e-PS questions go to the **e-Procurement Programme Management Office** (helpdesk 8107 0608), not this team. See [[08-enquiries]].

## CPAR escalation (AP s.7.1)

If a provider expresses disagreement on an adverse CPAR, B/Ds may escalate the case through the GITP Support Team.
