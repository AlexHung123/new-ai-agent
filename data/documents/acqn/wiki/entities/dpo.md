# Digital Policy Office

[[dpo|DPO]] (or the then **Office of the Government Chief Information Officer**) introduced the [[gitp-scheme]] in October 2023 (AP s.1.2). It is the scheme owner in this booklet: it maintains the list of [[gitp-providers]], publishes reference products and requirements on **ITG InfoStation**, provides the common [[conditions-of-contract]], and runs the [[e-ps|e-Procurement System]] workflow that is the only invitation avenue.

## What DPO does in GITP

- Maintains the list of GITP Providers who have undertaken the GITP Scheme Participation Terms and Conditions (AP s.1.2(c)).
- Consolidates sample IT products as a reference list under each provider and publishes it on ITG InfoStation (AP s.1.2(e)).
- Publishes reference technical requirements and reference maintenance service requirements — for reference; B/Ds may tailor within the sub-category (AP s.1.2(g)).
- Developed the GITP procurement workflow in e-PS (AP s.1.2(h)).
- Provides a **Client IT Services Manager** as a technical-advice contact alongside departmental [[itmu]]s (AP s.3.2.1).
- Receives copies of letters of acceptance and cancellation at `gitp_admin@digitalpolicy.gov.hk` (AP ss.3.8–3.9).
- Hosts the [[gitp-support-team]] for scheme enquiries and CPAR disagreement escalation (AP ss.7.1, 9).

DPO is **not** the approving authority for a B/D’s invitation or acceptance. Those ranks sit in the B/D (AP ss.3.3.2, 3.7; [[compare-approving-authorities]]). DPO is not a party obliged to sell; the listing is optional on both sides (AP s.1.2(f)).
