# ITMU / Client IT Services Manager

Departmental **IT Management Units (ITMUs)** and the **Client IT Services Manager of [[dpo]]** are the technical-advice contacts named in the booklet. They do not approve invitation or acceptance.

## Advice on the Brief (AP s.3.2.1)

B/Ds may approach ITMU staff or the Client IT Services Manager for technical advice when drawing up the [[brief]]. The contact list is on ITG InfoStation (`content/itmu/itmucontactlist.htm`). Specifications must still follow **SPR 350(b)** (functional and performance requirements). If B/Ds also approach [[gitp-providers]] for product information, they must not give any perceived advantage in the later invitation.

## Evaluation (AP s.3.5)

When needed, or when the estimated purchase amount is high, B/Ds should consider a panel comprising **end-user** representatives **and** ITMU staff or the Client IT Services Manager to evaluate Proposals. Technical evaluation should preferably be done by staff with IT knowledge; technical advice from ITMU / Client IT Services Manager should be sought, including on written clarifications (AP s.3.5.1). See [[proposal-evaluation]].

Personnel who prepare technical specifications or evaluate Proposals must declare [[conflict-of-interest]] (AP s.8.3).
