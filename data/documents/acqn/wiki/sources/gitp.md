# Source summary — GITP Acquisition Procedures

Booklet issued for B/Ds buying under the [[gitp-scheme|Listing Arrangement for Government Procurement of IT Products]]. Owner: [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]] (then OGCIO). Scheme launched **October 2023**. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_Acqn-Procedures_(GITP)_v1.3c_2090718397556084736.md` (546 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

The booklet is a **procedure manual under SPR Chapter II**, not a volume of the Government Regulations. Capitalised terms take their meaning from Clause 1 of the [[conditions-of-contract]] (AP s.1.1), which is a separate instrument.

## Force

GITP procedures apply to acquisitions of in-scope IT products and related services from [[gitp-providers]] via [[e-ps]]. They do not displace SPR. Where this booklet and SPR diverge on a live limit, AP s.3.11 says the booklet tables **follow SPR**. Circulars cited in the text (GLD Circular 3/2022; CSB Circulars 2/2004 and 4/2007) have their own force. See [[00-introduction]] and [[quotation-limit]].

## Reading the conversion

- The title-page image and Annex 1 flowchart are images; they are not transcribed.
- Annexes 2–14 are listed in the TOC and cited in the body but have **no body text** in this markdown.
- AP s.2.1 is missing the “i)” marker before “Supply of IT products”; treated as item (i).
- AP s.2.2 merges Category A5 and A6 into one table row. Reconstruction is on [[product-categories]] at `confidence: medium`.
- Note 5 (AP s.3.3.2) is split across lines; reconstructed as debarment after a related system-implementation contract.
- “dayto-day” and similar glues are reconstructed as ordinary English.
- Rank titles (SS II, ASO, EO II, …) are as printed; “or equivalent” is part of the rule.
