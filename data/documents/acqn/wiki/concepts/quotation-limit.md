# SPR quotation limit (GITP ceiling)

GITP is usable only while the estimated purchase sits **at or below** the prevailing quotation limit in **SPR 220(a)** (AP s.3.3.1). Above that limit, B/Ds **shall arrange an open tender** under SPR Chapter III. The booklet does not create a GITP path through the tender band.

Treat **$1.36 million** as the January 2024 snapshot printed in the booklet, not a promise it is still current. Rank bands are expressed as **20% / 50% / 100% of the prevailing quotation limit** plus the SPR 260(a) **$50,000** band — those percentages move with SPR 220(a). See [[compare-approving-authorities]].

## SPR 205

The booklet quotes SPR 205 in full. Limits are the **total value** of stores or services of a similar nature (or total revenue) obtained in a **single purchase or contract**. Do not evade by dividing into instalments or reducing usual contract duration. Consolidate similar requirements. Controlling Officers may be held **personally responsible** for amounts expended or revenue lost without proper authority.

The same non-splitting rule is restated for urgent ≤ $5,000 direct purchases that **leave GITP** (AP s.3.10; SPR 265 or 290).
