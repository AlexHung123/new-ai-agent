# Warranty Period

The **Warranty Period** is the **12-month** period, or a longer period agreed between the B/D and the Contractor, starting from the **Acceptance Date** (AP s.6.1). During it the Contractor warrants that delivered hardware and/or software is **new** and **free from defect** in design, material, workmanship, and installation, and provides **free-of-charge Maintenance Services**. Chapter: [[05-maintenance]]. Tests that unlock the Acceptance Date: [[acceptance-tests]].

## What the Brief must say

Normally one-year FOC maintenance in accordance with the [[maintenance-schemes|Maintenance Scheme]] specified in the [[brief]] (M1 Option A / M1 Option B / M2 / M3 for hardware; one of eight software schemes) (AP s.3.2.3). A longer warranty must be **clearly specified in the Brief**.

The Government is **not liable** for any Maintenance Charge during the Warranty Period (AP s.6.2.1). Product payment is due only after the Acceptance Certificate; maintenance is quarterly in arrears **from expiry of warranty** (AP s.5.1).

## After warranty

Paid maintenance is optional and at the B/D’s sole discretion. Specify the Maintenance Period in the Brief; terminate an item on **1 month’s** notice; extend in aggregate **not more than 12 months** under AP s.6.2.2 and **SPR 520**.
