# Conditions of Contract for Individual Procurement

A common set of terms prepared by [[dpo]] for B/Ds to adopt as the basis of contracts with [[gitp-providers]] (AP s.1.2(d)). **The CoC text is not in this compilation.** AP s.1.1: capitalised terms and expressions in the booklet take the meanings in **Clause 1** of the Conditions of Contract of the GITP scheme.

## Clauses the booklet cites

| CoC | What the booklet uses it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Clause 1** | Definitions, including **Estimated Contract Price** for price evaluation (AP ss.1.1, 3.5.2) |
| **Sample Brief Clause 7.3** | Proposals must be submitted via [[e-ps]] (AP s.3.4) |
| **Clauses 11.1 / 11.2** | Remedies if reliability tests still fail six weeks after first submission — including refrain from issuing further orders (AP s.4.3) |
| **Clause 14** | Daily liquidated damages for delay of Add-on Hardware/Software ready for use, or delay on Support Services (AP s.3.2.7) |
| **Clause 23** | Indemnity for data remaining in replaced storage media (AP s.8.6) |

**Contract Schedules Schedule 8** holds Reliability Levels used in [[acceptance-tests]] (AP s.4.3).

Related non-CoC instruments also not in this file: GITP Scheme Participation Terms and Conditions; Reference Maintenance Service Requirements (Clauses 1.11, 1.14 cited at AP s.8.6).
