# Trade-in services

**Trade-in** of used computer hardware is an in-scope related service under GITP (AP ss.1.2(b), 2.1(iv)). It is optional. Before using it, B/Ds should consider **alternative disposal** in **SPR Chapter XI** and **GLD Circular No. 3/2022** (as updated) (AP s.3.2.5). Control overlay: [[07-control]].

## If the B/D opts in

Stipulate trade-in requirements in the [[brief]]. The price quoted for Trade-in Item(s) **shall be taken into account in the price evaluation** (AP s.3.2.5). Invited [[gitp-providers]] may include Trade-in Items in the Proposal (AP s.3.1).

## Data and environmental law

Before trade-in, securely delete classified information following “Destruction of Classified Information” in the IT Security Documents (AP s.8.5).

Trade-in Items governed by the **Product Eco-responsibility Ordinance** (Cap. 603), the **Waste Disposal Ordinance** (Cap. 354), and subsidiary regulations (regulated electrical equipment; chemical wastes) must be handled by the Contractor in line with those statutes — including licences, export permits, and rules on collection, storage, packaging, and labelling — in the Contract and in any later transfer or export (AP s.8.5). Recycle labels at delivery: AP s.4.5 ([[03-acceptance]]).

Parts removed in hardware maintenance are a related but distinct rule (Contractor property if data erased; Government may retain uncleared media) — AP s.8.6.
