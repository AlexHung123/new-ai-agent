# Product categories and sub-categories

GITP groups IT products into three major categories (AP s.2.2). Each product is classified into **one sub-category only**. Invitation, minimum response time, and [[gitp-support-team]] contacts all fork on this map. Side-by-side: [[compare-product-categories]]. Hub: [[01-scope-categorisation]].

**OCR.** In the conversion, A5 (Network Software) and A6 (Power Equipment) share one table row with products concatenated. The split below treats network-management / monitoring / virtual security appliance software as **A5** and UPS / automatic transfer switch as **A6**. Confidence on that split is **medium**; verify against the printed booklet.

## Using categories in a buy

A [[brief]] may go to one or more sub-categories of one or more major categories; B/Ds are recommended to invite **all** [[gitp-providers]] under those sub-categories (AP s.3.1). Specifications must stay **within the scope of the sub-category** (AP s.1.2(g)). Minimum time to respond: **two weeks** for A and B, **one week** for C (AP s.3.3.4).
