# Maintenance schemes

GITP offers **four hardware** Maintenance Schemes and **eight software** Maintenance Schemes (AP s.3.2.3). B/Ds pick the required scheme in the [[brief]]. The scheme tables are **Annexes 3 and 4**, which are **not transcribed** in this conversion. Do not invent service levels. Chapter: [[05-maintenance]]. Catalogue: [[annexes]].

## Named hardware schemes

The body text names four hardware schemes (AP ss.3.2.2 Note 3, 3.2.3):

- **M1 (Option A)**
- **M1 (Option B)**
- **M2**
- **M3**

Installation must be carried out in the same **prime maintenance period** of the scheme to which the Government would subscribe (AP s.3.2.2 Note 3(b)).

The eight software schemes are not named in the body — only counted. Reference maintenance service requirements (including Clauses 1.11 and 1.14 on removed parts and storage-media erasure) live on ITG InfoStation and are cited at AP s.8.6 ([[07-control]]).

## How schemes attach to the contract

- During [[warranty]]: FOC maintenance to the specified scheme (AP s.3.2.3, s.6.1).
- After warranty: optional paid [[warranty|Maintenance Period]]; quarterly in arrears; 1-month item termination; 12-month aggregate extension (AP s.6.2).
- Price evaluation may use **5-year cost**, i.e. one-off plus four years of maintenance / periodic licence after the free year (AP s.3.2.4(c)).
