# GITP listing arrangement

The **Listing Arrangement for Government Procurement of IT Products (GITP)** is DPO’s October 2023 scheme for B/Ds to buy in-scope IT products and related services from listed [[gitp-providers]] through [[e-ps]]. It is a **quotation-band channel under SPR Chapter II**, not a standing contract and not a volume of the Government Regulations. Features: AP s.1.2. Scope: [[01-scope-categorisation]]. Procedure: [[02-acquisition]].

## Design

| Feature | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Catalogue | Products in [[product-categories]] A–C; one sub-category each |
| Services | One-off support, maintenance, [[trade-in]] — not custom development or on-site ongoing office/network support |
| Membership | DPO list; Participation Terms undertaken |
| Contract | Common [[conditions-of-contract]] for Individual Procurement |
| Reference list | Sample products on ITG InfoStation; optional to buy or sell |
| Specs | Reference technical / maintenance requirements; B/Ds may tailor inside the sub-category |
| Channel | e-PS only |

Purpose stated in AP s.1.2: flexibility and efficiency; competition among IT suppliers; faster access to current products for digital government services.

## Ceiling

GITP runs **up to** the prevailing SPR 220(a) [[quotation-limit]]. Above it, open tender under SPR Chapter III. Urgent ≤ $5,000 may leave the scheme entirely (AP s.3.10).
