# Conflict of interest

GITP does not write its own ethics code. It points officers at **SPR 185 and 186** and **CSB Circular No. 2/2004** for anyone involved in **preparing technical specifications** or **evaluating Proposals** (AP s.8.3). Bribery is a separate pointer: **Prevention of Bribery Ordinance** (Cap. 201) and **CSB Circular No. 4/2007** (AP s.8.2). Chapter: [[07-control]].

## Where a declaration is required

| Point in the process | What the booklet says |
| --- | --- |
| Before approaching providers for product information | No **perceived advantage** in the subsequent invitation (AP s.3.2.1) |
| Request for approval to **issue** invitation | Declaration of conflict of interest is a required item (AP s.3.3.3(f)) |
| Request for approval to **accept** a Proposal | Declaration of conflict of interests is a required item (AP s.3.7(g)) |
| Spec writing and evaluation | Declare under SPR 185–186; observe CSB Circular 2/2004 (AP s.8.3) |

Classification of Proposal correspondence from receipt/opening until a decision: **RESTRICTED** (AP s.8.1).

The booklet does not reproduce SPR 185–186. For the SPR text, use the current Stores and Procurement Regulations — this wiki does not wikilink into the SPR vault.
