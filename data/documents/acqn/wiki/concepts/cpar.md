# Contractor Performance Appraisal Report

A **CPAR** under GITP is optional and, when used, **adverse only**. An adverse CPAR is a CPAR rated **“Not satisfactory”** (AP s.7.1). Otherwise B/Ds are **not required** to submit CPARs. Form: Annex 14 (not transcribed; [[annexes]]). Chapter: [[06-performance]].

## Procedure

- If deemed necessary, submit at **completion or termination** of each individual contract.
- Indicate the unsatisfactory area; add supplementary information/justification where applicable.
- Forward the completed CPAR to the [[gitp-providers|GITP Provider]] for **acknowledgement and signing**.
- The provider may clarify and/or **disagree**. Disagreement may be escalated through the [[gitp-support-team]].

The booklet does not describe a satisfactory-rating CPAR, a mid-contract CPAR, or a link from CPAR to debarment (debarment in Note 5 is a **related implementation contract** provision, not this form).
