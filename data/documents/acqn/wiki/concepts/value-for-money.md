# Value for money

AP s.8.4 is a short operational instruction, not a restatement of SPR 107. When ordering against GITP, B/Ds should:

1. **aggregate**, as far as possible, departmental requirements for hardware, software, and services in order to obtain **bulk purchase discount** — purchases in small amount only in urgent cases; and
2. remember that the Government **does not confer any exclusive right** on [[gitp-providers]] to supply any hardware, software, or services.

That pairs with **SPR 205**, quoted at AP s.3.3.1: do not split similar requirements or shorten contracts to stay under the [[quotation-limit]]; Controlling Officers may be personally responsible. Urgent ≤ $5,000 direct buys (AP s.3.10) are the documented small-amount exception, and they **leave the scheme**.

Evaluation is allowed to look beyond one-off price: the [[brief]] may use **5-year cost** or a **marking scheme** under SPR 221A (AP s.3.2.4). Default award remains lowest conforming / highest combined score (AP s.3.6). See [[compare-evaluation-bases]].
