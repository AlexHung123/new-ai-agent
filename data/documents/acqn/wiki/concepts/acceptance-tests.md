# Acceptance tests

Hardware and software bought under GITP must pass **Installation and Function Tests** and **Reliability Tests** before acceptance (AP s.4.1). Related services are inspected. The **Acceptance Certificate** (Annex 9) is the payment trigger for products ([[04-payment]]). Chapter: [[03-acceptance]].

## Sequence

| Step | Who / duration | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Installation and Function Tests | Contractor runs; B/D authorised user observes | Annex 10 report, signed by user **and** Contractor |
| Reliability Tests | **Four consecutive weeks** against Schedule 8 Reliability Levels | Fault Record Annex 11 (immediate to Contractor); Summary Report Annex 12 on success |
| Fail reliability | Re-test another **four weeks** | — |
| Still failing **six weeks** after first submission to reliability tests | B/D may use CoC **Clauses 11.1 and/or 11.2** (including refrain from issuing further orders) | — |
| Pass | Issue Acceptance Certificate | Annex 9 |

Default calendar from written order (unless the [[brief]] says otherwise): installation/function inside **6 weeks**; reliability and completion inside **12 weeks**; at least 4 weeks reserved for reliability (AP s.3.2.2).

## Silence is a pass

If B/Ds **do not report any problems** during the reliability test period, the Contractor would assume the hardware/software has passed and is accepted (AP s.4.3). That is why fault records must go to the Contractor **immediately**.

## Alongside tests

- **Certificate of Product Patching** at acceptance: latest patches applied; list omissions with reasons (AP s.4.4; Annex 13).
- **Regulated electrical equipment**: EPD recycle label per unit; receipt with recycling-levy wording on or before invoice (AP s.4.5).

[[warranty]] starts from the **Acceptance Date** (AP s.6.1).
