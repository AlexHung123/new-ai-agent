# Brief

The **Brief** is the “requirement specifications document” B/Ds issue to invite Proposals from [[gitp-providers]] in the relevant sub-categories, **via [[e-ps]] only** (AP s.3.1). It is the evaluation constitution: if a criterion is not in the Brief, it is not used. Chapter: [[02-acquisition-brief]].

## Templates

- **Annex 2a** — Outline of Brief, evaluation on **overall** basis
- **Annex 2b** — Outline of Brief, evaluation **by group or item** basis (rev. 1.3, Feb 2024)

B/Ds may amend as deemed necessary. Specimen bodies are not in this conversion ([[annexes]]). Sample completed Briefs: ITG InfoStation `content/gitp/`.

## Scope discipline

Services in the Brief should only relate to set-up, configuration, and day-to-day operation of GITP IT products. Custom program development/maintenance and on-site ongoing office/network support are **out of scope** (AP s.3.2.1). Reference lists and reference specs may be tailored **inside the sub-category**; providers may offer items **not** on the reference list.

The Brief itself is an attachment to the request for approval to issue (AP s.3.3.3(d)).
