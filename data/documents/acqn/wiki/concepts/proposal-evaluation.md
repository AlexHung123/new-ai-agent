# Proposal evaluation

Evaluation follows the assessment criteria **already specified in the [[brief]]** (AP s.3.5). Technical first, then price. Default award: **lowest conforming offer**, or **highest combined score** if a marking scheme is used (AP s.3.6). Chapter: [[02-acquisition-evaluation]]. Bases compared: [[compare-evaluation-bases]].

## Technical

Preferably by staff with IT knowledge; optional panel with end-user plus [[itmu]] / Client IT Services Manager when needed or when value is high (AP s.3.5). Two tests (AP s.3.5.1):

1. written commitment on compliance; and
2. proposal details meet the Brief’s pre-defined technical/service requirements.

Written technical clarifications as necessary. **SPR 365** applies to clarification and missing information.

**Mandatory conformity at opening.** Only Proposals that conform fully to stipulated **mandatory** technical/service requirements **at proposal opening** can be considered. Overall basis: reject partial/incomplete Proposals. Group basis: reject partial/incomplete Proposals **within that group**.

## Price

Use **Estimated Contract Price** as defined in Clause 1 of the [[conditions-of-contract]] (AP s.3.5.2). No change to quoted price during evaluation except **arithmetical errors**. If trade-in is in the Brief, the quoted trade-in price is taken into account (AP s.3.2.5).

Price methodology must have been stated in the Brief: e.g. **5-year cost** (one-off plus maintenance / periodic licence for the four years after the one-year free [[warranty]]) or **one-off cost** (AP s.3.2.4(c)).

## Marking scheme

Optional. B/Ds may adopt a marking scheme for GITP procurements **in accordance with SPR 221A** (AP s.3.2.4(a)). If used, the default recommendation is the conforming offer with the highest combined score (AP s.3.6).

## Negotiation and non-default award

Negotiation for better terms: **SPR 385** and **Appendix III(J)** if considered necessary (AP s.3.6). A non-default recommendation (not lowest conforming / not highest combined score) needs full justifications and the **higher** acceptance rank (AP s.3.7; [[compare-approving-authorities]]).
