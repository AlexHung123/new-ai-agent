# Compare — evaluation bases

Three forks must be written into the [[brief]] **before** invitation (AP s.3.2.4): completeness basis, scoring method, and which money figure is compared. Changing them after opening is not provided for. Concept: [[proposal-evaluation]]. Templates: Annex 2a (overall) and Annex 2b (group or item) — [[annexes]].

## Completeness: overall / group / item

| | **Overall** | **Group** | **Itemised** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Outline** | Annex 2a | Annex 2b | Annex 2b |
| **Incomplete offer** | Partial/incomplete Proposals **must not** be considered (AP s.3.5.1) | Partial/incomplete Proposals **within a group** are rejected | Booklet does not spell a separate completeness rule; specify it in the Brief |
| **Use when** | One package, one winner | Distinct groups that can be awarded separately | Line-by-line comparison / award |

Mandatory technical/service requirements still apply at **proposal opening** on every basis (AP s.3.5.1).

## Scoring: price-only vs marking scheme

| | **Total offer price only** | **Marking scheme** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Authority** | Default unless the Brief says otherwise | Permitted **in accordance with SPR 221A** (AP s.3.2.4(a)) |
| **Default recommendation** (AP s.3.6) | **Lowest conforming** offer | Conforming offer with **highest combined score** |
| **Non-default** | Higher acceptance rank + reasons for rejecting the default offer (AP s.3.7) | Same |

Technical evaluation is not skipped on a price-only path: non-conforming mandatory requirements are out before price (AP s.3.5.1). Price uses **Estimated Contract Price** (CoC Clause 1); no mid-evaluation price change except arithmetical errors (AP s.3.5.2).

## Which cost is compared (AP s.3.2.4(c))

| Method | What is compared |
| --- | --- |
| **One-off cost** | The one-off price as quoted |
| **5-year cost** | One-off **plus** maintenance charges / periodic licence fee for the **4-year period after** the one-year free [[warranty]] |
| **Other** | Any other assessment criteria stated in the Brief |

[[trade-in]] prices, if the Brief asked for them, feed the price evaluation (AP s.3.2.5). Negotiation after ranking: SPR 385 and Appendix III(J) (AP s.3.6).
