# Compare — approving authorities

Two rank tables. **Issue** of invitation: AP s.3.3.2. **Acceptance** of a Proposal: AP s.3.7. Ranks are “not lower than”. Value bands below $50,000 follow SPR 260(a); the others are **percentages of the prevailing [[quotation-limit]]** and therefore move when SPR 220(a) moves (AP s.3.11). Chapter: [[02-acquisition-invitation]], [[02-acquisition-evaluation]].

The right-hand column is the price of **restricted competition** (not all relevant [[gitp-providers]] invited — Note 5 debarment is the worked example). On **acceptance**, that same higher column also applies when the recommendation is **not** the lowest conforming offer (no marking scheme) or **not** the highest combined score (marking scheme), with full justifications.

## Issue of invitation vs acceptance of Proposal

| Estimated / actual value | **Issue — all invited** | **Issue — not all invited** | **Accept — all invited, default offer** | **Accept — not all invited, or non-default offer** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ≤ **$50,000** (SPR 260(a)) | SS II or equivalent | Same | **ASO / EO II** or equivalent | Same |
| ≤ **20%** of quotation limit | ASO / EO II or equivalent | SO / EO I or equivalent | SO / EO I or equivalent | SSO / SEO or equivalent |
| ≤ **50%** of quotation limit | SO / EO I or equivalent | SSO / SEO or equivalent | SSO / SEO or equivalent | CSO / CEO or equivalent |
| ≤ **prevailing quotation limit** | SSO / SEO or equivalent | CSO / CEO or equivalent | CSO **or** CEO or equivalent | **D1** or equivalent |
| **Over** quotation limit | Not GITP — open tender, SPR Chapter III (AP s.3.3.1) | — | — | — |

Acceptance at ≤ $50,000 is one rank **above** invitation (ASO/EO II vs SS II). At the quotation ceiling, invitation (all invited) is SSO/SEO; acceptance is CSO/CEO; a restricted or non-default acceptance is **D1**.

## Pack contents (minimum)

Invitation approval (AP s.3.3.3): estimated value; sub-category; all-invited confirmation or names/reasons; the [[brief]]; SPR 350(b) confirmation; [[conflict-of-interest]] declaration; funds. Form: Annex 5.

Acceptance approval (AP s.3.7): price summary; recommended offer; Brief compliance; reasons if not default; last-purchase comparison if available; funds; COI declaration. Form: Annex 6.

Coordinating Officer for the booklet as a whole: not lower than **ASO / EO II** (AP s.8.1) — a compliance role, not a substitute for the tables above.
