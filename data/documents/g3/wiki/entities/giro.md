# Government Information Security Incident Response Office

The Government Information Security Incident Response Office (GIRO) provides central co-ordination and support to departmental [[isirt|ISIRTs]]. Each B/D shall establish an ISIRT; GIRO is the government-wide counterpart (G3 s.5.1.3). The **GIRO Standing Office** is GIRO’s executive arm. B/Ds may seek its advice on suspected system abnormalities for early detection across the Government (G3 s.18.1(b)). See [[incident-handling]] and [[compare-roles]].

## Functions (G3 s.5.1.3)

- maintain a central inventory and oversee handling of all information security incidents in the Government;
- prepare periodic statistics reports on government information security incidents;
- act as the central office to co-ordinate handling of **multiple-point** security attacks (simultaneous attacks on different government information systems);
- enable experience sharing and information exchange on incident handling among ISIRTs of different B/Ds.

[[ditso|DITSO]] oversees dissemination of security alerts on impending and actual threats **from GIRO** to responsible parties and project teams inside the B/D (G3 s.5.2.1). The [[isirt|ISIRT Commander]] collaborates with GIRO in reporting incidents for central recording and follow-up (G3 s.5.2.4).

## Membership

Core members are representatives from [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]], [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]], and Hong Kong Police Force (G3 s.5.1.3). [[govcert|GovCERT.HK]] collaborates with the Standing Office on co-ordinating information and cyber security incidents (G3 s.5.1.4).

## Clocks that land here

GIRO is the **receiving** end of the departmental reporting clocks. Detail lives on [[incident-handling]]. Headlines from G3 s.18.1(b):

- phone the Standing Office within **60 minutes** of becoming aware of an information security incident, and file the Preliminary Information Security Incident Reporting Form within **48 hours**;
- share scope, containment, line-to-take, and media enquiries when the incident hits critical e-government services, has significant security implications, or may attract media attention; daily recovery updates until those services resume;
- notify the Standing Office of any incident reported to HKPF, PCPD, or issued to media organisations;
- post-incident report no later than **one week** after resolution; if investigation runs longer, first interim at **14 days** then every **three months** until close.

Only the ISIRT Commander may share, or authorise sharing of, incident information (G3 s.18.1(b)).
