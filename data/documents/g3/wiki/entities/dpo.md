# Digital Policy Office

The Digital Policy Office (DPO) issues the Government IT Security Policy and Guidelines: Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] as the mandatory minimum, and *IT Security Guidelines* [G3] as the implementation standard B/Ds shall comply with (G3 s.2.3.2). Practice guides that sit under G3 (Internet gateway, risk management, threat management, SRA and audit, incident handling, security by design, cloud, email, media destruction, mobile, data centre) are published on the ITG InfoStation IT Security Theme Page. DPO produces and maintains G3 itself (G3 s.21). Version 10.1 (July 2024) renamed the issuer from the Office of the Government Chief Information Officer (OGCIO) to DPO. Treat DPO as the current issuer; do not treat OGCIO as a live source of S17 or G3. G3 still cites *OGCIO Circular No. 5/2023* by that name for extra pre-rollout tests on selected large-scale public-facing systems (G3 s.16.1(f)).

See [[compare-documents]], [[ismc]], and [[01-organisation]].

## Place in the Information Security Management Framework

DPO is a core member of [[ismc|ISMC]], [[itswg|ITSWG]], and [[giro|GIRO]] (G3 ss.5.1.1–5.1.3). On ISMC, DPO assists in reviewing and clarifying documents that B/Ds submit under G3 (G3 s.5.1.1). [[govcert|GovCERT.HK]] collaborates with the GIRO Standing Office on government cyber incidents; GovCERT scanning and alerts are the operational channel B/Ds actually consume (G3 ss.5.1.4, 14.6(b), 14.7(b)). Security Bureau and DPO jointly train [[ditso|DITSOs]]; DITSOs shall attend (G3 s.5.2.1).

DPO also:

- technically endorses an isolated wired LAN that the Government Security Officer at [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]] has approved for transmitting information classified higher than CONFIDENTIAL (G3 s.15.2(a));
- designates government-wide IT security awareness activities and security drills that B/Ds shall join (G3 ss.9.1(c), 18.1(d));
- may be consulted on system-classification doubts (G3 Appendix B);
- receives General Circular No. 6/2024 full incident reports (with Director of Bureau endorsement) for record, monitoring, and technical advice (G3 s.18.1(b)).

## Appendix D monitoring

[[annex-compliance-mechanism|Appendix D]] is DPO’s compliance-monitoring and audit mechanism (G3 s.20.2(d)). B/Ds submit Tier 2 / Tier 3 lists and classification assessments (Heads of B/D or delegated directorate endorsement); B/Ds with Tier 3 systems submit the IT security management unit’s establishment; DITSOs furnish SRA, audit, and privacy-impact documentation on regular request; Tier 3 SRA and audit reports go to DPO within 30 days of completion. DPO also runs government-wide compliance audits and periodic security surveys.
