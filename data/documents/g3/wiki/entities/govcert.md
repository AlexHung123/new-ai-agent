# GovCERT.HK

The Government Computer Emergency Response Team Hong Kong (GovCERT.HK) was established in April 2015 (G3 s.5.1.4). It is one of the five parties in the Government Information Security Management Framework (G3 s.5.1). See [[giro]], [[vulnerability-management]], and [[compare-roles]].

## Functions (G3 s.5.1.4)

- disseminate security alerts on impending and actual threats to B/Ds;
- act as a **bridge** between the Hong Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre (HKCERT) and other computer security incident response teams (CSIRTs) in handling cyber security incidents;
- collaborate with the [[giro|GIRO]] Standing Office in co-ordinating information and cyber security incidents within the Government;
- collaborate with the CERT community on incident information, threat intelligence, and best-practice exchange to strengthen regional capability.

G3 does not describe GovCERT.HK as the issuer of S17 or G3; that is [[dpo|DPO]].

## Threat intelligence and scanning

GovCERT.HK is a named source of security news, alerts, reports, and other publications used in vulnerability identification (G3 s.14.6(a)) and threat-intelligence gathering (G3 s.14.7(b)). B/Ds shall follow recommendations in GovCERT.HK security alerts when mitigating vulnerabilities (G3 s.14.6(g)).

The **GovCERT.HK Technology Centre** provides vulnerability scanning facilities for B/Ds to scan their **Internet-facing websites**, with assistance from GovCERT.HK (G3 s.14.6(b)). Annual / pre-rollout / major-change scanning duties for Internet-facing systems remain on the B/D (G3 s.20.2(c); [[annex-classified-protection|Appendix C]] for Tier 2/3).

[[ditso|DITSO]] oversees threat detection, monitoring, and intelligence inside the B/D, including dissemination of GIRO alerts; GovCERT alerts are part of that feed (G3 ss.5.2.1, 14.7(b)).
