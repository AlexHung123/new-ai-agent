# Departmental Security Officer

The Head of B/D designates a Departmental Security Officer (DSO) to perform departmental security related duties (G3 s.5.2.3). The DSO acts as an executive to:

- discharge responsibilities for **all aspects of security** for the B/D;
- advise on the set-up and review of the security policy.

“All aspects of security” in this appointment is the departmental security post (physical and personnel security as well as the security-policy advice G3 names). IT-security programme leadership is the [[ditso|DITSO]]’s job unless the same officer holds both posts.

See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-organisation]].

## Relationship with DITSO

The DSO **may take on the role of the DITSO**. Alternatively, where someone else is appointed DITSO, the DITSO shall collaborate with the DSO to oversee the B/D’s IT security (G3 s.5.2.3).

## Need-to-know and classified access

Classified information is provided only to persons who need it for the efficient discharge of their work and who have authorised access (the “need to know” principle). If there is any doubt whether an officer has authorised access to a particular document, classification, or information, the **Departmental Security Officer should be consulted** (G3 s.9.1(d)). The same consult-DSO instruction appears in the sample end-user instructions (G3 Appendix A).

For problems handling storage media that contain classified information, advice from the Departmental Security Officer **or** the Government Security Officer should be sought (G3 s.10.3(a)).

## Government Security Officer is a different person

The **Government Security Officer** sits in [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]], not in the B/D. G3 gives that officer (with [[dpo|DPO]] technical endorsement) approval of the isolated wired LAN required to transmit information classified higher than CONFIDENTIAL, and approval of the information system used for email transmission of classified information (G3 ss.15.2(a)–(b)). Do not collapse GSO into DSO.
