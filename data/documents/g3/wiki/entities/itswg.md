# IT Security Working Group

The IT Security Working Group (ITSWG) is the **executive arm** of [[ismc|ISMC]] for promulgation and compliance monitoring of government IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines. It was established in May 2000 (G3 s.5.1.2). See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-organisation]].

## Mandate

ITSWG’s responsibilities are to (G3 s.5.1.2):

- co-ordinate activities that give B/Ds guidance and assistance in enforcing IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines;
- monitor compliance with Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] at B/Ds;
- define and review the IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines;
- promote IT security awareness within the Government.

ISMC provides that guidance to B/Ds *through* ITSWG (G3 s.5.1.1). Incident inventory and multi-point attack handling sit with [[giro|GIRO]], not ITSWG.

## Membership

Core members are representatives from:

- [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]]
- [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]]
- Hong Kong Police Force (HKPF)
- Chief Secretary for Administration’s Office (CSO)

Representatives from other B/Ds are co-opted on a need basis for specific subject matters (G3 s.5.1.2).
