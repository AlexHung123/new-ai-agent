# Security Bureau

Security Bureau (SB) **authorises Security Regulations (SR)**. SR provides directives on what documents, material, and information need to be classified and ensures they receive an adequate level of protection in the conduct of government business (G3 s.2.3.1). G3 elaborates Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] and cannot waive SR. See [[compare-documents]] and [[classified-information]].

## Place in the Information Security Management Framework

SB is a core member of [[ismc|ISMC]], [[itswg|ITSWG]], and [[giro|GIRO]] (G3 ss.5.1.1–5.1.3). ITSWG’s other core members are [[dpo|DPO]], Hong Kong Police Force, and the Chief Secretary for Administration’s Office; GIRO’s other core members are DPO and HKPF.

SB and DPO jointly provide training so designated [[ditso|DITSOs]] can carry out their duties; DITSOs shall attend that designated training (G3 s.5.2.1).

## Government Security Officer

The **Government Security Officer** (GSO) is a Security Bureau post, not a departmental one, and is **not** the [[dso|Departmental Security Officer]]. G3 gives the GSO these approvals:

- **Isolated wired LAN.** Information classified higher than CONFIDENTIAL shall be transmitted only when encrypted and inside an isolated wired LAN **approved by the Government Security Officer** with the technical endorsement of DPO. An isolated LAN is a LAN in a single controlled environment with no connection to any other network, including other government networks, the Internet, and remote access (G3 s.15.2(a)).
- **Classified email path.** Email transmission of classified information shall be transmitted only on an information system **approved by the Government Security Officer**. For internal CONFIDENTIAL mail, the designated family is CMS, CMSG, MCMS, and approved sub-systems of CMMP (G3 s.15.2(b)).

Advice from the DSO **or** the Government Security Officer should be sought if there are problems handling storage media that contain classified information (G3 s.10.3(a)).
