# Information Security Management Committee

The Information Security Management Committee (ISMC) is the central body that oversees IT security across the whole Government. It was established in April 2000 (G3 s.5.1.1). It is one of the five parties in the Government Information Security Management Framework, together with [[itswg|ITSWG]], [[giro|GIRO]], [[govcert|GovCERT.HK]], and Bureaux/Departments (G3 s.5.1). See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-organisation]].

## Mandate

ISMC meets on a regular basis to (G3 s.5.1.1):

- review and endorse changes to government IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines;
- define specific roles and responsibilities relating to IT security;
- provide guidance and assistance to B/Ds in enforcing those instruments **through** the [[itswg|IT Security Working Group]].

ISMC does not run incidents day to day (that is [[giro|GIRO]] / departmental [[isirt|ISIRT]]) and does not issue S17 or G3 itself (that is [[dpo|DPO]]). It is the oversight and endorsement layer.

## Membership

Core members are representatives from:

- [[dpo|Digital Policy Office]]
- [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]]

Representatives from other B/Ds are co-opted on a need basis for specific subject matters (G3 s.5.1.1). DPO assists ISMC by reviewing and clarifying documents B/Ds submit as required in G3.
