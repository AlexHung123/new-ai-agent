# Departmental IT Security Officer

Head of B/D shall appoint an officer at **D3 or above** from senior management as Departmental IT Security Officer (DITSO). If the B/D has no D3-or-above directorate officer, the **highest-rank directorate officer** shall assume the post so accountability for IT security is not diluted (G3 s.5.2.1). [[security-bureau|Security Bureau]] and [[dpo|DPO]] provide designated training; DITSOs shall attend. See [[compare-roles]] and [[01-organisation]].

The [[dso|DSO]] may also be the DITSO. If someone else is appointed, the DITSO shall collaborate with the DSO (G3 s.5.2.3). An IT security management unit reports to the DITSO and assists in discharging the duties (G3 s.5.2.5). If the B/D does not set up an information security steering committee, the DITSO should take on that committee’s responsibilities (G3 s.5.2.2). For B/Ds with [[classified-protection|Tier 3]] systems, Appendix C makes a steering committee with senior-management and DITSO participation a **shall**.

## Programme duties (G3 s.5.2.1)

The DITSO shall participate in overall steering of IT security, understand the B/D’s priorities and the importance of its systems and data, and know the security level that shall be achieved. Defined duties include but are not limited to:

- establish and maintain an information protection programme so staff can protect the information and systems they use;
- establish a security governance process to evaluate, direct, monitor, and communicate IT security activities;
- drive regular senior-management discussions on IT security to acquire support and resources;
- lead establishment, maintenance, and implementation of IT security policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines;
- oversee, monitor, review, and improve IT security management through every stage of IT operations;
- monitor and ensure compliance with government IT security requirements, including overseeing satisfactory completion of security audits;
- oversee overall IT security awareness and training programmes;
- co-ordinate with other B/Ds on IT security issues;
- oversee the overall IT risk management process, including satisfactory completion of [[security-risk-assessment|security risk assessments]] and privacy impact assessments, subsequent rectification, and response to the evolving risk landscape, regulatory change, technology, and system criticality;
- oversee threat detection, monitoring, and threat-intelligence activities, including dissemination of security alerts from [[giro|GIRO]] to responsible parties and project teams;
- initiate investigation and rectification after a security breach and co-ordinate required incident-report submissions to the Director of Bureau.

LAN/System Administrators implement controls according to procedures the DITSO establishes (G3 s.5.3.3). Risk monitoring is reported to the DITSO (G3 s.7.2(c)). Vulnerability tracking provides the DITSO regular updates (G3 s.14.6(a)). Appendix D: DITSOs oversee SRA, audit, and PIA completion and subsequent rectification, and submit documentation to DPO on regular request.

## General Circular No. 6/2024

If an IT security incident in a government IT system has, in the Director of Bureau’s view, embarrassed the Government or undermined the image of its monitoring role: the Project Person-In-Charge reports for a “Specified IT System”; the **DITSO** reports for all other B/D information systems. Initial report to the Director of Bureau within **two calendar days** of onset; full report in **seven calendar days**. The full report, with recommended follow-up and Director of Bureau endorsement, goes to DPO (G3 s.18.1(b)). Detail: [[incident-handling]].
