# Departmental Information Security Incident Response Team

Each B/D shall establish an Information Security Incident Response Team (ISIRT) to handle information security incidents occurring in the B/D (G3 s.5.1.3). The ISIRT is the **central focal point** for co-ordinating incident handling for all information systems in that B/D (G3 s.5.2.4). Establishing an ISIRT and a security incident response plan is also how G3 says B/Ds improve the effectiveness of departmental IT security policy (G3 s.8.1(a)). Government-wide co-ordination is [[giro|GIRO]]. Clocks and plan contents: [[incident-handling]]. Roles: [[compare-roles]].

## ISIRT Commander

The Head of B/D should designate an officer from **senior management** as ISIRT Commander. The Commander should have authority to appoint core team members. Commander responsibilities (G3 s.5.2.4):

- overall supervision and co-ordination of information security incident handling for all information systems in the B/D;
- decisions on critical matters: damage containment, system recovery, engagement of external parties and the extent of involvement, and service-resumption logistics after recovery;
- trigger the departmental [[disaster-recovery|disaster recovery]] procedure where appropriate, depending on business impact;
- management endorsement of resources for incident handling;
- management endorsement of the **line-to-take** for publicity on the incident;
- collaborate with [[giro|GIRO]] in reporting incidents for central recording and necessary follow-up;
- facilitate experience and information sharing **within the B/D** on incident handling.

## Who may share incident information

Information about incidents is disclosed only on a need-to-know basis, and **only the ISIRT Commander** has the authority to share, or authorise others to share, information about security incidents with others (G3 s.18.1(b)). Staff shall not disclose identities of individuals, B/Ds, or specific systems that have suffered computer crime or abuse, or the methods used to exploit vulnerabilities, except to those handling the incident, responsible for the security of those systems, or authorised investigators (G3 s.18.1(e)).

The Commander’s GIRO-facing clocks (60-minute phone, 48-hour form, post-incident and interim reports) are on [[incident-handling]]. Two 7x24 contact points are a B/D duty, not a Commander-only duty (G3 s.18.1(c)).
