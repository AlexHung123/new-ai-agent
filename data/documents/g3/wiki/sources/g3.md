# Source summary — IT Security Guidelines [G3]

Government-wide implementation standard. Title as printed: *IT Security Guidelines* [G3]. Issued by the **Digital Policy Office**. This compilation is **version 10.2 (April 2025)**. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_G3_EN_2090718479378567168.md` (~3,167 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

G3 is **not** a volume of the Government Regulations. It elaborates Baseline IT Security Policy [S17] and sits under Security Regulations (Volume 5). B/Ds shall comply with G3; they may customise measures without lowering the security level (G3 s.1). See [[00-introduction]] and [[compare-documents]].

## Force

G3 “elaborates on the policy requirements, sets out the implementation standards for the security requirements specified in the Baseline IT Security Policy, and provides implementation guidance” (G3 s.1). B/Ds shall adopt all mandatory requirements in the body for [[classified-protection|Tier 1]] systems and the more stringent controls in [[annex-classified-protection|Appendix C]] for Tier 2 and Tier 3. **Shall** is mandatory; **should** is best practice whenever possible; **may** is desirable (G3 s.4.2). Staff, vendors, contractors, and consultants who provide IT services to the Government are in the target audience (G3 s.2.2).

## Reading the conversion

- Repeated running headers and TOC dots are not new sections.
- Section 8 is printed as “IT SECURITY POLICIES” without the number “8.”
- The Tier 1 definition wraps as “Information A related set of hardware…” — reconstruct as “Tier 1 Information Systems”.
- Four figures are images: SDLC security-considerations chart (s.2.1); document-relationship diagram (s.2.3); government Information Security Management Framework (s.5.1); sample departmental organisation chart (s.5.2). Roles are taken from surrounding captions, not invented boxes.
- Footnote bodies for superscripts 1–10 are missing. Do not reconstruct them.
- HTML entities (`&#x27;`) and `<eq>` markers (password table, s.11.4(b)) are reconstructed as ordinary English.
- Lotus Notes mail is still listed as a contact path in s.21 alongside CMMP.
