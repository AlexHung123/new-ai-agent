# Incident handling

B/Ds shall ensure a consistent and effective approach to managing information security incidents (G3 s.18). An information security incident, for the reporting clock, is an event of which the B/D has a reasonable degree of certainty that it has caused harm to the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of government information systems or data assets, or has compromised their operations (G3 s.18.1(b)). Departmental focal point: [[isirt]]. Government-wide inventory: [[giro]]. Plan and drills: G3 s.18.1(c).

## Clocks to GIRO Standing Office (G3 s.18.1(b))

Upon becoming aware of an incident, the departmental [[isirt|ISIRT]] **shall**:

| Clock | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **60 minutes** | Report to the GIRO Standing Office **by phone** |
| **48 hours** | Submit a completed Preliminary Information Security Incident Reporting Form |
| Daily (until resume) | Update recovery status for affected **critical e-government services** |
| On availability | If critical e-government services, significant security implications, or possible media attention: type/scope/damage/impact; containment and rectification actions; line-to-take; media enquiries and suggested responses |
| When it happens | Notify GIRO Standing Office of any incident reported to HKPF, PCPD, or issued to media organisations |
| **1 week** after resolution | Post-incident report |
| If investigation runs longer | First **interim** no later than **14 days** after the incident was first reported; then progress every **three months** until closed |

B/Ds may seek Standing Office advice on suspected abnormalities for early detection (G3 s.18.1(b)). Only the **ISIRT Commander** may share, or authorise others to share, incident information.

## General Circular No. 6/2024 overlay

If an IT security incident in a government IT system has, in the Director of Bureau’s view, embarrassed the Government or undermined the image of its monitoring role:

- **Specified IT System:** Project Person-In-Charge (PPIC) reports;
- **all other B/D information systems:** [[ditso|DITSO]] reports.

**Initial** government IT security incident report to the Director of Bureau within **two calendar days** from onset; **full** report in **seven calendar days**. The full report, with recommended follow-up and Director of Bureau endorsement, shall be submitted to [[dpo|DPO]] for record, monitoring, and technical advice (G3 s.18.1(b)). ## Response plan, drills, 7x24 contacts (G3 s.18.1(c))

A security incident response plan **shall** be established and documented, covering: team structure and roles; reporting procedures of s.18.1(b); mitigating impact, preserving evidence, investigating cause and impact; recovery plan; communication with stakeholders and the public; post-incident review. Review **at least once every two years**, or on material change in the operating environment. The plan **shall** be regularly tested. B/Ds **shall conduct drills at least once every two years, preferably annually**; incident-response team members shall participate. B/Ds shall also participate in security drills designated by DPO (G3 s.18.1(d)). Upon DPO request, the incident-response plan of [[classified-protection|Tier 3]] systems shall be submitted for inspection (Appendix D).

B/Ds **shall appoint two 7x24 contact points** for emergency IT-security calls. The contacts shall be capable of handling incidents or relaying messages to responsible personnel in time.
