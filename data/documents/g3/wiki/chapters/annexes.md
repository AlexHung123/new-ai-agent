# Appendices A–D

Catalogue of the four appendices to G3 v10.2 (April 2025). This page is a hub only. Detail lives on the four annex pages.

Appendix C **adds** floors for [[classified-protection|Tier 2 / 3]]; it does not relax the body of G3. Appendix D is the [[dpo|DPO]] monitoring overlay invoked from G3 s.20.2(d).

## Catalogue

| Appendix | Title | One line | Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Sample IT Security End User Instructions | Customisable DOs/DON’Ts for staff; **not** a replacement for SR, S17 or G3 | [[annex-end-user]] |
| **B** | Guidance on Classification Assessment | Worksheet: any aspect **High** → overall High → **Tier 2**; Tier 2 tied to essential services → **Tier 3** | [[annex-classification]] |
| **C** | Classified Protection of IT Security for Information Systems | Extra **shalls** for Tier 2 and Tier 3 (Tier 3 inherits Tier 2) | [[annex-classified-protection]] |
| **D** | Information Security Compliance Monitoring and Audit Mechanism | Eight DPO duties (lists, reports, 30-day clocks, government-wide audits, surveys) | [[annex-compliance-mechanism]] |

Body pointers: end-user instructions are a departmental-policy product (G3 s.8 / s.9); system classification is G3 s.7.2(b) and s.16.1(a); classified-protection extras overlay ss.5, 7, 9, 11, 14, 16, 19 and 20; the compliance mechanism is G3 s.20.2(d).
