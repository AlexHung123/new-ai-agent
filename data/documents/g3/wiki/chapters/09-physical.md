# Physical and environmental security

G3 s.13: B/Ds shall prevent unauthorised physical access, damage, theft or compromise of assets, and interruption to office premises and information systems. System-tier extras remain on [[classified-protection]]. Government Security Officer advice is a [[security-bureau]] channel. Off-site mobile practice also appears under remote working on [[07-access-control-mobile]].

## Site preparation (G3 s.13.1(a))

Critical IT equipment is normally housed in a data centre or computer room. Site preparation **shall** cover: site selection and accommodation planning; power supply and electrical requirement; air conditioning and ventilation; fire protection, detection and suppression; water leakage and flood control; and physical entry control.

B/Ds should start from existing site-selection and preparation guidelines, including the **Practice Guide to Data Centre Design and Site Preparation** on ITG InfoStation.

B/Ds **shall comply** with the physical security requirements according to the [[classified-protection|classification of information system(s)]] housed in the data centre or computer room. (G3 footnote 5 on those requirements is not in this conversion.) If office premises cannot fulfil the physical security requirements, B/Ds **shall seek advice from the Government Security Officer (GSO)** on individual merits.

If a wireless communication network is to be set up on the site, a **site survey shall** be conducted to ensure proper area coverage and to determine appropriate placement of wireless devices.

## Fire fighting (G3 s.13.1(b))

A fire-fighting party should be organised in each operating shift with well-defined responsibilities. **Regular fire drills shall** be carried out. Operators who are not party members shall be taught how to operate the fire detection, prevention and suppression system and portable extinguishers.

Hazardous or combustible materials should be stored at a safe distance from the office. Bulk stationery should not be stored in the data centre or computer room; stock inside should not exceed a shift's consumption.

Hand-held extinguishers should be in strategic locations, tagged for inspection, and inspected **at least annually**. Smoke detectors should supplement suppression systems, located high and below ceiling tiles throughout the computer area and/or underneath the raised floor. Heat detectors may be installed below ceiling tiles and should produce audible alarms when triggered.

**Gas-based fire suppression systems are preferred.** Where water-based systems are used, **dry-pipe sprinkling systems are preferred** over ordinary wet sprinklers. All fire-suppression systems should be inspected and tested **annually**, and segmented so that a fire in one area will not activate all suppression systems.

## Physical access control (G3 s.13.1(c))

All access keys, cards, passwords, and similar for entry to information systems and networks **shall** be physically secured under well-defined, strictly enforced procedures. Staff should not enter a password in front of unauthorised personnel, and should return card keys or access devices on resignation or dismissal. Acknowledgement of passwords and receipt of magnetic card keys **shall** be confined to authorised personnel; password records **shall** be stored securely. Card keys or entrance passwords should not be divulged to unauthorised persons.

Staff **shall** ensure office security. Offices that can be reached directly from public areas should be locked whenever not in use, however short the period, to protect systems and information assets inside.

A list of authorised personnel for data centres, computer rooms, or other areas supporting critical activities **shall** be maintained, kept up to date, and reviewed periodically. If possible, ask the cleaning contractor to assign a designated worker and obtain that person's particulars. External maintenance work **shall** be monitored by the responsible staff.

**Visitors** (vendor support, maintenance, project teams, other external parties) **shall not** enter unless accompanied by authorised staff. People permitted to enter shall display identification so that intruders can be spotted. A **visitor access record shall** be kept for audit: name and organisation, signature, date, time of entry and departure, purpose of visit, and similar.

Protected and secured areas shall be identified by conspicuous warning notices. The passage between the data centre/computer room and the data-control office, if any, should not be publicly accessible. Those areas should be physically locked and periodically checked. Acceptable locks include bolting door locks, cipher locks, electronic door locks, and biometrics door locks.

B/Ds should consider **video cameras (CCTV)** covering the whole computer area that hosts critical/sensitive systems, with images recorded. Recordings should be **retained for at least a month** for possible playback. Intruder-detection systems should be considered for the same areas.

## Equipment (G3 s.13.2(a))

All information systems **shall** be placed in a secure environment **or attended by staff**. Regular inspection of equipment and communication facilities **shall** be performed. For IoT devices, security controls **shall** protect against loss, theft, and damage according to the classification of information stored, processed, or transmitted.

When taking IT equipment away from sites, implement proper controls. For mobile devices and removable media, B/Ds **shall** keep an authorised equipment list and periodically inventory it, and **shall** adopt a **check-in / check-out** process or inventory documentation to identify what has been taken away. Staff taking equipment off-site should not leave it unattended in public places, or should lock it up when not attended. Staff **shall** safeguard approved business possessions and shall not leave them unattended without proper security measures.

Whenever leaving the workplace, a **password-protected screen saver shall** be activated, or the logon session/connection **shall** be terminated. For a prolonged period of inactivity, the workstation **shall** be switched off.

Staff **shall** position the display of an information system on which [[classified-information|classified information]] can be viewed so that unauthorised persons cannot see it. A **privacy screen filter** should be considered to limit the viewing angle.
