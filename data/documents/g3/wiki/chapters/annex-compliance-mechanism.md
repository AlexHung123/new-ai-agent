# Appendix D — DPO monitoring and audit

G3 Appendix D is the government **Information Security Compliance Monitoring and Audit mechanism**, introduced to streamline monitoring and assessment of B/Ds’ information-security compliance. G3 s.20.2(d) requires B/Ds to follow it. Issuer and collector: [[dpo|DPO]]. Departmental executive: [[ditso|DITSO]]. Tiers: [[classified-protection]].

Eight duties:

1. **T3 ITSMU structure to DPO.** B/Ds with Tier 3 systems **shall** submit the establishment, reporting mechanism, and roles and responsibilities of their **IT security management unit** to DPO. DPO may request clarification. On any **material change**, notify DPO **within 30 days**.

2. **T2/T3 list and assessment to DPO.** B/Ds **shall** submit the list of their Tier 2 and Tier 3 information systems, and the **assessment details** of the system classification, **endorsed by the Head of B/D or an explicitly delegated directorate officer**, to DPO. Inform DPO **within 30 days** of any change to the submitted list, including a change in classification. DPO may require further information so the B/D’s assessment aligns with classified protection in G3 s.7.2(b).

3. **T3 incident-response plan on request.** Upon request, the incident-response plan of Tier 3 systems **shall** be submitted to DPO for inspection and review.

4. **SRA / audit / PIA documentation on regular request.** B/Ds **shall** furnish DPO with relevant documentation for record and monitoring of SRA, security audit, and PIA exercises. DITSOs **shall** oversee satisfactory completion and subsequent rectifications, and arrange to submit documentation, assessment results and rectification status to DPO **upon regular requests**.

5. **T3 SRA reports within 30 days.** Reports of security risk assessments for Tier 3 systems — including **risk registers**, vulnerability-scanning reports, penetration-testing reports, and **rectification plans** for vulnerabilities — **shall** be submitted to DPO **within 30 days after assessment completion** for inspection and review.

6. **T3 audit reports and rectification plans.** Security-audit reports for Tier 3 systems **shall** be submitted to DPO **within 30 days after audit completion**, and **rectification plans within 30 days after submission of the audit report** in case of non-compliance.

7. **Government-wide compliance audits.** B/Ds **shall participate** in government-wide IT security compliance audits conducted by DPO and accord **priority** to complete the audit within the agreed schedule. The exercise assesses compliance against government information-security requirements and follow-up from the previous compliance audit.

8. **DPO security surveys.** B/Ds **shall complete** security surveys conducted periodically by DPO. The surveys collect security-status information on plans, practices and actions, and reveal readiness in response to security policies, threats or other issues.
