# Appendix B — Tier 2 / Tier 3 assessment

G3 Appendix B is **guidance** for assessing the classification of information systems. B/Ds should conduct their own evaluation against it. When in doubt, consult [[dpo|DPO]]. System-tier concept: [[classified-protection]]. Overlay versus the G3 body: [[compare-tiers]]. Extra *shalls* after the assessment: [[annex-classified-protection]].

## Tier 2 information systems

Tier 2 systems are those **crucial to the operations of the Government or society**, whose failure or disruption would have a **serious impact** on government operations or might cause public turmoil and catastrophes.

Impact on the Government if the system fails or is disrupted is rated in three levels:

| Impact | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **High (H)** | Major loss / serious damage or prejudicial to the Government |
| **Medium (M)** | Medium loss / some damage to the Government |
| **Low (L)** | Minor loss / little damage to the Government |

Rate each **aspect**. If **one or more aspects** are **High**, treat the **overall** impact as **High**. A system with overall impact **High** should be considered **Tier 2**.

Also consider impact of disruption on **other interdependent** information systems.

## Aspects

| Aspect | Considerations if failure or disruption may… |
| --- | --- |
| **Defence / security** | Endanger lives or property; inability to maintain law and order; inability to carry out statutory duties in time, with confidence/correctness, or within planned resource constraints; damage or loss of physical/information assets (facilities, systems, networks, equipment, supplies, data, personal information, IP); relaxed security controls. |
| **Financial** | Direct or probable government financial loss: direct loss; loss of or delay in revenue; delay in review of fees and charges; extra expenditure to clean up or tidy data; delay in payment that incurs extra expenditure or compensation. |
| **Government image** | Affect Government reputation; affect public confidence. |
| **Services to users** | Severity if the service is unavailable or degraded: number of users; whether targets are the public, the B/D only, or other B/Ds; disruption of analysis and decision-making by senior officers. |
| **Others** | Any additional area applicable to the system. |
| **Interdependency** | Impact of service disruption on other interdependent information systems (remark under the table). |

The printed worksheet leaves the Impact Level (H/M/L) column blank for the B/D to fill, plus an overall-impact row.

## Tier 3 information systems

**Tier 3** systems are **Tier 2** systems that are **directly related to the provision of an essential service**, whose disruption or destruction may cause **serious harm** to the economy, people’s livelihood, public safety, etc.

Identify the B/D’s **essential services** first, then the Tier 2 systems directly related to those services. Essential services typically sit in sectors with significant societal impact: **aviation, banking and finance, broadcasting, communications, energy, healthcare, land transport, maritime, media, security and emergency services, water and sewerage**, etc. Judge criticality from service nature and impact on the functioning and security of society and the economy.
