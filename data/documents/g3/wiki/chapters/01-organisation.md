## Government Information Security Management Framework (G3 s.5.1)

To coordinate and promote IT security in the Government, an Information Security Management Framework of **five parties** has been established: [[ismc|ISMC]], [[itswg|ITSWG]], [[giro|GIRO]], [[govcert|GovCERT.HK]], and Bureaux/Departments. The diagram captioned “Government Information Security Management Framework” is an **image** in this conversion. Roles below are from the surrounding text, not reconstructed boxes.

### ISMC (G3 s.5.1.1)

The Information Security Management Committee was established in **April 2000** to oversee IT security across the Government. It meets regularly to: review and endorse changes to government IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines; define specific roles and responsibilities relating to IT security; and provide guidance and assistance to B/Ds in enforcement **through ITSWG**.

Core members: Digital Policy Office (DPO) and Security Bureau (SB). Representatives from other B/Ds are co-opted as needed. DPO assists in reviewing and clarifying documents B/Ds submit as required in G3.

### ITSWG (G3 s.5.1.2)

The IT Security Working Group, established in **May 2000**, is the **executive arm of ISMC** for promulgation and compliance monitoring. Responsibilities: coordinate guidance and assistance to B/Ds on enforcement; monitor compliance with Baseline IT Security Policy [S17]; define and review IT security related regulations, policies, and guidelines; promote IT security awareness in the Government.

Core members: DPO, SB, Hong Kong Police Force (HKPF), and Chief Secretary for Administration’s Office (CSO). Other B/Ds co-opted as needed.

### GIRO (G3 s.5.1.3)

Each B/D **shall** establish an Information Security Incident Response Team ([[isirt|ISIRT]]). The Government Information Security Incident Response Office provides **central coordination and support** to those ISIRTs. The GIRO Standing Office is GIRO’s executive arm.

Major functions: maintain a central inventory and oversee handling of all government information security incidents; prepare periodic statistics; coordinate handling of **multiple-point** security attacks (simultaneous attacks on different government systems); enable experience sharing among B/D ISIRTs.

Core members: DPO, SB, HKPF.

### GovCERT.HK (G3 s.5.1.4)

The Government Computer Emergency Response Team Hong Kong was established in **April 2015**. It collaborates with the GIRO Standing Office on information and cyber security incidents in the Government, and with the CERT community on incident information, threat intelligence, and best practices.

Major functions: disseminate security alerts on impending and actual threats to B/Ds; act as a bridge between the Hong Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre (HKCERT) and other computer security incident response teams (CSIRTs) in handling cyber security incidents.

### Bureaux/Departments (G3 s.5.1.5)

B/Ds **shall** be responsible for the security protection of their information assets and information systems. Departmental roles: G3 s.5.2.

## Departmental IT security organisation (G3 s.5.2)

For sufficient [[segregation-of-duties|segregation of duties]], **multiple roles should not be assigned to an individual** unless there is a resource limitation. ### DITSO (G3 s.5.2.1)

The Head of B/D **shall** appoint an officer at **D3 or above** from senior management as Departmental IT Security Officer ([[ditso|DITSO]]), responsible for IT security. The DITSO shall participate in overall steering, and shall understand the B/D’s priorities, the importance of its systems and data, and the security level that shall be achieved.

If the B/D has **no directorate officer at D3 or above**, the **highest-rank directorate officer** shall assume DITSO, to uphold accountability in IT security.

SB and DPO provide training; DITSOs **shall** attend the designated training. Duties **shall** be clearly defined and include (non-exhaustive): an information-protection programme for all staff; a security governance process (evaluate, direct, monitor, communicate); regular senior-management discussion of IT security and resources; lead policies, standards, procedures, and guidelines; oversee effectiveness through every stage of IT operations; monitor compliance including satisfactory completion of security audits; oversee awareness and training; coordinate with other B/Ds; oversee IT risk management (security risk assessments, privacy impact assessments, rectifications, evolving risk, regulation, technology, and system criticality); oversee threat detection, monitoring, and intelligence, including dissemination of [[giro|GIRO]] alerts; initiate investigation and rectification on breach and coordinate incident reports to the Director of Bureau.

### Information Security Steering Committee (G3 s.5.2.2)

Senior management **should** consider setting up an information security steering committee that **reports to DITSO**, or include information security as a regular item in management meetings. Committee duties: leadership in promoting and prioritising IT security; direct and enforce development of security measures; provide resources; foster orderly and secure functioning of all systems; ensure participation and accountability at all levels; foster a culture of awareness and accountability; align IT security strategies with business objectives.

If the B/D does **not** set up a steering committee, **DITSO should undertake those responsibilities**.

### DSO (G3 s.5.2.3)

The Head of B/D designates a Departmental Security Officer ([[dso|DSO]]) for departmental security duties. The DSO acts as executive to discharge responsibilities for **all aspects of security** for the B/D and to advise on set-up and review of the security policy.

The DSO **may** take on the role of DITSO. If someone else is appointed DITSO, DITSO **shall** collaborate with the DSO to oversee IT security.

### ISIRT Commander (G3 s.5.2.4)

The [[isirt|ISIRT]] is the central focal point for coordinating incident handling in the B/D. The Head of B/D **should** designate an officer from **senior management** as ISIRT Commander. The Commander should have authority to appoint core team members.

Duties include: overall supervision and coordination of incident handling for all systems; decisions on damage containment, recovery, engagement of external parties, and service resumption; trigger departmental disaster recovery where appropriate; endorse resources and the line-to-take for publicity; collaborate with [[giro|GIRO]] on reporting; facilitate experience sharing in the B/D.

### IT Security Management Unit (G3 s.5.2.5)

B/Ds **shall** establish an IT Security Management Unit that **reports to DITSO** and assists DITSO. Size and composition may vary with business and operational needs.

Duties include: assist on security governance and IT security management for all IT operations; develop, establish, and maintain the overall IT security strategy and roadmap (policies, baselines, standards, directives); coordinate awareness and training; coordinate implementation of initiatives and monitor processes for effectiveness and government-requirement compliance; facilitate threat and risk management and support DR/BCP functions relating to IT security; coordinate incident investigation and rectification; liaise with other B/Ds; other duties as directed by DITSO.
