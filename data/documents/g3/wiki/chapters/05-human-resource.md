# Human resource security

G3 s.9 covers new employment, during employment, and termination: communicating roles, dissemination, training, personnel security, disciplinary warning, and post-employment confidentiality. [[classified-information|Classified information]] handling and [[dso|DSO]] advice sit here; awareness programmes sit under the [[ditso|DITSO]] duties in G3 s.5.2.1.

## Employment cycle (G3 s.9 intro)

Positively cultivating a strong security culture is crucial for the B/D’s security posture, reducing risks, complying with regulations, and building a resilient, trusted environment across the Government. B/Ds **shall** ensure that staff engaged in government work are **suitable for the roles**, understand their responsibilities, and are aware of information security risks. B/Ds **shall** protect government interests in the process of **new, changing, or terminating** employment.

“Staff” in G3 includes public officers, non-government secondees, and term-contract personnel, who may have different accessibility to classified information and different vetting (G3 s.4.1(j); S17 s.9).

## IT security responsibilities (G3 s.9.1(a))

IT security roles and responsibilities **shall** be communicated to all staff when they are assigned a **new post**, and **periodically** throughout employment. B/Ds **shall** ensure that all staff:

- are informed of the departmental IT security policy by **briefing or orientation** when they newly join; and
- are aware of, and periodically reminded of, their IT security responsibilities and government security requirements.

## Information dissemination (G3 s.9.1(b))

An effective dissemination mechanism **shall** be in place so that all personnel involved are fully aware of the policies and procedures governing their authority and usage of information systems.

## Training (G3 s.9.1(c))

Proper security training and updates on IT security policy **shall** be provided to **all staff regularly**, including users, developers, system administrators, and security administrators engaged in government work, to strengthen awareness. Awareness training **may** be classroom, computer-based, or self-paced.

Users **should** be made aware of training resources on the Civil Service College **Cyber Learning Centre Plus (CLC Plus)**, which includes general IT security courseware and a self-assessment package. B/Ds **may** use those resources when providing tailor-made training and materials to staff or contractors. CLC Plus: https://www.clcplus.csc.gov.hk.

Staff **may** also raise awareness by participating in security drills, attending seminars or showcases, or visiting theme pages with security intelligence and general security information (e.g. Cyber Security Information Portal, InfoSec website). B/Ds **shall** participate in the IT security awareness activities **designated by DPO**.

Proper education and training **should** also be provided to system administrators in implementing IT security procedures. They **should** know how to protect their systems from attack and unauthorised use. System administrators **shall** have a defined procedure for reporting security problems.

B/Ds **should** consider formulating an IT security **training programme** for targeted, structured awareness. The programme **should** include, but is not limited to:

| Element | What G3 expects |
| --- | --- |
| (i) Programme objectives | Objectives aligned with the B/D’s overall IT security strategy |
| (ii) Target audience | Specific groups or roles required to participate; tailor content to technical expertise, job functions, and needs |
| (iii) Training approach | Materials aligned with objectives and audience. Example topics: phishing awareness, incident response, regulatory compliance, data privacy, social-media-platform awareness. Delivery methods having regard to size, risks, resources, and audience — presentations, videos, interactive modules, practical exercises, case studies |
| (iv) Evaluation of effectiveness | Review effectiveness. Assessment **may** use post-training quizzes, feedback surveys, simulation exercises, and observing behaviour change or the number of security incidents |
| (v) Regular review and update | Continuously review and update for the evolving threat landscape, new technologies, and changes in regulations and compliance. Use feedback to identify improvements |

Oversight of overall awareness and training programmes is a [[ditso|DITSO]] duty (G3 s.5.2.1).

## Personnel security (G3 s.9.1(d))

[[classified-information|Classified information]] **shall** be protected from unauthorised access or unauthorised disclosure. Officers **shall not** publish, make private copies of, or communicate to unauthorised persons any classified document or information obtained in their official capacity unless they are required to do so in the interest of the Government.

The **need-to-know** principle **shall** be applied to all classified information: it **shall** be provided only to persons who require it for the efficient discharge of their work **and** who have authorised access. If in any doubt as to whether an officer has authorised access to a particular document, classification, or information, the [[dso|Departmental Security Officer]] **should** be consulted.

B/Ds **shall** ensure that personnel security risks are effectively managed, and **shall** assess the risk of allowing an individual to access classified information.

**Access to classified information higher than RESTRICTED is restricted to civil servants who have undergone appropriate integrity checks.** B/Ds **should** consult the departmental personnel section about the Integrity Checking Instructions.

For staff **other than civil servants**, appropriate **background verification** checks **should** be carried out commensurate with business requirements, the classification of the information the staff will handle, and perceived risks. Having addressed personal privacy issues, checks **may** include:

- independent identity check (Hong Kong Identity Card or passport);
- confirmation of claimed academic and professional qualifications;
- completeness and accuracy check of the provided curriculum vitae;
- availability of employment references;
- more detailed checks, such as credit checks or checks of criminal records, if considered necessary.

## Clear policies and procedures (G3 s.9.1(e))

Management **shall** establish clear policies and supporting procedures on the use of information systems, setting out allowed and disallowed actions. These actions should normally be covered in the departmental IT Security Policy.

The departmental IT Security Policy **shall** include a provision advising staff that if they contravene any provision of the policy, they **may be subject to different levels of disciplinary or punitive actions** depending on the severity of the breach.

Staff **shall** be **formally notified** of their authorisation to access an information system and of their responsibilities and duties on those systems.

## After termination or change of employment (G3 s.9.1(f))

Post-employment responsibilities and duties **shall** be defined in the terms and conditions of employment. Communication of termination responsibilities **shall** include **continuing information security requirements and legal responsibilities**. It **should** also include responsibilities in any confidentiality agreement and the terms and conditions of employment that **continue for a defined period after the end of employment**.

A **change** of responsibility or employment **shall** be managed as termination of the current responsibility or employment followed by commencement of the new one. Return of assets on exit: [[06-assets]].
