# Source summary — A Guide to Preparing the Annual Departmental Training Plan

Booklet issued by the [[cstc|Civil Service Training Centre]] to help departments write an annual [[departmental-training-plan]]. It is **item 2** in a six-booklet series. Raw file: `MinerU_markdown_A_Guide_To_PrepareThe_Annual_Departmental_Training_Plan_2110565173541298176.md` (192 lines). This wiki does not edit that file.

The booklet is **guidance**, not a volume of Civil Service Regulations. It tells departments to produce a Plan before each financial year and to send copies to [[csb]] and CSTC.

## Force

Every department is **expected** to formulate a Training Plan for the coming financial year (Guide p.1 / s.1). The Development Unit of CSTC will advise on implementation (Guide s.0). Dates for standard courses are known well in advance even when other details are not (Guide s.2). The Plan as drawn up is **unlikely to be final** (Guide s.5).

Appendix 1’s worked example uses **August 1994**. No other issue date or revision table appears in the conversion.
