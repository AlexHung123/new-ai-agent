# Overview — Annual Departmental Training Plan wiki

This is the compiled wiki of CSTC’s booklet *A Guide to Preparing the Annual Departmental Training Plan* (series booklet 2). The raw file is `MinerU_markdown_A_Guide_To_PrepareThe_Annual_Departmental_Training_Plan_2110565173541298176.md` (English PDF conversion, 192 lines). You read the wiki; the agent writes it. For any question, start at [[index]], then open only the pages you need.

## One planning path

```
Corporate/business plan + appraisal + other sources
        → [[training-needs-analysis]]
        → match against resources and [[departmental-training-policy]]
        → write the [[departmental-training-plan]]
        → endorse ([[departmental-training-committee]] / HRM Advisory Team)
        → copy to [[csb]] and [[cstc]]
        → notify staff and supervisors
        → amend during the year ([[incremental-approach]])
```

The fork that decides what belongs in the Plan is **formal classroom training versus [[informal-development]]**. Agreed classroom needs go on the [[annex-training-agreement|Training Agreement]] and into the Plan. Non-classroom development is as important, and it stays out of the Plan because it is carried out individually. See [[compare-formal-vs-informal]].

## Who does what

| Actor | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [[cstc|Civil Service Training Centre]] | Issued the series; Development Unit advises; receives a copy of each Plan to allocate central courses |
| [[csb|Civil Service Branch]] | Receives a copy of each Plan for reference |
| [[training-section]] | Consolidates needs, matches them to resources and policy, writes the Plan |
| [[departmental-training-committee]] | Funding and priorities; endorses the Plan (or the HRM Advisory Team does) |
| Appraiser and appraisee | Agree type and timing of formal training for the next year |
| Operational head | Optional: vet appraisal forms and set priorities before they reach the Training Section |
| Personnel section | Number of intakes, so induction can be planned |
| [[branch-rmu]] | Optional endorsement; notified of amendments |

## Three structural facts

1. **The Plan is this year’s needs, not last year’s catalogue.** Previous activities are a starting point only. Needs may have changed considerably (Guide p.2 / s.3).
2. **The Plan is not the sum of demands.** Funds and manpower will not cover every stated need. The Training Section matches demands against capacity **and** departmental training policies (Guide p.3 / s.3.2).
3. **First year need not be perfect.** Separate sectional plans, missing posting decisions, and empty headings are expected. The booklet asks for good faith and a move toward a master Plan ([[incremental-approach]]).
