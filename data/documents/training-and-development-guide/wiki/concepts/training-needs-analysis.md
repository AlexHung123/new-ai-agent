# Training needs analysis

How a department establishes the **actual training needs** that the [[departmental-training-plan]] must be formulated against (Guide s.3). CSTC’s *A Guide to Training Needs Analysis* is named as the practical account. That booklet is **not in this compilation**. This page records only what booklet 2 itself lists. Chapter: [[02-establishing-needs]].

## Main areas in this guide

1. **Corporate / business strategies** — after the corporate plan exists, HRM specialists or the [[departmental-training-committee]] advise on competencies, experience, numbers, and timing. New activities in the strategy make this especially important.
2. **Outside consultation** — if in-house HRM or training expertise is missing, a formal **training needs survey**; [[cstc]] advice; or a management consultancy firm if CSTC cannot help and funds are available.
3. **[[performance-appraisal-training]]** — type and timing agreed at appraisal; standard form; optional operational-head vetting.

Further influences that may be recorded in the Plan (Guide s.4 item 3): grade management, client feedback, unions, service-wide requirements, wastage-based manpower projections. See [[compare-needs-sources]].

A survey the [[training-section]] still intends to run belongs in the Plan with timing, scale, purpose, and any technical assistance (Guide s.4 item 8).
