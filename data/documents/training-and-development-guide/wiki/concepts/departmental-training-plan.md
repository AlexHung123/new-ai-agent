# Departmental Training Plan

A practical, working document that details the **full scope** of training activities the department will organise and conduct in the coming year, in-house or with outside resources (Guide s.1). Every department is expected to produce one **before the start of the financial year**. Chapter: [[01-what-and-why]].

## What it is not

- Not last year’s activity list. Previous activities are a starting point only (Guide s.3).
- Not the sum of training demands from appraisals and surveys. The [[training-section]] matches demands to capacity, resources, and [[departmental-training-policy]] (Guide s.3.2).
- Not a record of individual [[informal-development]]. Non-classroom learning is as important and stays out of the Plan (Guide s.3.1).

## What it must still cover

Eight common areas in nearly every case: policies, goals, sources, optional endorsement, [[classroom-training]], training communications, other training-related activities, planned surveys. See [[04-contents]].

## Circulation

Before the year: copy to [[csb]] and [[cstc]] (Guide s.1). After endorsement by the [[departmental-training-committee]] or HRM Advisory Team: one copy to CSB for reference, one to CSTC for central-course planning; notify participants and superiors; report amendments (Guide s.5).

The Plan as drawn up is unlikely to be final. First-year Plans may be incomplete: [[incremental-approach]].
