# Classroom activities

All the courses the [[training-section]] intends to **organise**, or have staff **enrolled into**, for the coming year (Guide s.4 item 5). This is the core of what the [[departmental-training-plan]] documents. Individual non-classroom learning is out: [[informal-development]].

## Per-course fields (if available at preparation)

Subject/title; objective in line with departmental goals; grade/rank of trainees; number of trainees; training institute if not in-house; timing; cost; number of trainee-days achieved.

## Breakdowns and totals

Course lists should be broken down into local or overseas, and into professional/technical, management/supervisory, language/communication, and IT/PC. See [[compare-course-categories]].

Overall statistics may include total trainee days, total number of trainees attending courses, and total costs.

Appendix 1 type codes (V / M / L / PC) are a related but shorter set used on the [[annex-training-agreement]] at appraisal, not a substitute for the Plan’s classroom breakdown.
