# Performance appraisal and training needs

Training needs are often established through performance appraisal, when the **type and timing** of training the appraisee should undertake in the following year is agreed (Guide s.3.1). This is one of three main areas for [[training-needs-analysis]]. Chapter: [[02-establishing-needs]].

## Course list for the interview

The [[training-section]] should supply **both** appraisers and appraisees with a list of:

- courses run by the department in the past
- [[cstc]] courses
- SSCC courses (not expanded in this file)
- other courses available to the department

## Two follow-up routes

After the interview, agreed training is captured on a **standard form** (sample: [[annex-training-agreement]]).

1. The Training Section notes the requirements and consolidates them into overall departmental needs for the coming year.
2. Alternatively, completed forms are first vetted by the **operational head**, who considers the needs, determines priorities, and submits views to the Training Section.

Those agreed, entered needs are the **formal** aspect of training. Non-classroom development is the informal aspect and does not go into the [[departmental-training-plan]]. See [[compare-formal-vs-informal]].

Whether [[departmental-training-committee]] recommendations then take precedence over supervisor–subordinate agreements is a [[departmental-training-policy]] choice (Guide s.3.2).
