# Individual training entitlement

The number of days each year which an individual is entitled to spend on training or training-related activities (Guide s.3.2). It is a parameter of [[departmental-training-policy]]. It may differ between grades and ranks, and will also differ between departments. Comparison: [[compare-entitlement]].

## CSTC view (floors)

In general:

| Officers | Entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| MPS Point **16 or below** | at least **2 days** training each year |
| MPS Point **17 or above** | at least **4 days** training a year |

This is printed as **the CSTC view**, not as a Civil Service Regulation in this booklet.

## Ceilings

Departments should also set ceilings for **maximum** entitlement. The booklet does not prescribe the numbers.

Entitlement is one of the filters the [[training-section]] uses when matching stated needs to the [[departmental-training-plan]]. It will not cover every demand from appraisals and surveys.
