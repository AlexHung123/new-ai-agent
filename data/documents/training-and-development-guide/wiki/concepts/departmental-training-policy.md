# Departmental training policies

Guidelines on how the training function of a particular department should be managed (Guide s.3.2). They are a filter on stated needs: the [[departmental-training-plan]] is not the sum of demands. The Plan should also **record** the civil service and/or departmental policies that guided it (Guide s.4 item 1).

CSTC’s *Formulating Departmental Training Policies and Strategies* is named as the description of the parameters. That booklet is **not in this compilation**. The series list titles it *A guide to Formulating Departmental Training Policies*.

## Parameters this guide calls particularly important

1. [[training-entitlement]] — days per year; may differ by grade, rank, department; CSTC 2-day / 4-day view; departmental maximum ceilings.
2. Training **priorities** for induction, professional/technical, management, language, communication, and information technology (including PCs).
3. Whether recommendations of the [[departmental-training-committee]] take precedence over needs agreed between supervisors and subordinates.
4. The **proportion of departmental expenses** to be spent on training and development.

Priority categories overlap the classroom split used when listing courses: [[compare-course-categories]].
