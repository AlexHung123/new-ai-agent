# Informal development

Non-classroom learning and development activities agreed around appraisal. This guide calls them the **informal aspect** of training. They are **as important** as the formal (classroom) aspect. They **do not feature** in the [[departmental-training-plan]] because they are carried out **individually** (Guide s.3.1). Comparison: [[compare-formal-vs-informal]].

The conversion says “the informal aspect consulting of non-classroom learning”; read as **consisting of**.

## What still goes in the Plan

Guide s.4 item 7 lists **arrangements for the informal sharing of information** among staff after some have attended a course, as an *other training-related activity*. That is organised departmental effort, so it is in the Plan. Individual non-classroom development remains out.

Secondment, guest speakers, and purchase of training equipment are also “other” activities in the Plan. They are not the informal-aspect exclusion in s.3.1.

Formal classroom needs sit on the [[annex-training-agreement]] and in [[classroom-training]].
