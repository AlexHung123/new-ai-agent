# Introduction — series and purpose

Guide s.0 is the front of the booklet: which series this is, who should read it, and who to ask. The Plan itself is defined at [[01-what-and-why]]. The source summary is [[adtp]].

## Series

[[cstc|Civil Service Training Centre]] issued a series of booklets to help departments systematize and enhance training and development. Each booklet looks at one major area. It gives conceptual analysis and practical guidelines.

| No. | Title in this file |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A guide to Formulating Departmental Training Policies |
| 2 | **A guide to Preparing the Annual Departmental Training Plan** (this booklet) |
| 3 | A guide to Induction Training |
| 4 | A guide to Training Evaluation |
| 5 | A guide to Training Needs Analysis |
| 6 | A guide to Roles and Functions of Training Personnel in Departments |

Items 1 and 3–6 are **not in this compilation**. The body later cites item 5 by name, and cites item 1 as *Formulating Departmental Training Policies and Strategies* (wording differs from the list). See [[training-needs-analysis]] and [[departmental-training-policy]].

## Audience

All staff dealing with training and development matters are encouraged to read the series and refer to it regularly. Staff with managerial responsibilities would also find the booklets useful.

## Purpose of this booklet

Every department is expected to formulate a [[departmental-training-plan]] to meet its projected needs over each financial year. This guide is to help with that process.

## Advice

Departments may approach the **Development Unit** of CSTC for more advice on implementing the recommendations. The same unit will be pleased to help with queries of any kind.
