# Follow-up action and an incremental approach

Guide p.7 / s.5–s.6. After the Plan is written: copies, notification, amendments. The booklet then tells departments not to wait for a perfect first Plan. Concept: [[incremental-approach]].

## Follow-up (s.5)

Once the Plan has been endorsed by the department’s [[departmental-training-committee|Training Committee]] or Human Resources Management Advisory Team:

- one copy to [[csb|Civil Service Branch]] for reference
- another copy to [[cstc|Civil Service Training Centre]] to assist in planning its **central courses**

Staff who will participate in the activities outlined in the Plan, **and their superiors**, should be notified — even if exact timings have not yet been established.

The Training Plan as drawn up is **unlikely to be final**. It may not be implemented in full. A number of amendments are to be expected. Periodically, such changes should be notified to the Training Committee and/or the [[branch-rmu|Branch Resource Management Unit]].

## An incremental approach (s.6)

Most departments are not yet experienced in producing a Training Plan in the manner outlined. It should not prove too difficult if the initial steps in the training process are already being followed — for example, if a Training Committee has already been formed. Departments at present practising only a **rudimentary** training function may have a lot of catching up to do.

During the **first year**, not every Training Plan can be expected to achieve perfection.

- Some information required for formulation may not be available at the start of the year — for example, **posting decisions** which will definitely affect training may not have been finalised.
- Some departments may have little to say under some of the topics in [[04-contents]].

Once formulating a yearly Training Plan becomes a natural part of a department’s process, it is a matter of time before such forward planning becomes complete and sophisticated.

Several training sections in one department may file **separate** plans in year one or two, then move toward a master Plan ([[03-matching]]).

## Purpose and stance

The purpose is to help departments plan and organise their training programmes much more systematically, to the mutual benefit of department and staff. The project should be approached **in good faith**, with care and thought for the future.
