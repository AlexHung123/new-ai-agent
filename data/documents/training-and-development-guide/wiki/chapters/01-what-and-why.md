# What the Plan is and why it is needed

Guide pp.1 / s.1–s.2. Definition of the [[departmental-training-plan]] and the four reasons every department is expected to produce one. How to write it starts at [[02-establishing-needs]].

## What it is (s.1)

The Departmental Training Plan is a **practical, working document**. It details the **full scope** of the training activities the department will organise and conduct in the coming year.

Those activities may be undertaken:

- through the department’s own resources; or
- with the help of outside training and development resources.

Every department is expected to produce the Plan **before the start of the financial year**. A copy should be sent to both the [[csb|Civil Service Branch]] and the [[cstc|Civil Service Training Centre]] for reference. Follow-up copies after endorsement: [[05-follow-up]].

## Why it is needed (s.2)

| Reason | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Forward planning | The department thinks and plans ahead, and conducts training in an organised manner |
| Year-end assessment | The Plan is a basis on which to assess the department’s training activities at the end of the year |
| Release management | Supervisors/managers anticipate releases due to course attendances and organise work schedules. Not all details may be available when the Plan is formulated; dates for **standard courses** are known well in advance |
| Central allocation | The system helps CSTC allocate **its own** training resources in the most effective manner |

The same document therefore serves the department and the centre.
