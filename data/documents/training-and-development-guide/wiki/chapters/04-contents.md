# What the Training Plan covers

Guide p.5 / s.4. The Plan should cover **everything** on which the department intends to spend effort and resources during the next year on staff training and development. Content varies with policies and needs. These eight areas should be covered in nearly every case.

## 1. Departmental training policies

The relevant civil service and/or departmental training policies that guided formulation of the Plan. The most important of those are already outlined at [[03-matching]] and [[departmental-training-policy]].

## 2. Departmental goals

Outlining departmental objectives helps explain why certain training activities are required and the priorities they receive. Examples in the booklet:

- a department about to start operating on **training fund status** (as printed; `(source unclear; verify against printed CSTC booklet)` — may be trading fund status)
- helping realise an office automation plan
- dealing with changes in legislation affecting the department’s work

All of these may require staff to acquire extra skills.

## 3. Sources

Sources for establishing training needs may be included. Besides those in [[02-establishing-needs]], these factors may have an influence:

| | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | Grade management’s opinions |
| (b) | Feedback from clients |
| (c) | Unions’ views |
| (d) | Service-wide requirements, e.g. customer service training |
| (e) | Manpower projections based on wastage patterns |

The conversion labels the last two items both **(d)**. Reconstruction as (d) and (e) is documented in [[adtp]]. Full map: [[compare-needs-sources]].

## 4. Endorsement

Optional. If the Plan has been endorsed by senior management, the [[departmental-training-committee]], or the [[branch-rmu|Branch’s Resource Management Unit]], it may be useful to make this known.

## 5. Classroom activities

All courses the [[training-section]] intends to organise, or have staff enrolled into, should be documented. Details, **if available** at the time of preparation, may include for each course:

| | Field |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | Subject / title |
| (b) | Objective (in line with stated departmental goals) |
| (c) | Grade / rank of trainees |
| (d) | Number of trainees |
| (e) | Training institute (if not an in-house course) |
| (f) | Timing |
| (g) | Cost |
| (h) | Number of trainee-days achieved |

Course lists should be broken down:

- local or overseas
- professional / technical; management / supervisory; language / communication; information technology / personal computer training

Overall statistics may also be given: total trainee days, total number of trainees attending courses, total costs. See [[classroom-training]] and [[compare-course-categories]].

## 6. Improving training communications

If the Training Section intends to improve the department’s internal communications — for example by disseminating training information such as CSTC circulars, prospectuses, or newsletters — that should be recorded in the Plan.

## 7. Other training-related activities

These could be:

- arrangements for **informal sharing** of information among staff after some have attended a training course
- **secondment** to other departments or organisations as part of career development
- **ad hoc** training, such as guest speakers on topics relevant to the department and its needs
- purchase of new **training equipment**

Organised post-course sharing belongs here. Individual non-classroom development still stays out of the Plan: [[informal-development]], [[compare-formal-vs-informal]].

## 8. Surveys to be conducted

If the Section intends to conduct a training needs survey covering certain grades or ranks, the Plan should indicate timing, scale, and purpose, and any technical advice and assistance the Section will be receiving.
