# Appendix 1 — Training Agreement

Guide p.9 / App.1. Sample **standard form** for appraisers to complete after the appraisal interview (Guide s.3.1). It captures the **formal** training agreed for the appraisee and feeds the [[departmental-training-plan]]. Informal non-classroom development is not this form: [[informal-development]].

This page describes the specimen. It is not a live form.

## Type codes

As printed in the footnote:

| Code | Category |
| --- | --- |
| V | vocational |
| M | management |
| L | language |
| PC | personal computer |

These codes are **not** identical to the Plan’s classroom breakdown (local/overseas; professional/technical; management/supervisory; language/communication; IT/PC). See [[compare-course-categories]].

## How it is used

The [[training-section]] designs a standard form on this pattern and asks appraisers to complete it after the interview. Forms may go straight to the Section for consolidation, or first to the operational head for priority (Guide s.3.1). Detail: [[performance-appraisal-training]].
