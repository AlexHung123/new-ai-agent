# Training Section

The departmental **Training Section or unit** is the writer of the [[departmental-training-plan]]. It consolidates needs, matches them to capacity and policy, and documents classroom and other activities for the coming year.

## Establishing needs (s.3.1)

- Supplies appraisers and appraisees with a list of past departmental courses plus [[cstc]], SSCC, and other courses available.
- Notes agreed training and consolidates it into overall needs for the coming year.
- Designs a standard form for appraisers after the interview ([[annex-training-agreement]]).
- Receives forms either directly or after the operational head has vetted them and set priorities.

## Matching and writing (s.3.2)

The Section must **not** treat the Plan as a sum of demands. It matches needs against funds, manpower, and [[departmental-training-policy]]. In formulating the Plan it may:

- work with the [[departmental-training-committee]] on funding and priorities
- seek CSTC advice on management, language, and communication training, and other institutes on technical training
- liaise with the personnel section on the number of intakes for induction

Where several sections share training responsibility, they should aim at a **master** Plan, using the [[incremental-approach]] if a single plan is not yet practicable.

## What it records in the Plan (s.4)

Classroom courses it will organise or enrol staff into ([[classroom-training]]); improvements to training communications; other training-related activities; surveys it intends to conduct.
