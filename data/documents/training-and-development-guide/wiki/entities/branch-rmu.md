# Branch Resource Management Unit

The **Branch’s Resource Management Unit** appears twice. The booklet does not define its membership or its relationship to [[csb]].

| Guide | Role |
| --- | --- |
| s.4 item 4 | Optional to record that the Plan has been **endorsed** by the Branch’s Resource Management Unit (or by senior management, or the training committee) |
| s.5 | Amendments to the Plan should periodically be **notified** to the [[departmental-training-committee]] and/or this Unit |

Copies of the endorsed Plan go to CSB and [[cstc]], not to this Unit, on the wording of Guide s.5.
