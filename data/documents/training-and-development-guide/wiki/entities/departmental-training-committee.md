# Departmental Training Committee

The departmental training committee (also “Training Committee” in later sections) is the in-department body this booklet expects to sit over needs, funding, priorities, and endorsement of the [[departmental-training-plan]]. A Human Resources Management Advisory Team may endorse the Plan instead (Guide s.5).

## Where it appears

| Guide | Role |
| --- | --- |
| s.3.1 | HRM specialists who study the corporate plan **may be** in the HRM team **or** the departmental training committee |
| s.3.2 | [[departmental-training-policy]] may say whether Committee recommendations **take precedence** over needs agreed between supervisors and subordinates |
| s.3.2 | The [[training-section]] may consult and work with the Committee on funding, priorities, etc. |
| s.4 item 4 | Optional to record endorsement by the training committee (or senior management, or [[branch-rmu]]) |
| s.5 | Endorsement by the Training Committee **or** HRM Advisory Team triggers copies to [[csb]] and [[cstc]]; amendments are notified to the Committee and/or the Branch RMU |
| s.6 | Having already formed a Training Committee is an example of “initial steps” already being followed |

The booklet does not prescribe membership, quorum, or meeting frequency.
