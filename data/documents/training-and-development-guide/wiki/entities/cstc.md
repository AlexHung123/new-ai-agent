# Civil Service Training Centre

[[cstc|CSTC]] issued this series of booklets to help departments systematize and enhance training and development (Guide s.0). This wiki is built from booklet 2 only. Later names of the centre are outside this file.

## What CSTC does in this booklet

- **Development Unit** — departments may approach it for advice on implementing the recommendations, and for queries of any kind (Guide s.0).
- **Receives a copy** of each departmental Plan, before the financial year, **for reference** (Guide s.1) and to assist in planning **central courses** (Guide s.5).
- **Allocates its own training resources** more effectively because departments file Plans (Guide s.2).
- **Professional advice** on a formal training needs survey when the department lacks in-house HRM or training expertise. If CSTC does not have the resources to help and funds are available, the department may appoint a management consultancy firm (Guide s.3.1).
- **Advice when formulating the Plan**, particularly on training in management, language, and communication (Guide s.3.2). Technical training advice is sought from other professional/training institutes.
- **Course list** supplied to appraisers and appraisees includes CSTC courses (Guide s.3.1). The Appendix 1 example is a CSTC Management Development Course ([[annex-training-agreement]]).
- **Entitlement view** — MPS Point 16 or below, at least 2 days a year; MPS Point 17 or above, at least 4 days. See [[training-entitlement]].
- **Circulars** — disseminating CSTC circulars is an example of improving training communications in the Plan (Guide s.4 item 6).

CSTC is **not** the writer of a department’s Plan. That is the [[training-section]], working with the [[departmental-training-committee]].
