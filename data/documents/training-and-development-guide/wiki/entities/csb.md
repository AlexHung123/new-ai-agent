# Civil Service Branch

[[csb|CSB]] is printed in this booklet as **Civil Service Branch**. Every department is expected to send a copy of its [[departmental-training-plan]] to CSB **for reference**, before the start of the financial year (Guide s.1). After endorsement by the Training Committee or HRM Advisory Team, one copy goes to CSB and another to [[cstc]] (Guide s.5).

The booklet does not give CSB a drafting or approving role in the Plan. CSTC, not CSB, is named as the reader who uses copies to plan central courses.

Later bureau names are outside this file.
