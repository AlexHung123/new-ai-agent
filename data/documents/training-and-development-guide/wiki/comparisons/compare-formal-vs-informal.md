# Compare — formal classroom vs informal individual

Guide s.3.1 splits agreed training after appraisal into two layers. Only the formal layer feeds the [[departmental-training-plan]]. Guide s.4 item 7 then puts **organised** post-course sharing back into the Plan as an “other training-related activity”. That is departmental effort, not the individual informal exclusion.

| | **Formal classroom** | **Informal individual** | **Organised “other” in the Plan** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guide** | s.3.1 (formal aspect); s.4 item 5 | s.3.1 (informal aspect) | s.4 item 7 |
| **What it is** | Type and timing of classroom training agreed at appraisal | Non-classroom learning and development | Arrangements the department will spend effort on |
| **Captured on** | Standard form ([[annex-training-agreement]]) | Not this form | Plan text, not the Agreement |
| **In the Plan?** | **Yes** — [[classroom-training]] | **No** — carried out individually | **Yes** |
| **Importance** | Important | **As important** as the formal aspect | Departmental activity, not a second appraisal layer |
| **Who carries it out** | [[training-section]] organises or enrols staff | The individual | The Training Section / department |
| **Examples in this booklet** | Courses listed in the Plan; Appendix 1 CSTC Management Development Course | “Non-classroom learning and development activities” (no further list) | Informal **sharing** after a course; secondment; guest speakers; training equipment |

The conversion’s “informal aspect consulting of” is read as **consisting of**. See [[informal-development]].

## What this split is not

- It is not a ranking. Informal development is as important as classroom training.
- It is not a ban on all informal-sounding words in the Plan. Sharing after a course **does** go in, because it is organised.
- Secondment, guest speakers, and equipment purchases are Plan item 7 activities. They are not the s.3.1 informal exclusion.

Whether [[departmental-training-committee]] recommendations then override supervisor–subordinate classroom agreements is a [[departmental-training-policy]] choice (Guide s.3.2), not part of this split.
