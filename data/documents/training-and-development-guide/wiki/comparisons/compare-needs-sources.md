# Compare — sources of training needs

Guide s.3.1 lists **three main areas** for establishing needs. Guide s.4 item 3 says those, plus further influences, **may** be recorded in the Plan. Every stated need still goes through matching against funds, manpower, and [[departmental-training-policy]] ([[03-matching]]). The Plan is not the sum of these rows.

| Source | Guide | When it is used | How it reaches the Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Corporate / business strategies** | s.3.1(a) | After the corporate/business plan exists | HRM specialists or the [[departmental-training-committee]] advise on competencies, experience, numbers, timing. New activities in the strategy make this especially important |
| **Outside consultation / survey** | s.3.1(b) | In-house HRM or training expertise is missing | Formal training needs survey; [[cstc]] advice; or a consultancy if CSTC cannot help **and** funds are available |
| **[[performance-appraisal-training]]** | s.3.1(c) | Type and timing agreed for the next year | Standard form ([[annex-training-agreement]]); optional operational-head vetting; **formal** classroom needs only |
| Previous year’s activities | s.3 | Always available | **Starting point only.** Needs may have changed considerably |
| Grade management’s opinions | s.4 item 3(a) | Influence; optional to record | May be listed under Sources in the Plan |
| Feedback from clients | s.4 item 3(b) | Influence; optional to record | Same |
| Unions’ views | s.4 item 3(c) | Influence; optional to record | Same |
| Service-wide requirements | s.4 item 3(d) | e.g. customer service training | Same. Conversion used **(d)** twice; this row is the first (d) |
| Manpower projections (wastage) | s.4 item 3(e) | Influence; optional to record | Same. Reconstructed as (e); see [[adtp]] |
| Survey still to be conducted | s.4 item 8 | Training Section intends to run one | Record **timing, scale, purpose**, and any technical assistance — a planned activity, not a completed source |

## Two layers in the booklet

1. **How to establish needs** (s.3.1) — corporate plan, outside survey, appraisal. Companion booklet *A Guide to Training Needs Analysis* is named and **not in this compilation**.
2. **What the Plan may list as sources** (s.4 item 3) — the three above “apart from those previously outlined”, plus grade management, clients, unions, service-wide requirements, wastage projections.

Appraisal is the only source with a specimen form. Informal non-classroom development agreed around appraisal does **not** become a Plan source: [[compare-formal-vs-informal]].
