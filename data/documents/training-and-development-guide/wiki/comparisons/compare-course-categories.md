# Compare — course category schemes

This booklet uses **three related lists**. They overlap; they are not the same taxonomy. Do not substitute Appendix 1 type codes for the Plan’s classroom breakdown.

| | **Policy priorities** | **Plan classroom lists** | **Appendix 1 type codes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guide** | s.3.2(b)(ii) | s.4 item 5 | App.1 footnote |
| **Job** | Parameters of [[departmental-training-policy]] — what to prefer when matching needs | How to **break down** courses the [[training-section]] will organise or enrol staff into | Category on the appraisal [[annex-training-agreement]] |
| **Place / mode** | — | **Local or overseas** (separate split) | — |
| **Induction** | Named as a priority | Not a classroom-list heading. Planned via personnel intakes (s.3.2(c)) | — |
| **Professional / technical** | professional/technical | professional / technical | **V** vocational |
| **Management** | management | management / **supervisory** | **M** management |
| **Language** | language | language / communication (one heading) | **L** language |
| **Communication** | communication (own item) | bundled with language | — |
| **IT** | information technology (including PCs) | information technology / personal computer | **PC** personal computer |

## Who advises on which bucket (s.3.2)

When formulating the Plan, the Training Section may:

- seek [[cstc]] advice particularly on **management, language, and communication**
- seek other professional/training institutes on **technical** training
- liaise with the personnel section on the number of intakes, so as to plan **induction**

## Do not mix the lists

- Policy priorities include **induction** and split **language** from **communication**. The Plan’s course-list headings do neither.
- The Plan adds a **local / overseas** split that the other two lists do not have.
- Appendix 1 uses **vocational (V)** where the other lists say professional/technical, and has no overseas or induction code.
- Overall Plan statistics (total trainee days, trainees, costs) sit on [[classroom-training]], not on these labels.
