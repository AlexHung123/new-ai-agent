# Compare — individual training entitlement

Guide s.3.2: the number of days each year an individual is entitled to spend on **training or training-related activities**. It is a parameter of [[departmental-training-policy]]. It may differ between grades and ranks, and will also differ between departments. Concept page: [[training-entitlement]].

| | **MPS Point 16 or below** | **MPS Point 17 or above** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CSTC view (floor)** | at least **2 days** training each year | at least **4 days** training a year |
| **Maximum** | Department should set a ceiling | Department should set a ceiling |
| **Who sets the departmental figures** | The department, as policy | The department, as policy |
| **Status in this booklet** | Printed as **the CSTC view**, not as a Civil Service Regulation | Same |
| **Use when writing the Plan** | One of the filters the [[training-section]] applies when matching stated needs | Same |

## What both columns share

- Entitlement is **days per year**, covering training and training-related activities, not classroom courses only.
- Floors are CSTC’s general view. Departments may differ by grade, rank, and department.
- Ceilings are required in policy (“should also set”) but **no numbers** are prescribed.
- Even with these days on the books, funds and manpower mean the Plan will **not** accommodate every demand from appraisals and surveys ([[03-matching]]).

This wiki does not treat the 2-day / 4-day figures as later Civil Service College rules. Later texts are out of scope unless added as a new raw source.
